# If something happens to a stranger on the road, should you walk away or stop?

This is the long version of Section 13, Clause 2.According to the beneficiary scale of the book, strangers are the lowest of the four levels: there is little hope of repayment, and you do not understand the other person’s character.So I’m not going to advise you to do anything. I’m just going to list all the bills you have to pay for each of the three paths, and you can make your own choice.

**This article does not give any probability.** The costs at each level below are real and can be checked, but there are no public statistics on the incidence rate of "what proportion of rescuers have actually incurred the cost". The rule of the book is that we would rather not write down uncertain numbers.Don’t read “listed seven levels” as “all seven levels will happen.”

## Three roads

| | Walk away | Stop but don't touch | Stop and help till the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legality | Legal.Ordinary people have no obligation to rescue strangers, and there is no penalty in the criminal law for the crime of "knowing and not saving" | Legal | Legal |
| Time | 0 | A few minutes; calling 120 will leave a number | From half a day, you may go to the hospital |
| The possibility of being found later | Lowest | You are the one who left the number | Highest |
| Opponent's profit | 0 | Ambulance arrives early | Maximum |

## Costs that may occur after stopping, from light to heavy

**1. Call back and ask questions.** When you call 120 or 110, your number will be left. You may be called back to confirm your location, or you may be asked about it afterwards.If the criminal reporter does not want to make his name public, the case-handling agency shall keep his secret (Article 111 of the Criminal Procedure Law); 120 There is no corresponding provision.

**2. Being approached by family members.** When the vehicle responsible for the accident cannot be found in the surveillance, the person who left the number and was present at the time is the easiest person for the family to find.Phone calls and door-to-door visits can happen.

**3. Being listed as a defendant.** The party who says you hit him must provide evidence by himself. If he fails to do so, he will bear the adverse consequences (Article 90 of the Interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law) - **But this only guarantees the outcome of the judgment and does not guarantee that you will not be involved**.The time limit for the conclusion of the first-instance ordinary procedure is 6 months. Under special circumstances, it can be extended for another 6 months, and the summary procedure is 3 months (Articles 152 and 164 of the Civil Procedure Law).Even if you win completely, the only things that can be passed on to the other party are case acceptance fees, application fees, and travel and lost time expenses for witnesses and appraisers to appear in court (Articles 6 and 29 of the Litigation Fee Payment Methods), and **lawyer fees are not included**.

**4. It causes trouble in your unit.** Those who disrupt the order of enterprises and institutions, causing work to be unable to proceed normally, will be given a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan, and for serious cases, they will be detained for 5 to 10 days and fined not more than 1,000 yuan; for those who gather a crowd, the ringleaders will be detained for 10 to 15 days (Article 26, Paragraph 1 of the Public Security Administration Punishment Law).Those who cause nuisance, pestering, stalking and interfering with normal life shall be punished according to Article 50, Item 5, and may also be ordered to prohibit contact within a certain period with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ.If the company takes this advantage of you, it is generally an illegal termination, and the compensation is calculated as 2N (Section 19, Article 6) - but you have to go to the police and arbitration yourself, and the loss of losing your job first will not be made up by getting 2N in the future.

**5. Being photographed and posted online.** The most important thing to know in this level is: **You may not be able to stop shooting**.You are not allowed to make, use, or disclose other people's portraits without consent (Civil Code Article 1019), but "for the purpose of carrying out news reports, the unavoidable production, use, and disclosure of the portrait owner's portrait" is a fair use (Article 1020, Paragraph 2).What you can do is not give interviews, appear on camera, or speak in front of the camera - you decide for yourself whether you agree or not, others cannot agree for you.

**6. Secondary spread and cyber violence.** Once the video spreads, the comment area is not under your control.The available self-help means are written in the "Cyber Violence Information Governance Regulations" (August 1, 2024(Article 23); Platforms should provide protection settings such as blocking unfamiliar users or specific users, limiting the visibility of personal information, prohibiting reprinting or commenting, and only accepting private messages from friends or rejecting all private messages (Article 23); should provide a quick evidence collection function for online violence information (Article 25); should set up a special quick complaint reporting portal in a prominent location (Article 26); if a suspected illegal crime is discovered, the platform should promptly report the case to the public security agency and provide clues (Article 15).See Section 8, Article 14 for the practice of retaining certificates and reporting to the police.

**7. When you can’t bear it anymore.** The above six levels can all be dealt with using clauses, but the seventh level is not.There is no reliable Chinese data to cite on the emotional impact of ongoing cyber attacks. This book does not give numbers, only the outlet: Psychological Assistance Hotline 12356, see Section 1, Article 25.

## Two situations that make “walk away” no longer free

- **You are the operator, manager or organizer of mass activities in this place** (hotels, shopping malls, banks, stations, airports, stadiums, entertainment venues, etc.): You have safety protection obligations, and if you fail to fulfill your obligations, you will bear tort liability; if a third party causes damage and you fail to fulfill your obligations, you shall bear corresponding supplementary liability (Article 1198 of the Civil Code).The staff on duty walking away from the venue is not the same as walking away from passers-by.
- **It's about you**: the danger you hit, your car, your previous actions.That is not "should you care about others", but the traffic accident handling and escape issues in Section 8, Article 1.

## When deciding to stop, the easiest way to do it

1. **Can’t move by hand.** This is the only action that does not have the second answer. The reason is that medical is not legal - hard support may aggravate cerebral hemorrhage or spinal injury.On the other side of the law, damage caused during the rescue process is exempted from Article 184 of the Civil Code.
2. Shout from a distance to determine whether you are breathing; if you are not breathing, press (Section 13, Article 1); if you are breathing, call 120 to report the position.
3. Invite a passerby to join you and start recording on your mobile phone.Personal and physical evidence are more useful than arguing after the fact.
4. Don’t carry him into the car alone, and don’t pay money to send him to the hospital.This is the most expensive and least rewarding section of the entire road.
5. If you are injured and paid to save someone, how do you get the money back? See Section 13, Article 40.
