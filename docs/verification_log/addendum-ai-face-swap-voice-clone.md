Rating B: the official material gives identification criteria and individual case figures, but no effect size for "how much the fraud rate drops after verification by this method". The benefit magnitude is set as "large" — the approach is money, and transfer frauds impersonating acquaintances and leaders usually involve a single transaction in the tens of thousands of yuan, which falls under "large" by the money threshold; the cost is money=0 time=little willpower=some (one must restrain the intuition that "he was even on video" and make one more phone call), and the composite cost-effectiveness is "high".
