**Addendum: Browse Overseas Information Only – Do Not Save, Forward, or Comment · Verification Record (2026-09-08)**

**Task Source:** A matter raised by a reader – Content on overseas websites (especially political content) should only be browsed; do not save, forward, or comment on it, and do not blindly follow or believe it. Doing so yields no substantial benefit to the individual, but the cost could be imprisonment.

**Existing Coverage:**
*   Section 9, Item 1 covers rumors regarding disasters, epidemics, and police incidents (Article 29 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law + Article 291-1 of the Criminal Law).
*   Item 2 covers insulting martyrs and the national flag/anthem.
*   Neither of these involves the chapter on Crimes Endangering National Security.
*   Section 11, Item 9 covers "selling circumvention tools or setting up nodes for others" under Illegal Business Operations and Article 14 of the Interim Provisions, which does not address how to handle content after viewing it.
*   Section 8’s provisions on online speech cover reputation rights and defamation.
*   None of these overlap. The outcome is the addition of a new Item 3 in Section 9, with the original Items 3 through 18 renumbered as Items 4 through 18 respectively.

**Reasoning for Placement:** The sorting criterion for this section is "easiest to inadvertently violate × most severe consequences." Forwarding and posting comments are casual actions (easy to violate), and Article 105, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law stipulates that for "ringleaders or those whose crimes are serious," the sentence is five years or more, with no upper cap other than the general maximum of 15 years for fixed-term imprisonment. Furthermore, crimes endangering national security shall carry the additional penalty of deprivation of political rights and may include confiscation of property (consequences are more severe than Item 3’s dissemination of obscene materials). Therefore, it is placed after Items 1 and 2 (which are also speech-related) but before the provision on disseminating pornography.
