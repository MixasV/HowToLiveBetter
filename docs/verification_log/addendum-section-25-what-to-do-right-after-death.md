# Addendum: What to Do Right After Someone Dies (First Five Items of Section 25) · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task source: after reading Section 25, the author pointed out that "it is all about problems, with nothing on what to do specifically when a body is found, or the process."

Existing coverage: Section 25 originally had 5 items, all falling after the fact — the funeral charging list, price violations, intermediary filing, housing provident fund and social insurance benefits, and the deceased's personal information rights. The period from "the person is gone" to "getting the death certificate, the body entering the funeral home, and canceling the household registration" was completely blank: searching the whole book for "death certificate" appears only once in the materials list of Section 25, item 4, and "funeral home", "cremation", "mortuary", "autopsy", and "canceling household registration" return zero hits.

Placement: 5 new items are added in front of Section 25, arranged in the order in which things happen — item 1 the scene and calling the police, item 2 the death certificate, item 3 body transport and cremation, item 4 objection to the cause of death and autopsy, item 5 canceling household registration; the original items 1 to 5 shift to 6 to 10. A sentence is added to the introduction of the section explaining that the first five items are arranged by time sequence and the rest by cost-effectiveness. Section 25 goes from 5 to 10 items, and the whole book from 408 to 413 items (A 255 → 260, B and C unchanged; cost-effectiveness high 206 → 210, average 130 → 131, extremely high unchanged at 72, with the proportions changing from 18/50/32 to 17/51/32; disputed 42 and TODO 36 unchanged). The Section 25 item list in README and CLAUDE.md and the question row in the README guide table are supplemented with the corresponding words. No other file in the book cites Section 25 by item number, so no references need to be changed back.

## Item 1 (the scene and calling the police)

|---|---|---|
| Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1958). Household Registration Regulations of the People's Republic of China (Article 8). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (published on the Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal) | Yes | Article 8, paragraph 2 "Where a citizen dies due to an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the head of the household or the discoverer shall immediately report to the local public security police station or the township or town people's committee" |
| State Council (2026). Regulations on the Administration of Funeral and Interment (State Council Order No. 824, effective March 30, 2026). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> | Yes | Article 23 "Where a natural person dies a normal death, the medical and health institution shall promptly issue a death certificate. For the deceased in abnormal death cases (incidents) handled by public security organs in accordance with the law and not treated by a medical and health institution, the public security organ shall issue the death certificate." |

Set at A: both are verbatim-checkable legal provisions. Benefit magnitude "medium", approach recorded as "freedom" — what this item prevents is the investigation and procedural trouble of not being able to explain things after the scene is disturbed and not being able to get the death certificate issued, not money.

The sentences in the main text "do not move the body, do not wash and change clothes, do not take away medicine bottles and notes" are operational guidance derived from the provision that "abnormal deaths are certified by the public security", and are the author's wording, without a separate legal provision, so they are not listed separately as quotations in the benefit section.

## Item 2 (death certificate)

| General Office of the National Health and Family Planning Commission (2014). | National Health Commission, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Civil Affairs, National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine, National Disease Control and Prevention Administration (2026). Notice on Strengthening the Registration of Resident Death Medical Certificate Information and the Management of Electronic Certificates (Guo Wei Gui Hua Fa [2026] No. 5, February 27, 2026). <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm> (reposted by the Fujian Provincial Health Commission) | Yes | "The paper version of the 'Death Certificate' and the electronic certificate have the same legal effect"; for a normal death with a clear cause, it shall be issued within one day after the death occurs; if lost, one may apply to the original issuing institution for a replacement once; if the basic information is incorrect, one may apply to the original issuing unit for re-issuance with materials (limited to once) |

Set at A: both are checkable ministerial documents. Approach recorded as "time" and benefit magnitude as "medium" — there is no amount or mortality rate here, and the mechanical threshold cannot be applied, so it is set by judgment: the death certificate is the common prerequisite for canceling household registration, cremation, housing provident fund, social insurance, insurance, and inheritance, and if it is missing or the information is incorrect, one has to repeatedly run back and forth between multiple windows, a magnitude higher than "one-time".

Content not written into the main text: to whom the four copies of the "Death Certificate" are given respectively. The standards only write the format requirement of "four copies (the last three are consistent)", without specifying the collecting unit for each copy, and practices vary across regions, so the main text only writes the uses and not the copy numbers.

Set at A. Benefit magnitude "medium", approach money: what it prevents is the outsourcing of mortuaries and the price increases of "one-stop" services at the transport stage, at the same magnitude as items 6 and 7.

## Item 4 (objection to the cause of death and autopsy)

| Literature | Rechecked | Original quoted passage |
|---|---|---|
| State Council (2018). Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (State Council Order No. 701). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm> | Yes | Article 26 "Where a patient dies and both doctors and patients object to the cause of death, an autopsy shall be conducted within 48 hours after the patient's death; where conditions for freezing the corpse exist, it may be extended to 7 days" and "the autopsy shall be consented to and signed by the close relatives of the deceased; refusal to sign shall be deemed as the close relatives of the deceased not agreeing to the autopsy" and "where the autopsy is not agreed to or is delayed, exceeding the prescribed time and affecting the determination of the cause of death, the party who did not agree or delayed shall bear responsibility"; Article 24 sealing and unsealing medical record materials "shall be carried out in the presence of both doctors and patients" |

Set at A. Benefit magnitude "large": the determination of the cause of death directly determines whether a medical damage compensation claim can be made, and the amount can reach the tens of thousands level or above; willpower is recorded as "some", because the difficulty is whether the family members can still think of raising it at that moment. How the autopsy fee is advanced and borne was not obtained in a verbatim-checkable provision this time, so the main text does not write it.

## Item 5 (canceling household registration)

| Literature | Rechecked | Original quoted passage |
