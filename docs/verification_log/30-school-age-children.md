## Several writing decisions

- **The cost of item 2 is recorded as "do it early or late", not as the treatment itself.** The action in this item is "don't push a treatment that has a window until after the exams"; the money and time the treatment should cost do not change with timing, so the cost tag is recorded as `money=0 time=little willpower=some`, and the cost column also states that the brace itself has additional costs and requires wearing at least 18 hours a day, so that readers do not think it is free.
- **Scoliosis is used as the example, rather than saying vaguely "don't delay".** It satisfies three conditions at once: there is a randomized trial, there is a window tied to bone age, and it happens to be one of the key items in student physical examinations, so it connects with item 5. Strabismus, amblyopia, dental caries, and elective surgery for congenital heart disease are only named in the remarks, without writing unverified window periods.
- **Item 6 is given B.** USPSTF is a recommendation grade, not a convertible effect size, and it is a US recommendation; there is no corresponding official screening recommendation in China, and the main text states this.
- **"One psychological assessment per school year" was not written as a screening method.** The original wording of the ten measures is "each student receives psychological assessment generally no more than once per academic year", which is a rule controlling the frequency of assessment, not a diagnostic basis, and the main text accordingly writes "assessment is not equal to diagnosis".
- **Item 11 does not write the coverage of free programs.** A search of the policy library with `searchfield=title` for "children's oral health" and "healthy oral cavity" both returned zero hits, and no verbatim-checkable national document was obtained, so it only writes "many regions have free programs; ask the local CDC or community health service center".
- **Only testicular torsion was quantified among emergencies.** It is one of the few systematic reviews with survival rates by hourly intervals; the time curves for appendicitis perforation and delay were not obtained in a checkable original text this round (the corresponding title search on Europe PMC returned no results), so they are only named in the remarks without figures.
- **Section 6 was changed in passing**: the sentence "myopia prevention and control in children is another topic; this book has not verified the evidence in this field and gives no advice" was changed to point to Section 30, items 4, 10, and 7 — this section has now verified the evidence in that field, and the original sentence is void.
