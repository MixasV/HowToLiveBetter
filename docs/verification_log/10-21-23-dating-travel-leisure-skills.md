# Verification Record: Section 10 Expansion + New Sections 21, 22, 23

Verification date: 2026-09-07. This round added 35 new items (Section 10 +8, Section 21 10, Section 22 10, Section 23 7), bringing the book from 274 to 309 items.

The method is the same as in previous rounds: for English literature, Europe PMC REST (`search?query=TITLE:"…"&resultType=core`) was used to retrieve the bibliographic record and original abstract text; for psychology journals not indexed in PubMed, Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=`) was used for the bibliographic record and Semantic Scholar (`api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:…`) for the abstract. For Chinese regulations, the State Council policy document library JSON interface (`sousuo.www.gov.cn/search-gov/data?t=zhengcelibrary`) was used for locating, after which the original-text page was scraped, stripped of tags, and checked article by article; laws enacted by the National People's Congress are not in that library, so the full texts reposted on the Supreme People's Procuratorate official website were used instead.
