# Addendum: Enforcement and Dishonesty, Fraudulent Loans with Forged Materials, Candy and Drinks from Strangers · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task source: the reader raised four things — what to do after being listed as a dishonest person, whether the evasion-of-enforcement practices in short videos ("transfer all the money and the house to relatives, register the villa you live in under the company's name as employee dormitory") work, a group forging materials to take out loans to "farm wool" and then running abroad with the money, and preventing new-type drugs (not eating candy from strangers, not drinking drinks given by others in public places).

Existing coverage: Section 7, item 20 only wrote about the duration and deletion of the dishonest person list (the road to starting over), not what to do and not do during enforcement; Section 8, item 11 only wrote about IOUs and suretyship; Section 9, item 4 wrote about withdrawing and transferring cash for others (two cards), not fraudulent loans; Section 22, item 3 wrote about leaving when someone hands something to you in an entertainment venue, not the side of "being tricked into swallowing it". None of the four things overlaps, and the placements are respectively 2 new items in Section 8 (items 12 and 13), 1 new item in Section 9 (item 5), and 1 new item in Section 22 (item 4). The original Section 8 from item 12 onward, Section 9 from item 5 onward, and Section 22 from item 4 onward shift accordingly.
