# Addendum: Legal Ways Out When You Cannot Raise a Child, and the Red Lines · Verification Record (2026-09-09)

Task source: a reader saw the news that "a woman who had just given birth strangled her newborn and threw it in a trash can" and asked whether a related item should be added.

Judgment: the act in the news itself does not need an item of advice — no one will change their decision after reading "strangling an infant is intentional homicide". What is valuable is the fork in the road around it: **where the legal exit is when you cannot raise a child, and where the line between "giving the child away" and "selling the child" lies**. This passage had zero coverage in the whole book: searching for "giving a child for adoption", "adoption", "abandonment", "trafficking", and "abandoned infant" under book/ returned zero hits.

Existing coverage was checked first to avoid duplication: postpartum depression screening is in Section 27, item 16, the red-line signal of "thoughts of harming yourself or harming the child" is already written in the "go to the hospital immediately" list in Section 27, item 7, Section 1, item 25 has the handling of suicidal thoughts, and Section 8, item 13 has the right of close relatives to send someone for treatment. So the mental state side is not lacking; what is lacking is the legal exit side.

Placement: one new item 19 appended at the end of Section 9 (appended at the end, with zero renumbering). Section 9's positioning is "legal red lines that ordinary people easily step on, with an official case for each item where possible", and this item happens to be a line that you will step on if you do not know it, with the heaviest consequences. The remark of Section 27, item 16 adds one sentence pointing to it.
