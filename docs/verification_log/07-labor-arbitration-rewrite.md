## 5. "Law on Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration" (Presidential Order No. 80)
- URL: <https://chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn/h5/c/2022-07-15/356212.shtml> (China Employment Network of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security)
- Status: opened successfully.
- Article 27: "劳动争议申请仲裁的时效期间为一年。……劳动关系存续期间因拖欠劳动报酬发生争议的，劳动者申请仲裁不受本条第一款规定的仲裁时效期间的限制；但是，劳动关系终止的，应当自劳动关系终止之日起一年内提出。" ("The limitation period for applying for arbitration of a labor dispute is one year. … Where a dispute arises over unpaid labor remuneration during the existence of the labor relationship, the worker's application for arbitration is not subject to the arbitration limitation period stipulated in paragraph 1 of this article; however, if the labor relationship has terminated, it shall be filed within one year from the date of termination of the labor relationship.")
- Article 43: "仲裁庭裁决劳动争议案件，应当自劳动争议仲裁委员会受理仲裁申请之日起四十五日内结束。……延长期限不得超过十五日。" ("The arbitral tribunal shall conclude the arbitration of a labor dispute case within 45 days from the date the labor dispute arbitration committee accepts the arbitration application. … The extension period shall not exceed 15 days.")
- Article 44: "仲裁庭对追索劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或者赔偿金的案件，根据当事人的申请，可以裁决先予执行，移送人民法院执行。" ("For cases of pursuing labor remuneration, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation, or damages, the arbitral tribunal may, upon the party's application, rule for advance execution and transfer the case to the people's court for enforcement.")
- Article 47: "追索劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或者赔偿金，不超过当地月最低工资标准十二个月金额的争议" ("disputes over pursuing labor remuneration, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation, or damages, where the amount does not exceed twelve months of the local monthly minimum wage standard") are final rulings.
- Articles 48 and 49: if a worker is dissatisfied with a final ruling, he or she may sue within 15 days; an employer may only apply to the intermediate court to set it aside within 30 days.
- Article 50: "当事人对本法第四十七条规定以外的其他劳动争议案件的仲裁裁决不服的，可以自收到仲裁裁决书之日起十五日内向人民法院提起诉讼" ("Where a party is dissatisfied with the arbitration award for other labor dispute cases beyond those stipulated in Article 47 of this Law, he or she may file a lawsuit with the people's court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the arbitration award")
- Article 53: "劳动争议仲裁不收费。劳动争议仲裁委员会的经费由财政予以保障。" ("Labor dispute arbitration is free of charge. The funds of the labor dispute arbitration committee shall be guaranteed by the finance department.")

The high courts of each province may set local standards within this range.
