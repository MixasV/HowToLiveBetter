# Section 12 Source Verification Record (2026-09-07)

Verification method: the WebSearch quota for this session was exhausted, so regulation locating was switched to the Chinese government website policy document library search interface (sousuo.www.gov.cn/search-gov/data, used only to find URLs, not as a source). Each URL was first opened with WebFetch to confirm the title, document number, and provisions; full-text law pages were additionally downloaded with curl to scratchpad (s12/page_*.html), stripped of tags, and the original text was located verbatim by "Article X"; the quoted passages below all come from local locating. The MOHRSS page has an anti-scraping script, and WebFetch returned blank, so curl was used with the cookie computed by the script to open it and obtain the full text (the page title and version line were checked). The Ministry of Commerce franchise system certificate did not match the domain, and WebFetch reported an error, so curl -k was used to open it and check the title. The DOI redirect via doi.org to pubsonline.informs.org returned 403, so the Crossref API and Semantic Scholar API were used to check the bibliographic record and abstract.

## Confirmed sources

### 1. Civil Code
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> (Supreme People's Procuratorate laws and regulations database)
- Page title "Civil Code of the People's Republic of China", version line "adopted at the third session of the 13th National People's Congress on May 28, 2020". Confirmed by both WebFetch and local locating.
- Article 56: "For the debts of an individual industrial and commercial household, if it is operated by an individual, they shall be borne with the individual's property; if it is operated by the family, they shall be borne with the family's property; if it cannot be distinguished, they shall be borne with the family's property."
- Article 184: "Where damage is caused to the recipient by a voluntary act of emergency rescue, the rescuer shall not bear civil liability."
- Article 469: "The parties may conclude a contract in written form, oral form, or other forms. Written form is a form that can tangibly express the content contained therein, such as a contract document, letter, telegram, telex, or fax."
- Article 585: "The parties may agree that if one party breaches the contract, it shall pay a certain amount of liquidated damages to the other party according to the breach … if the agreed liquidated damages are lower than the losses caused, the people's court or arbitration institution may, upon the request of the party, increase them; if the agreed liquidated damages are excessively higher than the losses caused, the people's court or arbitration institution may, upon the request of the party, appropriately reduce them."
- Article 586: "The parties may agree that one party shall pay a deposit to the other party as security for the creditor's rights. The deposit contract is established when the deposit is actually delivered. The amount of the deposit shall be agreed upon by the parties; however, it shall not exceed 20 percent of the subject matter amount of the principal contract, and the excess portion does not have the effect of a deposit."
- Article 587: "If the party paying the deposit fails to perform the debt … it has no right to request the return of the deposit; if the party receiving the deposit fails to perform the debt … it shall return double the deposit."
- Article 588: "Where the parties have agreed on both liquidated damages and a deposit, and one party breaches the contract, the other party may choose to apply the liquidated damages or the deposit clause."
- Article 668: "A loan contract shall be in written form, unless otherwise agreed between natural persons for a loan. The contents of a loan contract generally include clauses such as the type of loan, currency, purpose, amount, interest rate, term, and repayment method."
- Article 681: "A suretyship contract is a contract in which the surety and the creditor agree that, when the debtor fails to perform the due debt or the circumstances agreed by the parties occur, the surety shall perform the debt or bear liability, in order to guarantee the realization of the creditor's rights."
- Article 687: "Where the parties agree in the suretyship contract that the surety shall bear suretyship liability when the debtor cannot perform the debt, it is general suretyship. The surety in a general suretyship has the right to refuse to bear suretyship liability to the creditor before the dispute over the principal contract has been tried or arbitrated and enforcement against the debtor's property in accordance with the law still cannot satisfy the debt"
- Article 688: "Where the parties agree in the suretyship contract that the surety and the debtor bear joint and several liability for the debt, it is joint and several suretyship. Where the debtor in a joint and several suretyship fails to perform the due debt … the creditor may request the debtor to perform the debt, or may request the surety to bear suretyship liability within the scope of its suretyship."
- Article 1064: "Debts borne by the joint expression of intent of both husband and wife, such as joint signatures of both husband and wife or subsequent ratification by one spouse … are joint debts of the husband and wife. Debts borne by one spouse in his or her own name during the existence of the marriage relationship beyond the needs of daily family life are not joint debts of the husband and wife; however, this does not apply where the creditor can prove that the debt was used for the joint life of the husband and wife, for joint production and business operations, or was based on the joint expression of intent of both husband and wife."

### 2. Company Law (2023 revision)
- URL: <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm> (Chinese government website)
- Page title "Company Law of the People's Republic of China", version line includes "second revision at the seventh session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress on December 29, 2023". Confirmed by both WebFetch and local locating; the article numbers were checked against the 2023 revision.
- Article 4: "Shareholders of a limited liability company shall be liable to the company to the extent of the capital contributions they have subscribed; shareholders of a joint stock limited company shall be liable to the company to the extent of the shares they have subscribed."
- Article 23: "Where a shareholder of a company abuses the independent legal personality of the company and the limited liability of shareholders to evade debts and seriously damages the interests of the company's creditors, the shareholder shall bear joint and several liability for the company's debts. … Where a company has only one shareholder, and the shareholder cannot prove that the company's property is independent of the shareholder's own property, the shareholder shall bear joint and several liability for the company's debts."
- Article 47: "The registered capital of a limited liability company is the capital contributions subscribed by all shareholders as registered with the company registration authority. The capital contributions subscribed by all shareholders shall be paid in full by the shareholders within five years from the date of the company's establishment in accordance with the company's articles of association."
- Article 49: "Shareholders shall pay in full and on time the capital contributions they have subscribed as stipulated in the company's articles of association. … If a shareholder fails to pay the capital contribution in full and on time, in addition to paying the company in full, it shall also bear compensation liability for the losses caused to the company."
- Article 50: "At the time of establishment of a limited liability company, if a shareholder fails to actually pay the capital contribution in accordance with the company's articles of association … the other shareholders at the time of establishment shall bear joint and several liability with that shareholder within the scope of the insufficient capital contribution."
- Article 53: "After the company is established, shareholders shall not withdraw their capital contributions. Whoever violates the preceding paragraph shall return the withdrawn capital contribution"
- Article 54: "Where a company cannot pay off its due debts, the company or a creditor whose claim has become due has the right to require a shareholder who has subscribed for capital contribution but whose capital contribution period has not yet expired to pay the capital contribution in advance."

### 9. Criminal Law (1997 revised text)
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> (Supreme People's Procuratorate laws and regulations database)
- Page title "Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China (1997 Revision)". Confirmed by both WebFetch and local locating.
- Article 205: "Whoever falsely issues a special value-added tax invoice or falsely issues other invoices used to defraud export tax refunds or to offset tax payments shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined not less than 20,000 yuan and not more than 200,000 yuan; if the amount of tax falsely issued is relatively large or there are other serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years and not more than ten years, and shall also be fined not less than 50,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan; if the amount of tax falsely issued is huge or there are other especially serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment … Falsely issuing a special value-added tax invoice or falsely issuing other invoices used to defraud export tax refunds or to offset tax payments means any of the acts of falsely issuing for another person, falsely issuing for oneself, having another person falsely issue for oneself, or introducing another person to falsely issue." - Article 225: "Whoever, in violation of state provisions, commits any of the following illegal business operations, disrupting market order, if the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention, and shall also or only be fined not less than one time and not more than five times the illegal gains; if the circumstances are especially serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years … (1) engaging in the operation of monopolized or specially sold goods or other goods whose sale is restricted as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations without a license; … (3) illegally engaging in securities, futures, or insurance business without the approval of the relevant state competent department, or illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business" (the page notes that this item was amended according to Amendment (VII)).
