# Verification Record: New Sections 24 and 25

Verification date: 2026-09-07. New Section 24 "Seeing a Doctor: How to Spend Less and Take Fewer Detours" with 6 items, and Section 25 "What to Do After Someone Passes Away" with 5 items, were added, bringing the book from 333 to 344 items and from 23 to 25 sections.

Both sections are adjacent to existing sections, and boundaries were drawn before writing: benefit eligibility and assistance are in Section 7, long-term chronic disease management is in Section 16, emergency treatment is in Section 13, arrangements before death for the elderly are in Section 17, and work-related death benefits are in Section 19. These two sections write only the parts not covered by those sections, and wherever there is overlap it is written as cross-references.

## I. Original text verified item by item in Section 24

The source is the General Office of the State Council's "Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of the Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System" (April 2026) <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>.

| Item | Original text verified |
| --- | --- |
| Deductible calculated continuously | "For insured patients within the coordinating region who are referred level by level through primary medical and health institutions, the inpatient deductible at the higher-level hospital may be calculated continuously; for inpatients transferred down from a higher-level hospital to a primary medical and health institution, no separate inpatient deductible shall be set within the same disease cycle." |
| Reimbursement rates differ by about 10 percentage points at each level | "Appropriately widen the inpatient reimbursement levels of insured persons at medical and health institutions of different grades according to local conditions, and in principle widen the gap by about 10 percentage points level by level within the coordinating region." "Where conditions permit, the outpatient reimbursement levels of insured persons at medical and health institutions of different grades may be appropriately widened according to the guidance of tiered diagnosis and treatment." |
| Reserved appointment slots and referral centers | "Lead hospitals shall reserve a certain proportion of appointment slots and beds for primary medical and health institutions, and promptly receive patients referred through primary institutions." "Medical institutions shall strengthen the unified management of referral services, set up referral centers or designate fixed departments to undertake patient referral services, and achieve full coverage by 2027." |
| Necessity assessment for cross-province medical care | "In principle, personnel with the title of associate chief physician or above in secondary and tertiary hospitals shall assess the necessity of patients seeking medical care across coordinating regions or across provinces." "The reimbursement level of personnel seeking temporary medical care outside the province shall maintain a reasonable difference from the reimbursement level of medical institutions of the same grade in the place of insurance." |
| Tertiary hospitals appropriately reduce general outpatient services | "Tertiary hospitals shall focus on acute and critical illnesses and difficult and complex diseases … and gradually and appropriately reduce general outpatient services such as follow-up visits for common diseases and chronic diseases with a clear diagnosis and stable condition." ## IV. Approach and benefit magnitude

Of the six items in Section 24, four have money as the approach and two have time. The benefit magnitude is set according to the money thresholds: the deductible and the reimbursement rate differences are both in the range of several hundred to several thousand yuan, so they are set as "medium"; none falls in the tens of thousands of yuan range, so this section has no "large". The first three items of Section 25 are about avoiding illegal charges of several hundred to several thousand yuan, so they are set as "medium"; item 4 involves the housing provident fund balance and social insurance benefits, which can reach the tens of thousands of yuan range, so it is set as "large"; item 5 has freedom as the approach (including personal information), and like the two items in Section 14, it belongs to the situation where the threshold does not apply, so it is set as "medium" by judgment.

The cost of both sections is almost zero, but several items record "willpower = some": the hard part is not knowing the rules, but being willing to go to the community first at the moment, and being willing to be meticulous about a price list while handling a funeral.
