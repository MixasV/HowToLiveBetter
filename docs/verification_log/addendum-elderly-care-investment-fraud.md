# Addendum: Investment-for-Elderly-Care and Housing-for-Pension Fraud · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task source: following the review in the round where "someone said to add fraud prevention", the second real blank was addressed. Section 17 originally had only item 3 (keeping the elderly person's money in a separate account, with two-person confirmation for large expenditures) and item 4 (the shield script) touching on this topic, both at level C and without sources, only saying "after a long period of building trust, harvest in one go", without writing what specific names are used or what the legal consequences are. The previous round's "Addendum - Chronic Disease Long-Term Care Insurance and Personal Information Rights" explicitly noted that "official cases of elderly fraud (housing-for-pension, health product sales meetings): no citable SPP typical case was obtained, so this item in Section 17 was not written" — this round they were obtained.

Placement: Section 17 adds 2 items (items 5 and 6), and the original item 5 (long-term care insurance) shifts to item 7. Items 3 and 4 are retained as they are, without changing their grade or source. No other section cites Section 17 by number, so no consequential changes are needed.

The reason for splitting into two items rather than one: the former category is paying money to buy a promise (cards, beds, apartments, travel-and-residence, elderly products), and the action to prevent is "do not hand over the money", with the upper limit of the money being the cash in the elderly person's hands; the latter category is taking the house to mortgage for cash and then buying wealth management products, and the action to prevent is "do not mortgage, do not sign", and what is lost is the house plus owing a loan, with a different magnitude and disposal path.

Legitimate product: "Legitimate housing for pension refers to the housing reverse mortgage pension insurance for the elderly, that is, an innovative commercial pension insurance combining housing mortgage with lifetime pension annuity insurance. Elderly people with complete lawful property rights to a house mortgage the property to an insurance company, continue to have the rights to possess, use, benefit from, and dispose of the house with the consent of the mortgagee (insurance company), and receive a pension according to the agreed conditions until death"; "it is still in the pilot stage in China and is relatively niche"; fraud: "the 'housing for pension' claimed by lawbreakers has nothing to do with the housing reverse mortgage pension insurance piloted by the state; it is merely a means for them to use national policy to build momentum and publicize illegal fundraising activities"; "it is often a 'new-for-old' Ponzi scheme. Some participants do not even know that their property has been mortgaged, and ultimately lose the house and are burdened with loans"; threshold: "if a wealth management product promises a return rate of more than 6%, you should question it; more than 8% is very dangerous; above 10%, you should be prepared to lose all principal; 'guaranteed principal with high returns' is financial fraud"; also "treat the contract signing stage cautiously, and do not sign blank contracts" |

Rating B: the regulatory authorities' risk reminder wording and empirical thresholds; 6%/8%/10% are judgment references for consumers, not legal standards, and there is no effect size for "how much the fraud rate drops after following this".
