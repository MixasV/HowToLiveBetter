### 1. Road Traffic Safety Law (2021 amendment)
- URL: <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html> (repost page of the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau Traffic Management Bureau, a local official website)
- Page title "中华人民共和国道路交通安全法【2021年修订】" ("Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China [2021 Amendment]"), version line on the page: "2021年4月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议修订" ("revised at the 28th session of the Standing Committee of the 13th National People's Congress on April 29, 2021"). Confirmed.
- Article 70, paragraph 1: "在道路上发生交通事故，车辆驾驶人应当立即停车，保护现场；造成人身伤亡的，车辆驾驶人应当立即抢救受伤人员，并迅速报告执勤的交通警察或者公安机关交通管理部门。因抢救受伤人员变动现场的，应当标明位置。" ("When a traffic accident occurs on the road, the vehicle driver shall immediately stop and protect the scene; if personal injury or death is caused, the vehicle driver shall immediately rescue the injured and promptly report to the traffic police on duty or the traffic management department of the public security organ. If the scene is changed due to rescuing the injured, the position shall be marked.") Paragraph 2: "未造成人身伤亡，当事人对事实及成因无争议的，可以即行撤离现场，恢复交通，自行协商处理损害赔偿事宜" ("If no personal injury or death is caused and the parties have no dispute over the facts and causes, they may immediately leave the scene, restore traffic, and handle the compensation for damages through self-negotiation"); paragraph 3: "仅造成轻微财产损失，并且基本事实清楚的，当事人应当先撤离现场再进行协商处理。" ("If only minor property damage is caused and the basic facts are clear, the parties shall first leave the scene and then handle it through negotiation.")
- The full text of the five paragraphs of Article 91 has been located: "饮酒后驾驶机动车的，处暂扣六个月机动车驾驶证，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款。因饮酒后驾驶机动车被处罚，再次饮酒后驾驶机动车的，处十日以下拘留，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款，吊销机动车驾驶证。" ("Driving a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol shall result in temporary detention of the motor vehicle driver's license for six months and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan and not more than 2,000 yuan. If, after being punished for driving a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol, the person again drives a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol, he shall be detained for not more than 10 days, fined not less than 1,000 yuan and not more than 2,000 yuan, and his motor vehicle driver's license shall be revoked.") "醉酒驾驶机动车的……吊销机动车驾驶证，依法追究刑事责任；五年内不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。" ("Driving a motor vehicle while intoxicated … the motor vehicle driver's license shall be revoked, and criminal responsibility shall be pursued in accordance with the law; the person may not obtain a new motor vehicle driver's license within five years.") "饮酒后驾驶营运机动车的，处十五日拘留，并处五千元罚款，吊销机动车驾驶证，五年内不得重新取得" ("Driving an operating motor vehicle after drinking alcohol shall result in 15 days of detention, a fine of 5,000 yuan, revocation of the motor vehicle driver's license, and no new motor vehicle driver's license may be obtained within five years") "醉酒驾驶营运机动车的……十年内不得重新取得机动车驾驶证，重新取得机动车驾驶证后，不得驾驶营运机动车。" ("Driving an operating motor vehicle while intoxicated … no new motor vehicle driver's license may be obtained within ten years, and after obtaining a new motor vehicle driver's license, the person may not drive an operating motor vehicle.") "饮酒后或者醉酒驾驶机动车发生重大交通事故，构成犯罪的……终生不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。" ("If a major traffic accident occurs after driving a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol or while intoxicated and a crime is constituted … the person shall never be allowed to obtain a new motor vehicle driver's license for life.")
- Article 101, paragraph 2: "造成交通事故后逃逸的，由公安机关交通管理部门吊销机动车驾驶证，且终生不得重新取得机动车驾驶证。" ("Whoever escapes after causing a traffic accident shall have his motor vehicle driver's license revoked by the traffic management department of the public security organ, and shall never be allowed to obtain a new motor vehicle driver's license for life.")

### 2. Implementing Regulations of the Road Traffic Safety Law (2017 amendment)
- URL: <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75> (National Administrative Regulations Database of the Ministry of Justice)
- Version line on the page: "2004年4月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第405号公布 根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订" ("promulgated by State Council Order No. 405 of the People's Republic of China on April 30, 2004, revised according to the 'Decision of the State Council on Amending Some Administrative Regulations' of October 7, 2017"). Confirmed.
- Article 92: "发生交通事故后当事人逃逸的，逃逸的当事人承担全部责任。但是，有证据证明对方当事人也有过错的，可以减轻责任。当事人故意破坏、伪造现场、毁灭证据的，承担全部责任。" ("If a party escapes after a traffic accident occurs, the escaping party shall bear full responsibility. However, if there is evidence proving that the other party is also at fault, responsibility may be reduced. If a party intentionally destroys or forges the scene or destroys evidence, that party shall bear full responsibility.")
- Article 86: "机动车与机动车、机动车与非机动车在道路上发生未造成人身伤亡的交通事故，当事人对事实及成因无争议的，在记录交通事故的时间、地点、对方当事人的姓名和联系方式、机动车牌号、驾驶证号、保险凭证号、碰撞部位，并共同签名后，撤离现场，自行协商损害赔偿事宜。" ("Where a traffic accident not causing personal injury or death occurs on the road between a motor vehicle and a motor vehicle or between a motor vehicle and a non-motor vehicle, and the parties have no dispute over the facts and causes, after recording the time and place of the traffic accident, the names and contact information of the other parties, the motor vehicle plate number, driver's license number, insurance certificate number, and collision location, and jointly signing, they shall leave the scene and handle compensation for damages through self-negotiation.")

### 3. Criminal Law (1997 revised text)
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> (Supreme People's Procuratorate laws and regulations database)
- Page title "中华人民共和国刑法（1997年修订）" ("Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China (1997 Revision)"). Confirmed. The provisions cited in this section — Articles 20, 133, 234, 243, 258, and 266 — have not been changed in their text by any of the successive amendments since 1997 (the amendment pages were checked as below), and Article 246 had a third paragraph added by Amendment (IX).
- Article 20: "……属于正当防卫，不负刑事责任。正当防卫明显超过必要限度造成重大损害的，应当负刑事责任，但是应当减轻或者免除处罚。" ("… it is justified defense and no criminal responsibility shall be borne. If justified defense clearly exceeds the necessary limit and causes major damage, criminal responsibility shall be borne, but the punishment shall be mitigated or exempted.")
- Article 133: "……处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；交通运输肇事后逃逸或者有其他特别恶劣情节的，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑；因逃逸致人死亡的，处七年以上有期徒刑。" ("… shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention; whoever escapes after causing a traffic accident or has other especially vile circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years and not more than seven years; whoever causes death due to escape shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years.")
- Article 234: "故意伤害他人身体的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制。犯前款罪，致人重伤的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑" ("Whoever intentionally injures another person's body shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance. Whoever commits the crime in the preceding paragraph and causes serious injury shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years and not more than ten years")
- Article 243: "捏造事实诬告陷害他人，意图使他人受刑事追究，情节严重的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制；造成严重后果的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑。……不是有意诬陷，而是错告，或者检举失实的，不适用前两款的规定。" ("Whoever fabricates facts to falsely accuse and frame another person, intending to have that person subject to criminal investigation, if the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance; if serious consequences are caused, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years and not more than ten years. … If it is not intentional framing but a mistaken accusation, or the report is inaccurate, the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall not apply.")
- Article 246: "以暴力或者其他方法公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人，情节严重的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利。前款罪，告诉的才处理，但是严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的除外。" ("Whoever publicly insults another person by violence or other methods, or fabricates facts to slander another person, if the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights. The crime in the preceding paragraph shall be handled only upon complaint, except where it seriously endangers social order and national interests.")
- Article 258: "有配偶而重婚的，或者明知他人有配偶而与之结婚的，处二年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。" ("Whoever has a spouse and commits bigamy, or knowingly marries a person who has a spouse, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than two years or criminal detention.")
- Article 266: "诈骗公私财物，数额较大的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处或者单处罚金；数额巨大或者有其他严重情节的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；数额特别巨大或者有其他特别严重情节的，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。" ("Whoever defrauds public or private property, if the amount is relatively large, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and shall also or only be fined; if the amount is huge or there are other serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years and not more than ten years, and shall also be fined; if the amount is especially huge or there are other especially serious circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated.")

### 6. Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>
- On the page: "（2022年9月2日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十六次会议通过）" ("(adopted at the 36th session of the Standing Committee of the 13th National People's Congress on September 2, 2022)"), effective date 2022-12-01. Confirmed.
- Article 20: "国务院公安部门会同有关部门建立完善电信网络诈骗涉案资金即时查询、紧急止付、快速冻结、及时解冻和资金返还制度，明确有关条件、程序和救济措施。公安机关依法决定采取上述措施的，银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当予以配合。" ("The public security department of the State Council, together with relevant departments, shall establish and improve the system for instant inquiry, emergency stop-payment, rapid freezing, timely unfreezing, and fund return of funds involved in telecom and online fraud, and shall clarify the relevant conditions, procedures, and remedies. - Article 44: "违反本法第三十一条第一款规定的，没收违法所得，由公安机关处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二万元的，处二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处十五日以下拘留。" ("Whoever violates Article 31, paragraph 1 of this Law shall have the illegal gains confiscated, and the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than one time and not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, detention of not more than 15 days shall also be imposed.")

### 8. Criminal Procedure Law (2018 amendment)
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Page title "中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法" ("Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China"), version is the third amendment of October 26, 2018. Confirmed.

### 13. Supreme People's Court Provisions on Bride Price Disputes (Fa Shi [2024] No. 1)
- URL: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/423442.html>
- Title, document number Fa Shi [2024] No. 1, adoption on 2023-11-13, and effectiveness on 2024-02-01 are all confirmed.
- Article 2: "禁止借婚姻索取财物。一方以彩礼为名借婚姻索取财物，另一方要求返还的，人民法院应予支持。" ("Extorting property by marriage is prohibited. Where one party, in the name of bride price, extorts property by marriage, and the other party requests its return, the people's court shall support the request.")
- Article 3: "下列情形给付的财物，不属于彩礼：（一）一方在节日、生日等有特殊纪念意义时点给付的价值不大的礼物、礼金；（二）一方为表达或者增进感情的日常消费性支出；（三）其他价值不大的财物。" ("Property given under the following circumstances does not constitute bride price: (1) gifts or gift money of small value given by one party on festivals, birthdays, and other occasions of special commemorative significance; (2) daily consumption expenditures by one party to express or enhance affection; (3) other property of small value.")
- Article 5: "双方已办理结婚登记且共同生活，离婚时一方请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，人民法院一般不予支持。但是，如果共同生活时间较短且彩礼数额过高的，人民法院可以根据彩礼实际使用及嫁妆情况，综合考虑彩礼数额、共同生活及孕育情况、双方过错等事实，结合当地习俗，确定是否返还以及返还的具体比例。人民法院认定彩礼数额是否过高，应当综合考虑彩礼给付方所在地居民人均可支配收入、给付方家庭经济情况以及当地习俗等因素。" ("Where both parties have completed marriage registration and live together, and one party requests the return of bride price given according to custom upon divorce, the people's court generally does not support it. However, if the time of living together is relatively short and the amount of bride price is excessively high, the people's court may, based on the actual use of the bride price and the dowry situation, comprehensively considering facts such as the amount of bride price, the time of living together and childbearing, and the fault of both parties, and in light of local customs, determine whether to return it and the specific proportion of return. In determining whether the amount of bride price is excessively high, the people's court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the per capita disposable income of residents in the place where the bride price payer is located, the family economic situation of the payer, and local customs.")
- Article 6: "双方未办理结婚登记但已共同生活，一方请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，人民法院应当根据彩礼实际使用及嫁妆情况……确定是否返还以及返还的具体比例。" ("Where both parties have not completed marriage registration but have lived together, and one party requests the return of bride price given according to custom, the people's court shall, based on the actual use of the bride price and the dowry situation … determine whether to return it and the specific proportion of return.")

### 14. Interpretation (I) on the Marriage and Family Chapter of the Civil Code (Fa Shi [2020] No. 22)
- URL: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>
- Title, document number, and effectiveness on 2021-01-01 confirmed.
- Article 5: "当事人请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，如果查明属于以下情形，人民法院应当予以支持：（一）双方未办理结婚登记手续；（二）双方办理结婚登记手续但确未共同生活；（三）婚前给付并导致给付人生活困难。适用前款第二项、第三项的规定，应当以双方离婚为条件。" ("Where a party requests the return of bride price given according to custom, the people's court shall support it if it is ascertained to fall under the following circumstances: (1) both parties have not completed marriage registration procedures; (2) both parties have completed marriage registration procedures but indeed did not live together; (3) the gift was given before marriage and caused the giver to live in difficulty.
