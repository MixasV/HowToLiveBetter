# Section 10 Addendum: Two Items (Parents Contributing to a Home Purchase, Marital Joint Debts) Source Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Origin: the reader mentioned a plot from a short video — when dividing real estate in a divorce, the contributing parents produce a loan agreement signed only by their own child, claiming that the money used to buy the house back then was a loan, so the other party has to share the debt. This section originally had only one item, "check the legal default rules first for the money account", which discusses the Civil Code's default property rules, with no determination of the nature of parents' contribution to a home purchase and no rules for determining marital joint debts, so two items were added.

Tools: WebSearch + WebFetch; the Supreme People's Court gazette site (gongbao.court.gov.cn) returned 502 to WebFetch, so curl through the local proxy was used to fetch the raw HTML (the page is GBK) and parse it locally.

## Item 11 "When parents contribute to a home purchase, write clearly at the time of transfer whether it is a loan or a gift"

### Interpretation (I) on the Marriage and Family Chapter, Article 29
- Page: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>: opened with WebFetch; Fa Shi [2020] No. 22, effective January 1, 2021
- Quoted passage: "Where, before the parties marry, parents contribute funds to purchase a house for both parties, the contribution shall be recognized as a gift to their own child individually, unless the parents clearly express that it is a gift to both parties. Where, after the parties marry, parents contribute funds to purchase a house for both parties, it shall be handled according to the agreement; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, it shall be handled according to the principle stipulated in Article 1062, paragraph 1, item 4 of the Civil Code."
- This link is already in use for the bride price item in Section 8, and the page is reachable

### Interpretation (II) on the Marriage and Family Chapter, Article 8
- Page: <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452771.html>: opened with WebFetch; Fa Shi [2025] No. 1, adopted at the 1933rd meeting of the Judicial Committee on November 25, 2024, effective February 1, 2025, with 23 articles in total
- Quoted passage (Article 8): "During the existence of the marriage relationship, where a husband and wife purchase a house and one party's parents contribute the full amount, if the gift contract clearly stipulates that it is a gift only to their own child, it shall be handled according to the agreement; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, when dividing the joint property of the husband and wife upon divorce, the people's court may rule that the house belongs to the child of the contributing party, and, comprehensively considering the circumstances of living together and bearing common children, divorce fault, the magnitude of contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of divorce, determine whether the party obtaining the house shall compensate the other party and the specific amount of compensation. During the existence of the marriage relationship, where a husband and wife purchase a house and one party's parents contribute part of the funds or both parties' parents contribute funds, if the gift contract clearly stipulates that the corresponding contribution is a gift only to their own child, it shall be handled according to the agreement; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, when dividing the joint property of the husband and wife upon divorce, the people's court may, according to the parties' litigation request, based on the source and proportion of the contribution, and comprehensively considering the circumstances of living together and bearing common children, divorce fault, the magnitude of contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of divorce, rule that the house belongs to one of the parties, and the party obtaining the house shall give reasonable compensation to the other party."
- The typical cases on marriage and family disputes released in the same batch <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452761.html>: opened with WebFetch; the gist of case 2 (the divorce dispute between Fan Moumou and Xu Moumou) is "where, during the existence of the marriage relationship, a house purchased with the full contribution of one party's parents is transferred and registered in the names of both husband and wife, when dividing the joint property of the husband and wife upon divorce, the house may be ruled to belong to the child of the contributing party based on the source of the contribution to that property", and it was ruled to belong to the child of the contributing party, with 70,000 yuan compensation to the other party. The case serves only as supporting evidence for understanding the provision, and the main text does not cite the figure
- Article 1062, paragraph 1, item 4 of the Civil Code (property inherited or received as a gift is joint property of the husband and wife) was already verified in item 10 of this section, see `10-结婚划不划算.md`
- Not verified: Interpretation (II) has no provision on the burden of proof for "whether parents' contribution is a loan or a gift", and there is no marital joint debt provision among the 23 articles either (checked against the full text with WebFetch), so the main text does not write conclusions such as "the court will never recognize the IOU"

## Item 12 "Large loans taken out by one spouse, which you did not sign and did not ratify, do not automatically become your debt"

### Civil Code Article 1064
- Page: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>: opened with WebFetch
- Quoted passage: "Debts borne by the joint expression of intent of both husband and wife, such as joint signatures of both husband and wife or subsequent ratification by one spouse, as well as debts borne by one spouse in his or her own name during the existence of the marriage relationship for the needs of daily family life, are joint debts of the husband and wife. Debts borne by one spouse in his or her own name during the existence of the marriage relationship beyond the needs of daily family life are not joint debts of the husband and wife; however, this does not apply where the creditor can prove that the debt was used for the joint life of the husband and wife, for joint production and business operations, or was based on the joint expression of intent of both husband and wife."
- Note that the wording is "joint signatures", not "joint signing"

### Articles 15 and 16 of the Private Lending Provisions
- Page: <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html>: WebFetch returned 502, and curl through the proxy fetched a 24 KB GBK page that was parsed successfully locally
- Version: adopted at the 1655th meeting of the Judicial Committee on June 23, 2015, first amended according to the decision of the 1809th meeting on August 18, 2020, and second amended according to the decision of the 1823rd meeting on December 23, 2020 (article numbers follow the second amended version)
- Quoted passage (Article 15, paragraph 2): "Where the defendant argues that the lending act has not actually occurred and can make a reasonable explanation, the people's court shall, in light of the loan amount, delivery of funds, the parties' economic capacity, the local or the parties' usual transaction methods and practices, changes in the parties' property, witness testimony, and other facts and factors, comprehensively judge and verify whether the lending fact occurred."
- Quoted passage (Article 16): "Where the plaintiff files a private lending lawsuit based only on the transfer voucher of a financial institution, and the defendant argues that the transfer was repayment of a previous loan or other debt between the two parties, the defendant shall provide evidence to prove his claim. After the defendant provides corresponding evidence to prove his claim, the plaintiff shall still bear the burden of proof for the establishment of the lending relationship."
- The two provisions were also checked against the WebSearch summary, and the wording is consistent; the article numbers hold only for the second amended version, and the version is noted when cited

## Determination notes

- Both items have money as the approach: what is gained back is the attribution of the home purchase money (in the range of several hundred thousand to over a million yuan) upon divorce division and the sharing of the debt, and according to the item format's amount threshold (tens of thousands of yuan is large), the benefit magnitude is set as "large"
- All three cost items are zero: writing a one-page statement and signing it on the day of the contribution, and not signing on the spouse's IOU, cost no money and take no time; item 12 requires a little willpower (refusing the family member's or spouse's request to sign), marked willpower=some; item 11 is marked willpower=no
- Both evidence grades are set at A: all come from currently effective laws and judicial interpretation provisions, and the provisions themselves give checkable rules, without relying on judgment
- The main text does not write "an IOU signed by only one party definitely does not count as a joint debt": Article 1064, paragraph 2 of the Civil Code leaves the exception of "used for the joint life of the husband and wife", and when the home purchase money becomes a jointly owned or jointly occupied house, the creditor can often prove this layer, and in practice the rulings vary across regions; this section found no official statistics giving proportions, so the main text only writes the allocation of the burden of proof and the order of the two layers of cross-examination
- Correction made in passing: in the main text of the original item 15 (now item 17), "the health benefit of item 2", "the relationship quality of item 5", and "items 3, 4, and 6" are numbers from an early version of this section and do not match the current items, so they were changed to cite by the current numbers (items 8, 15, 9, 10 to 12, and 16).
