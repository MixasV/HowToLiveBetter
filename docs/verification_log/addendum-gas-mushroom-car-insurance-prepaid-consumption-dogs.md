## III. Approach and benefit magnitude

- Poisonous mushrooms and gas appliances are set as "mortality, large": the intervention is completely avoiding a fatal exposure, not reducing a percentage.
- The compulsory traffic accident insurance item is set as "money, large", and is one of the few "money=a lot" items in the book (commercial third-party liability insurance costs over a thousand yuan a year): what it buys back is tail risk, and there is no backstop for compensation exceeding the compulsory insurance limit.
- Prepayments, live streaming e-commerce, individual account mutual aid, and minors' in-game purchases are all set as "money, medium": the amounts are in the range of several hundred to several tens of thousands of yuan.
- Dog-keeping liability is set as "money, large": medical expenses plus lost work expenses plus vaccines and immunoglobulin commonly exceed ten thousand yuan at a time, and the magnitude is higher when children are injured.
- The minors' in-game purchase item records the time cost as "medium": the real expense is evidence collection and repeated negotiation with the platform, not making the claim itself.
