# Section 8 Addendum "After Being Accused and Falsely Accused" 5 Items · Verification Record (2026-09-09)

Task source: the reader's exact words — "Is there anything about avoiding being falsely accused? In China many women falsely accuse men of rape; a man was locked up for years, innocent, and even with evidence it was useless, and the woman can falsely accuse him casually with no consequences."

## Handling of the reader's premises

There are three checkable assertions in the reader's question, and they were handled separately:

1. **"Many women falsely accuse"** — there are no public statistics on false accusation filings and handling nationwide. Neither the State Council policy document library nor the Supreme People's Procuratorate or Supreme People's Court has false accusation data broken down by gender or by charge, and this book's rule is that figures must be found in the original text, so the main text does not write an incidence rate or a gender distribution, and the remark of item 31 states this. Likewise, the individual case mentioned by the reader could not be located in a checkable official notice this time, so the main text does not cite the individual case.
2. **"Even with evidence it is useless"** — the institutional level is written clearly, and what is missing is enforcement rather than the provisions: Article 55 of the Criminal Procedure Law requires proof beyond a reasonable doubt for conviction, Article 200, item (3) provides that a verdict of not guilty shall be made when the evidence is insufficient, Article 56 requires the exclusion of confessions obtained by torture, and Articles 252 and 253 provide the channels for appeal and retrial. So this became item 32, while the remark states "these are the bottom lines written into the system, not a guarantee of the outcome", and declares that this book has no checkable official data on China's acquittal rate in criminal cases or the rate of sentence reversal on appeal, so no proportions are written.
3. **"Falsely accusing casually has no consequences"** — inaccurate, but the source of the reader's feeling is real. The Public Security Administration Punishments Law (2025 revision), Article 50, lists fabricating facts to falsely accuse and frame as a public security violation, and Article 243 of the Criminal Law is the crime of false accusation and framing; but paragraph 3 of the same article states "where it is not intentional framing but a mistaken accusation, or the report is inaccurate, the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall not apply" — to hold the other party accountable requires proving "fabricating facts" plus "intentional framing", not proving "I did not do it", and the difficulty of proof for these two things differs greatly. The main text of item 31 writes out the provisions and this boundary together, neither exaggerating nor denying.

## Placement

No new section was opened. Section 8's original item 5, "When accused or summoned, hire a lawyer first, do not settle privately, do not delete records", already covers the timing of entrusting a lawyer, the duration of summons, the prohibition of compelled self-incrimination, not settling privately and not deleting records, and the existence of the crime of false accusation and framing and state compensation. What was missing was the preventive side and the specific post-hoc remedies, so following the section's existing practice, 5 items were appended at the end (items 29 to 33), and a sentence pointing to items 31, 32, and 33 was added to the remark of item 5.

## Sourcing and pitfalls encountered

- **Public Security Administration Punishments Law 2025 revision**: the State Council policy document library only contains the 2005 original text (this library does not contain new versions of laws enacted by the National People's Congress), and the SPP law section does not have it either. In the end, the full text reposted on the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau official website (`gaj.beijing.gov.cn`) was used, and the revision adoption date, the original text of Article 50, and the effective date of January 1, 2026 in Article 144 were checked. Following the repository's existing practice, the source column notes "reposted full text".
- **Criminal Law Article 274**: the SPP version is the 1997 original text, and this article was amended by Criminal Law Amendment (VIII) (adding "extortion committed multiple times", and adding the tier of not less than ten years and fines), so it cannot be copied directly. This item follows the consolidated text link already used in the Section 13 nude-chat extortion item (reposted by the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau Traffic Management Bureau), and the amount standards separately cite the original text of Fa Shi [2013] No. 10 (directly accessible on the Supreme People's Court official website).
- **State compensation daily standard**: the SPP release page of May 19, 2026 is directly accessible, and 495.94 yuan, the effective date of May 18, 2026, and the calculation basis were verified verbatim. This figure is updated every May, so the remark of item 33 states "check the figure for the current year when using it; do not directly apply the one in this item".
- **Why item 29 is given B**: Criminal Law Article 236 only writes the four characters "other means", and "inability to give valid consent under intoxication or drowsiness" is the prevailing judicial view; no verbatim-checkable current public interpretation was obtained this time (the Two Supreme People's Courts and Two Ministries interpretation on minors does not apply to the adult side), so the inference from the legal provision to the specific circumstance is handled at level B, and this boundary is stated in the remark.

## Addendum: Item 34 and the Guo Li case (same day, after a reader raised "a man was arrested for claiming compensation over milk powder")

What the reader described is the Guo Li case (the brand involved is "Shien", not Sanlu itself; both belong to the 2008 melamine incident). This case exposed a blind spot in item 30 of this round: item 30 writes "the other party uses a hold over you to demand money and it is handled as extortion", which read literally could make people think "claiming compensation with a high price = extortion", whereas the official conclusion of the Guo Li case is exactly the opposite. Therefore item 34 was added, and the remarks of items 30 and 33 were backfilled.

Official original texts verified:

- **Guangdong Provincial People's Procuratorate retrial recommendation** (reposted by the Heyuan Municipal People's Procuratorate, `jcy.heyuan.gov.cn/node/142`; the original Guangdong Provincial Procuratorate site `gd.jcy.gov.cn` failed local TLS certificate verification, so the repost page is cited and noted in the source column): the examination result of Guo Li's daughter was "several punctate strong echoes visible within the central collecting system of both kidneys"; in June 2009 the two parties reached an agreement, Shien Company compensated 400,000 yuan, and Guo Li issued written materials stating that he would no longer pursue the matter and waived the compensation claim; after that the company reported the case, and the Chao'an County People's Court sentenced him to five years of fixed-term imprisonment for extortion. The three reasons of the procuratorial organ are quoted verbatim in the main text: "the existing evidence is insufficient to prove that Guo Li subjectively had the purpose of illegal possession", "he had the right to claim compensation from the milk powder manufacturer, and his compensation claim had a legal basis", and "consumers have the right to expose and criticize through the mass media acts that harm their lawful rights and interests". On April 7, 2017, the Guangdong Provincial High People's Court declared him not guilty upon retrial.
- **State compensation decision** (selected judicial documents on the Guangdong Court Network, Guangdong Provincial High People's Court Compensation Committee (2018) Yue Wei Pei No. 11): the original sentence of the retrial reversal reason, "the facts that the original trial found that Guo Li, with the purpose of illegal possession, used threats and coercion to forcibly demand property are unclear and the evidence is insufficient, and the nature of Guo Li's act did not exceed the scope of a civil dispute"; the detention was from July 23, 2009 to July 22, 2014, a total of 1,826 days; personal freedom compensation of 472,733.14 yuan was calculated at the 2016 national average daily wage of employees of 258.89 yuan; compensation for mental damage was 165,450 yuan; the portions of Guo Li's claims exceeding this, such as 88.88 million yuan for ten years of mental damage compensation, were not supported, and lost work expenses, compensation for the right to life and health, property losses, lawyer fees, and detention center living expense subsidies were found not to fall within the statutory scope of state compensation.

Writing decisions:

- **Item 34 is given B**, because it is a single case rather than a generalizable proportion. It proves two things at the same time — the compensation claim itself is lawful, and it can still lead to a criminal case — and both the main text and the remark state this, without writing "not guilty" as a necessary outcome.
- **Two details in the reader's paraphrase were not written into the main text**: first, personal circumstances such as "an annual salary of several hundred thousand, a lame leg, and a lost wife", for which there is no verbatim-checkable record in the official documents; second, the specific sequence of "Sanlu asked him to come over, and he was arrested when he went", whereas the procuratorate notice only writes "Guo Li was arrested after the company reported the case", so the main text retains only the operable reminder "be careful when the other party asks you to meet in person at their location; reporting and case filing usually happen at the other party's location", without retelling the details.
- **The remark of item 33 adds the actual calculation of this case**, because it illustrates two easily misunderstood things: the daily standard uses the annual standard applicable at the time the compensation decision is made (the detention ended in 2014, but the 2016 standard of 258.89 yuan was used), and the statutory scope of state compensation does not include lost work expenses, lawyer fees, or property losses.
