Set at level B: the official material gives script breakdowns and individual cases, with no crime rate and no statistic on "how much the fraud rate dropped after teaching this lesson". Benefit magnitude "large" — money approach; the individual case losses in the material are 7,000 yuan to 73,000 yuan, reaching the tens of thousands level; cost money=0 time=little willpower=some (must truly achieve "if scammed, say it first, do not scold"), composite "high".

The landing point of the item is to reduce the six types to one executable judgment: **the last step of all six types is to have the child operate the parent's phone or report a verification code**, so the main text's title is written as this sentence, and the specific types are placed in the benefit column as an identification checklist. Another repeatedly appearing script is "you violated the rules, your parents will go to prison", and the main text points out that this sentence itself is a sign of a scam.

Not used: "Multiple children have already fallen for it! Beware of 'impersonating the police' scams during summer vacation" (2026-08-26) and "Lottery to give autographs, add the idol's private account? Fraud gangs target student star-chasers" (2026-05-13) in the same column overlap with type 5 of this item and are not cited repeatedly. No official source was found for national statistics on minors being defrauded (the Ministry of Public Security official website mps.gov.cn consistently returns 521), so the main text does not write a proportion.
