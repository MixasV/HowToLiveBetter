Content not written into the main text and reasons:

- **"Newborns enrolled within 90 days of birth can enjoy benefits from the date of birth" — the specific number of days is not written.** This is widely circulated, but this round no nationally unified wording that can be checked verbatim was obtained from the Chinese government website or the public documents of the National Healthcare Security Administration; what was obtained was only the 2024 press conference's "enroll online with the birth medical certificate, reimburse upon discharge". Therefore the main text writes only the verifiable part and notes in the remarks that the number of days varies by place and is subject to the local healthcare security department.
- **The specific grading of the five-color maternal pregnancy risk assessment.** The "Work Specifications for Maternal Pregnancy Risk Assessment and Management" is not included in the State Council policy document library (a recheck with `searchfield=title` still returned no hit), and the verbatim-checkable original text cannot be obtained, so the main text writes only the qualitative statement "the earlier the booklet is created, the earlier the pregnancy risk grading is done", without writing the colors and corresponding handling.
- **National penetration rate of painless delivery and China's cesarean section rate.** No verbatim-checkable official annual statistics were obtained, so the main text does not write them.
- **The specific method of counting fetal movements (how many times per hour, how many times in 12 hours).** Formal fetal movement counting schemes have not shown a mortality-reducing effect in large randomized trials, which is not the same thing as "markedly reduced fetal movement requires medical care"; the main text retains only the latter (merged into the CDC list in item 7) and does not give a counting standard.
- Item 8 is set at B rather than A: the Beijing Municipal Health Commission science popularization page gives the handling actions but does not explain the mechanism of umbilical cord prolapse; the mechanism sentence is taken from the Beijing Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital (official website of a public medical institution), and neither material is a guideline or meta-analysis.
- The benefit magnitude of item 10 is set as "medium" rather than "large": the WHO statement gives the negative conclusion "no further improvement above 10%", not the effect size of "how much mortality can be reduced by not performing cesarean sections without medical indication".
