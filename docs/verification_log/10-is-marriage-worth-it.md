### Civil Code of the People's Republic of China
- National Laws and Regulations Database page <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?title=...&id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>: WebFetch rendered only to the navigation (single-page application); curl was used to call its backend interface `<https://flk.npc.gov.cn/law-search/search/flfgDetails?bbbs=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf`, which returned JSON: title "Civil Code of the People's Republic of China", flxz "Law", zdjgName "National People's Congress", gbrq "2020-05-28", sxrq "2021-01-01", and the provision tree contains nodes for Articles 1062 to 1066, 1076 to 1079, and 1088; the interface gives only article numbers, not the text, and its PDF attachment is an image version from which text cannot be extracted
- The text of the provisions was verified from the Supreme People's Court gazette repost page <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html> ("Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (Continued)", Laws and Regulations section): fetched with curl and parsed locally
- Quoted passages:
  - Article 1062: "The following property acquired by the husband and wife during the existence of the marriage relationship is the joint property of the husband and wife and is jointly owned by the husband and wife: (1) wages, bonuses, and remuneration for labor services; (2) income from production, business, and investment; (3) income from intellectual property rights; (4) property inherited or received as a gift, except as provided in Article 1063, paragraph 1, item (3) of this Law; (5) other property that should be jointly owned. The husband and wife have equal rights to dispose of the joint property."
  - Article 1063: "The following property is the personal property of one spouse: (1) one party's premarital property; (2) compensation or indemnification obtained by one party for personal injury; (3) property determined by a will or gift contract to belong only to one party; (4) daily necessities for the exclusive use of one party; (5) other property that should belong to one party."
  - Article 1065: "A man and a woman may agree that the property acquired during the existence of the marriage relationship and premarital property shall be owned separately, jointly, or partly separately and partly jointly. The agreement shall be in written form. If there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, the provisions of Articles 1062 and 1063 of this Law shall apply. An agreement between husband and wife on the property acquired during the existence of the marriage relationship and premarital property is legally binding on both parties."
  - Article 1076: "Where both husband and wife voluntarily divorce, they shall sign a written divorce agreement and personally apply to the marriage registration authority for divorce registration. The divorce agreement shall state the expression of intent of both parties to divorce voluntarily and the consensus opinions on matters such as child support, property, and debt handling."
  - Article 1077: "Within 30 days from the date the marriage registration authority receives the divorce registration application, if either party is unwilling to divorce, he or she may withdraw the divorce registration application from the marriage registration authority. Within 30 days after the expiration of the period stipulated in the preceding paragraph, both parties shall personally apply to the marriage registration authority for the issuance of the divorce certificate; if they do not apply, the divorce registration application shall be deemed withdrawn."
  - Article 1079: "Where one spouse requests a divorce, the relevant organization may mediate or the spouse may directly file a divorce lawsuit with the people's court. The people's court trying a divorce case shall conduct mediation; if the affection is indeed broken and mediation is ineffective, a divorce shall be granted. Divorce shall be granted if mediation is ineffective under any of the following circumstances: (1) bigamy or cohabitation with another person; (2) domestic violence or abuse or abandonment of family members; (3) repeated gambling, drug use, and other bad habits that are not corrected despite repeated education; (4) separation for two full years due to incompatibility; (5) other circumstances causing the breakdown of the husband and wife's affection. … After the people's court has ruled not to grant a divorce, if the parties have again lived apart for one full year and one party again files a divorce lawsuit, a divorce shall be granted."
  - Article 1088: "Where one spouse bears more obligations such as raising children, caring for the elderly, and assisting the other spouse in work, he or she has the right to request compensation from the other spouse upon divorce, and the other spouse shall give compensation. The specific method shall be agreed upon by both parties; if no agreement is reached, the people's court shall make a judgment."

### Roelfs 2011, Am J Epidemiol 174(4):379–389, doi 10.1093/aje/kwr111

- Quoted passage: "The authors used meta-analysis to examine 641 risk estimates from 95 publications that provided data on more than 500 million persons. The comparison group consisted of currently married individuals. The mean hazard ratio for mortality was 1.24 (95% confidence interval: 1.19, 1.30) among multivariate-adjusted hazard ratios with a high subjective quality rating.
