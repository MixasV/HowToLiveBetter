# Verification Record: HowToLiveLonger Merge + Sections 14 to 19 + Supplements to Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 13

Date: 2026-09-07. This round all sources were opened one by one and the title, authors, journal volume and pages, document number, and figures were checked.

## I. Comparison with geekan/HowToLiveLonger

The original text was taken from <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/geekan/HowToLiveLonger/main/README.md> (33,511 bytes).
The evidence chain of that project is mostly "Zhihu/Sohu/NetEase paraphrase + source link", and this project's citation rules only accept original literature, so each item was checked again against the original text.

Merged in (14 items, see the item-by-item verification below): nuts, coffee, tea, white meat replacing red meat, betel nut, chili, milk, eggs, toothbrushing and oral care, racket sports, fragmented vigorous activity, sun exposure, sweetened beverages (including sugar-free), and bathing; plus pessimism (placed in Section 3).

Not merged in and reasons:
- Multivitamins and glucosamine: Section 6 of this book already included them as "do not buy" based on randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses, which is contrary to the conclusions of the single observational studies cited by that project, so this book's original judgment is retained.
- Metformin, spermidine, NMN: no human trials proving life extension in healthy people were found, so they are not written this round.
- Sodium: that project cites Messerli 2021 to argue that the higher the sodium intake, the lower the mortality. The original text was verified to indeed be an ecological study of 181 countries, and its evidence level is lower than the SSaSS randomized controlled trial cited in Section 2 of this book, so the conclusion is not changed, and it is only written into the dispute part of that item's "note".
- Calorie restriction, juice, COVID, wealth and poverty: not yet handled.
