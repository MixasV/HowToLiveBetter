# Section 12 Addendum: Three Items — Registration, Tax Filing, and Tax-Related Fraud · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task source: the reader asked about the process, materials, and costs of starting a company and how to file taxes, and said that after registering as an individual business, they received a call impersonating the government and were led to the other party's official account mini-program to pay per use for "tax filing on their behalf". The check found that among the original 14 items in Section 12 there was no registration process, no filing obligation, and no such fraud, so three items were added this round, and one outdated tax citation was fixed in passing.

Order adjustment: the new items are inserted in the order of starting a business, and the original items 6 to 14 shift accordingly, making this section now 17 items. The order is entity selection (3) → registration (6) → licenses (7) → filing obligation (8) → invoices (9) → tax-related fraud (10). There is no cross-reference in the form of "Section 12, item N" within the section, so the shift does not affect other items.

Tool note: this round two retrieval subtasks first looked for the official original texts separately, and then the main task checked the key quoted passages item by item. The "Rechecked" column in the table below indicates which ones the main task personally opened the original page to verify. npc.gov.cn reported an SSL handshake failure via WebFetch, so the legal provisions instead cite the State Taxation Administration regulations database (fgk.chinatax.gov.cn) and the Supreme People's Procuratorate portal; mps.gov.cn returns 521 across the whole site, and the original page of the Ministry of Public Security still cannot be obtained.

## One outdated citation fixed in passing

The original item 7 (now item 9), "invoices are only issued according to real transactions", cited the Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration Announcement No. 19 of 2023, "monthly sales of 100,000 yuan or less are exempt from VAT". Upon verification, the VAT Law has been effective since 2026-01-01, and the threshold is now prescribed by the State Council: Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration Announcement No. 10 of 2026, Article 1 "From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027 … (1) where one month is the tax calculation period, the threshold is monthly sales of 100,000 yuan. Where one quarter is the tax calculation period, the threshold is quarterly sales of 300,000 yuan. (2) where tax is paid per transaction, the threshold is 1,000 yuan per transaction (day)"; Article 6 "the domestic VAT preferential policies prescribed in documents issued before December 31, 2025 shall cease to be implemented at the same time"; the supporting State Taxation Administration Announcement No. 4 of 2026 has clearly repealed Announcement No. 1 of 2023. The amount threshold has not changed, but the basis has been completely replaced, and the item has been rewritten according to Article 23 of the VAT Law and Announcement No. 10 of 2026, with the pages opened and verified by the main task.
