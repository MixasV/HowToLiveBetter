## Candidates not included and reasons
- Drunk riding an electric bicycle: the distinction standards and precedents for electric bicycles and electric motorcycles are only on China Court Network (which cannot be opened); Guiding Case No. 271, after opening, is an assisted-driving drunk-driving case and unrelated to electric bicycles, so it was not included under the requirement "only write what can be clearly verified"; the title of Section 8, item 4 already covers electric bicycles.
- Fireworks and firecrackers, open-air fires: the 2025 version of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law has no general provision on setting them off (only Article 28 on setting them off in sports and cultural venues and Article 43 on launching open-flame airborne objects), and the original texts of the Fireworks and Firecrackers Safety Management Regulations and the Forest Fire Prevention Regulations were not verified, so they were not included.
- Lending two cards / the crime of assisting information network criminal activities, online defamation: already covered in Section 8; item 4 of this section only writes the angle of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and cross-references it.
