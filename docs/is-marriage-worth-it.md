# Is marriage worthwhile? Divide one confusing account into five clear accounts

The reader is asking an overall question: Is marriage a loss or a gain? Can the other party provide emotional value, share housework, make money, or just do tasks for the elders.This question is impossible to answer as a whole because it adds together several things that are not convertible to each other.This article breaks it down, using only figures that can be found in official statistics or meta-analyses for each account, explaining clearly where the calculations cannot be made, and finally giving a list that you can fill in by yourself.The whole article does not draw a conclusion on whether it should be knotted or not.

## 1. Break the problem apart first

"Is it a good deal to get married?" contains at least five accounts with different calibers:

1. Money: direct expenses (bride price, wedding, wedding room) and legal property rules.The former has no official statistics, while the latter has regulations.
2. Time: Housework, caregiving, unpaid labor in concessions to each other’s work.Caliber is daily minutes.
3. Health: Differences in mortality between married and unmarried people.Caliber is relative risk, and only relative.
4. Legal risks: how to divide the property, how long it will take to exit, and what obligations can be compensated for.The caliber is the provisions.
5. Relationship quality: emotional value, conflict, support.Caliber is the effect size and cannot be exchanged for money or time.

The fifth stroke is the hardest to quantify, but it’s closest to what the reader is really asking.

## 2. Data and sources of each account

### Registration data: marriage rate and divorce rate are two rough rates

The Ministry of Civil Affairs' "Statistical Bulletin on the Development of Civil Affairs in 2024": In 2024, 6.106 million pairs of marriages were registered according to law across the country, a decrease of 20.5% from the previous year, and the marriage rate was 4.3‰; 3.513 million couples handled divorce procedures in accordance with the law, of which 2.622 million couples were registered for divorce by the civil affairs department, and 891,000 couples were divorced by court judgment or mediation, with a divorce rate of 2.5‰.The annotation of the communiqué states the calculation formula: divide the number of marriages (divorces) in that year by the average total population in that year, and then multiply by 1000‰.

The denominator of both numbers is the entire population, so it is called roughness. The answer is "How many couples registered for marriage or divorce per 1,000 people this year". The answer is not "Whether a marriage will eventually divorce."The latter requires queue data, which is not found in the official original text of this article.

### Health account: Married people have lower mortality rates, but this is correlation, not causation

Three meta-analyses gave consistent magnitudes:

- Manzoli et al. (2007) summarized 53 comparison groups and more than 250,000 elderly people. The RR of all-cause death for married versus unmarried (including widowed, divorced or separated, and unmarried) was 0.88 (95% CI 0.85–0.91); widowed 1.11, divorced or separated 1.16, and unmarried1.11; does not vary with gender, study quality, or European and American regions; most conservative recalculation RR 0.94.
- Roelfs et al. (2011) summarized 95 documents and more than 500 million people. The HR of all-cause death for never married people compared with married people was 1.24 (1.19–1.30); the effect decreased with age, and the higher the quality of the study, the smaller the effect.
- Wang et al. (2020) summarized 21 studies and more than 7.89 million people. Non-marriage is related to all-cause, cancer, and cardiovascular death in both genders. Men have a stronger association with cardiovascular and all-cause death. Compared with women, men who have never been married have a 31% higher risk of stroke death and a 9% higher risk of all-cause death.

All three were observational studies: People who are healthy, have high incomes, and socialize more are inherently more likely to get married and live longer, which is called a selection effect; Manzoli found signs of publication bias.These numbers should read "mortality rates are lower among married people" rather than "getting married makes you 12% less likely to die."The samples are mainly from Europe and the United States, and can only be used as reference for domestic purposes.

### Time Account: The gender gap in unpaid labor is a ceiling reference

The Third National Time Use Survey of the National Bureau of Statistics (May 2024): Participants in the field of unpaid labor spend an average of 2 hours and 45 minutes a day, men 1 hour 52 minutes, and women 3 hours 29 minutes; the participation rate is 75.6%, men 67.5%, and women 83.9%.Broken down, residents spend an average of 1 hour and 17 minutes on housework per day (participants: 1 hour and 59 minutes, 28 minutes less than in 2018, and a participation rate of 64.9%); they spend an average of 30 minutes on accompanying and caring for family members (participants: 1 hour and 46 minutes, a participation rate of 28.4%).

The second survey in 2018: Residents spent an average of 1 hour and 26 minutes doing housework, 45 minutes for men and 2 hours and 6 minutes for women, with a participation rate of 40.4% for men and 75.6% for women; an average of 36 minutes for accompanying and caring for children, 17 minutes for men and 53 minutes for women, with a participation rate of 12.3% for men and 25.1% for women.

Both surveys were only grouped by gender, urban and rural areas, and age, but not by marital status. Therefore, there are no official figures on "how much more housework you do every day after getting married."What can be used is the gender gap: the difference in unpaid labor between men and women in 2024 is 1 hour and 37 minutes, which can be used as the upper limit of the gap that may occur if the division of labor is not clearly discussed.

### Money Accounts: Default Rules, Written Agreements, and Housework Compensation

There are no official statistics on betrothal gifts, weddings, and wedding rooms, so this article will not include figures.What can be written are the rules in the Marriage and Family Section of the Civil Code (effective from January 1, 2021):

- Article 1062: Wages, bonuses, remuneration for labor services, income from production, operation, investment, income from intellectual property rights, inherited or donated property (except those that are clearly owned by one party only in a will or gift contract) earned during the marriage relationship are joint property of the husband and wife, and the husband and wife have equal rights to deal with it.
- Article 1063: One party's pre-marital property, compensation or compensation for personal injury, property determined to belong only to one party in a will or gift contract, and daily necessities exclusively used by one party are personal property.
- Article 1065: Both men and women may agree that the property before and after marriage shall be owned by each other or jointly, or may be partly owned by each other or partly jointly owned. The agreement shall be in writing and shall be legally binding on both parties. If there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, the preceding two articles shall apply.
- Article 1088: If one spouse has greater responsibilities due to raising children, taking care of the elderly, assisting the other spouse in work, etc., the spouse has the right to request compensation from the other spouse when divorcing. The specific method shall be agreed upon by both parties. Failure to reach an agreement shall be decided by the court.

This set of rules is useful for both parties: the party with the higher income will look at Articles 1062 and 1065, and the party with more unpaid labor will look at Article 1088.The agreement only requires writing, and notarization is not a legal requirement.

### Exit costs: cooling-off period and litigation conditions

- Article 1076: If both parties want to divorce voluntarily, they must sign a written divorce agreement and apply in person to the marriage registration office. The agreement should state the unanimous opinions on child support, property and debt settlement.
- Article 1077: Within thirty days from the date of receipt of the application by the registration authority, either party may withdraw the divorce if he or she is unwilling to do so; both parties shall apply in person for issuance of a divorce certificate within thirty days after the expiration of the period. Failure to apply shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.
- Article 1079: If one party requests a divorce, he or she may mediate or directly file a lawsuit; the court shall mediate first, and divorce shall be granted if the relationship has truly broken down and mediation is ineffective; divorce shall be granted if bigamy or cohabitation with others, domestic violence or abuse and abandonment, gambling, drug abuse and other bad habits persist, or separation for more than two years due to emotional discord and mediation is ineffective; and divorce shall be granted if the divorce is ineffective and the person has been separated for one year after being pronounced inseparable.

Of the 3.513 million divorces in 2024, 891,000 will be through court judgment or mediation.The cooling-off period only applies to registered divorces.

### Relationship quality: more worthy of calculation than "whether you are married or not"

Robles et al. (2014) summarized 126 studies and more than 72,000 people: higher marital quality is associated with better health, with effect sizes r ranging from 0.07 to 0.21, where the risk of death r = 0.11 and the cardiovascular response to marital conflict r =−0.13; although the effect size is small, it is of the same magnitude as the association between diet and other health behaviors and health; some outcomes are subject to publication bias; studies that explicitly test gender differences have basically found no differences between men and women; most designs cannot infer cause and effect.Dhindsa et al.'s (2020) cardiovascular review also noted that marital dissatisfaction and marital quality have a significant impact on cardiovascular risk.

This article did not find a meta-analysis that directly compared the mortality rates of "low-quality marriage" and "unmarried". "Bad marriage is worse than no marriage" is currently an inference, not a numerical conclusion.A tenable argument is that the effect of "whether it is knotted or not" has a selection effect, and "whether the quality is good or not" is another independent variable. Decisions that only look at the status without looking at the quality will miss the second account.

## 3. How to calculate for yourself: a fill-in-the-blank list

Fill in the numbers separately according to caliber. Numbers of different calibers do not add up.If you can't fill in the box, write "I don't know." This is information in itself.

What can the other party provide (by caliber)

- Money: How much is the other party’s income, liabilities, and pre-marital property? Should post-marital property be based on default rules or written agreement, and what is the content of the agreement.
- Time: How much unpaid labor does the other party do every day (national average: 1 hour and 52 minutes for male participants and 3 hours and 29 minutes for female participants); how to divide it after marriage; how to divide it when there are children or elderly people to take care of; whose work can be compromised.
- Relationship quality: How many conflicts have you had in the past six months and how did they end; what did the other party do when you were sick, unemployed, or depressed; what did you do in the same situation.
- Emotional value: Whether your energy increases or decreases when you are with the other person; it takes several months of getting along to determine this.
, the condition list cannot be seen.

What do you have to pay (by caliber)

- Money: How much money is spent on the marriage itself (betrothal gift, wedding, wedding room), and where this money would be used if not married.
- Time: How much unpaid labor is expected to increase every day, and how many favors are expected to increase every week.
- Health: Will your daily routine, diet, and exercise get better or worse due to marriage? This is closer to you than the RR in the meta-analysis.
- Exit cost: Registering for divorce requires a 30-day cooling-off period plus 30 days to apply, and filing a lawsuit requires meeting the conditions of Article 1079; can you bear this cycle?

How to protect yourself legally (applicable to both parties)

- Is there any evidence for the pre-marital property (house purchase contract, bank statement, gift contract, will)? Can it be proved that it was "pre-marital" or "clearly belongs to only one party".
- Should a written property agreement be required? Should the agreement cover pre-marital property, post-marital income, or both?
- Who will bear more unpaid labor after marriage? Does this party know the right to claim compensation in Article 1088? Are both parties willing to agree on compensation methods in advance?
- Debt: Which amounts borrowed in individual names after marriage are considered joint debts, and whether both parties know about it.

Fill in the column for elders’ marriage separately.

- What are the benefits to the elders: peace of mind, face, stop urging marriage, and someone to provide for them in old age.
- Which of these benefits belong to the elders, and which are actually your own.
- The costs in the above three columns are all borne by yourself, and the benefits from your elders will not automatically be converted into your own health or relationship quality.
- After writing separately, it is up to the person filling out the form to decide whether the account is important or light.

## 4. Common misunderstandings

Divorce rates are confusing.The crude divorce rate (number of divorces per 1,000 people, 2.5‰ in 2024) and "divorce ratio" (number of divorces divided by number of marriages in that year, 351.3 / 610.6 ≈ 57.5%) are two different things.The numerator and denominator of the divorce ratio are not the same group of people. Most of the people who divorced this year were married many years ago. The decline in the number of marriages will mechanically push up the divorce ratio.It is not "half of marriages will divorce", and there is no such indicator in the communiqué.

Treat correlation as causation.The mortality rate of married people is about 12% lower. This is the combined result of meta-analysis. However, health, income, and social interaction all affect "whether you can get married" and "whether you can live longer" at the same time.This account can be calculated, but it must be discounted, and it cannot be used to replace the judgment of the specific relationship.

Make marriage a mission.Task-based marriage only looks at "status" and not "quality." The effect of relationship quality on health is another account independent of marital status, and is often omitted in task-based decisions.

Think of the national average as your number.The gender gap in the time use survey is the national average; the property rule is the default and can be changed in writing.The purpose of national figures is to give magnitude and a starting point for negotiations, not to give a conclusion.

Where there are no official figures, do not use private figures.There are no official statistics on betrothal gifts, weddings, and parenting costs. This article is left blank. Readers filling in their own figures are more accurate than citing estimates from any agency.
