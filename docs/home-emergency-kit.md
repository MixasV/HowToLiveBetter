# Home emergency gear checklist: What to buy, where to put it, and how often to check it

Corresponds to README Section 1, Item 26.This list only answers "what should be kept at home" without repeating how to use it: fire escape, cardiopulmonary resuscitation, treatment of heavy bleeding, burns, and carbon monoxide poisoning are all in Section 13, smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms are in Section 1, Item 3, and gas hoses and stoves are in Section 1, Item 4.

## 2. Firefighting three-piece set

**Fire extinguisher.** Portable fire extinguishers are compulsory certification products and should have the CCC mark on the product; the implementation standard is GB 4351-2023 "Portable Fire Extinguishers".Pressure-storage fire extinguishers are equipped with a pressure indicator (except carbon dioxide fire extinguishers). The pointer should point within the green zone. If it points to the red zone or yellow zone, it will become invalid.The scrappage period is based on XF 95: 6 years for water-based type, 10 years for dry powder, and 12 years for carbon dioxide. The steel stamp on the bottle body can be found from the factory date.For home use, ABC dry powder is generally chosen. Place one at the door of the kitchen and one at the entrance. Do not put it in the cabinet directly above the stove. If it is out of reach in the event of a fire, it means it is not there.

**Fire blanket.** It is listed alongside the fire extinguisher in the official list. It has two uses: to cover the oil pan fire and to wear it on the body to escape.Keep one in your kitchen drawer, it doesn't take up space and it won't expire.When the pot catches fire, turn off the heat and then cover it. Do not open it immediately after covering it.

**Escape breathing mask.** The official name is Fire Filtering Self-Rescuing Respirator, which implements GB 21976.7-2012 "Building Fire Escape and Refuge Equipment Part 7: Filtering Fire Self-Rescuing Respirator". It is the current mandatory national standard and was implemented on June 1, 2013. The review conclusion in 2023 is that it will continue to be valid.When buying, look for the standard number and CCC mark. One for each person in the family, and place it within reach of the bedside in the bedroom. Do not lock it in the storage room.It relies on a canister to absorb poisons in smoke. It is disposable, has a rated protection time, and has only one purpose: put it on and go outside.TODO (To be verified: The standard's original description of the applicable lower limit of oxygen content and rated protection time. ## 3. What to put in the first aid kit?

The official list only says "external medicines: hemostatic powder, hemostatic patches, gauze bandages, etc., used to treat wounds."Based on the verified disposal actions in Section 13, reasonable contents of a home first aid kit are:

- Hemostasis: a spinning tourniquet, sterile gauze and bandages, triangle towel, and medical tape.Tourniquet usage, contraindications and "do not loosen to bleed" see Section 13, Article 11
- Debridement: iodophor cotton swabs, saline, band-aids, sterile dressings, disposable gloves, scissors and forceps
- Burns and scalds: no ointment is required, cold running water for 20 minutes is enough to treat them, see Section 13, Article 13
- Records: A card with a list of family members’ allergies, chronic diseases and common medications. Give it directly to first responders when they arrive at the scene.

People with known severe allergies should also prepare an epinephrine auto-injector pen, which is a prescription drug and should be prescribed by a doctor, see Section 13, Article 14.

This section of the content list is Level C: the official document only gives the four words "external medicines", and the specific varieties are inferred based on the first aid actions that have been verified in this book, and there is no direct documentation.

## 4. Lighting, communication and distress

Choose a flashlight that is waterproof and explosion-proof according to official instructions, and charge or replace the battery regularly; a lifeguard whistle is used to continuously send signals when trapped, which is more labor-intensive than shouting and can also penetrate noise; a radio is used to receive emergency broadcasts.The Beijing version also lists emergency escape ropes and multifunctional combination scissors.Mobile phone power bank is considered to be the most cost-effective item in this category, and most families already have one.

## 5. Check once a year, ten minutes

- Fire extinguisher: Check whether the pointer of the pressure gauge is in the green zone, and check whether the factory date of the bottle has reached the end of life.
- Smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms: press the test button and replace the batteries once a year, see Section 1, Item 3
- Respiratory mask: Whether the packaging is damaged and whether the nominal shelf life has been exceeded
- First aid kit: whether the medicines and dressings have expired, and whether the lock of the tourniquet can still be locked
- Flashlight and power bank: whether there is still power

The original words of the Beijing Municipal Emergency Management Bureau are "Regularly inspect the status of emergency supplies and promptly replace emergency supplies that have expired."Schedule it to be done together on the day when the alarm battery is replaced every year, and it will be done in one go.

## 6. No need to buy

- **Home AED.** The unit price is $1,500–15,000, and there is no evidence to support self-purchase in the family scene.The correct action in a cardiac arrest is to perform compressions immediately, ask someone to call 120 and go to the nearest public place to get an AED, see Section 13, Item 1
- **"Fire mask" and "Gas mask" without CCC mark.** Article 24 of the Fire Protection Law stipulates that fire protection products subject to compulsory product certification in accordance with the law "can only be produced, sold, and used after they have been certified by a legally qualified certification agency in accordance with the mandatory requirements of national standards and industry standards."The poisons that industrial canisters prevent are not the same thing as fire smoke.
- **A complete set of "Emergency Gift Packs".** This type of bag usually contains flashlights and masks at low prices. It saves money to buy fire extinguishers, fire blankets, masks and first aid kits separately, and it is easier to check the certification.
- **Store food and water for more than three days.** The official list is 3 days per person, the excess is overdue loss, see Section 5, Article 24

## 7. How useful are these equipments?

The honest answer is: the evidence is weaker than the other entries in Section 1 of the book.

A Cochrane systematic review of 98 studies and 2,605,044 people assessing home safety education and the provision of safety equipment found that home safety interventions may reduce the incidence of injury, IRR 0.89 (95% CI 0.78 to 1.01), with a confidence interval spanning 1; interventions delivered in the home, IRR 0.75 (95% CI 0.62 to 0.91); and interventions without the provision of safety equipment IRR0.78 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.92).In other words, the effect is more like education and transformation from home entry, rather than from self-made things.The review concluded otherwise: There was no evidence that home safety interventions reduced the incidence of thermal burns or poisoning.

In the same review, the intervention did increase the proportion of safe behaviors: having a working smoke alarm OR 1.81 (95% CI 1.30 to 2.52), having an escape plan OR 2.01 (1.45 to 2.77), properly storing medicines OR 1.53 (1.27 to 1.84), and having protective doors on stairs OR 1.61 (1.19 to 2.17).

Source: Kendrick D et al (2012). Home safety education and provision of safety equipment for injury prevention. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD005014.pub3>

Therefore, the positioning of this section is: buying all at once according to the official list is a low-cost and reasonable preparation, but don’t think of it as buying it to be safe.Smoke alarms, escape plans, and removing the source of injury are the three items that have been repeatedly shown to be effective by research. These three items are not on the shopping list, or only account for a very small part of it.
