# 7. How to Survive When Broke

This section's dimension is money and protection: how much you can receive, how much is waived, what services you get, and the conditions. No conversion to lifespan. Policies change yearly and vary by region; all amounts and thresholds follow the current year's local announcements. Ordering prioritizes "zero cost, few formalities, fast effect"; items requiring long-term qualification or high regional variation come later; experiential items go last.

### 1. If unemployed, first apply for unemployment insurance online
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; apply via the "Palm 12333" app, e-social-security-card mini program (WeChat/Alipay), or the national social insurance service platform; social security card or ID suffices; most regions need no in-person visit; prerequisite is completed unemployment registration.
- Say it in plain language: If your employer and you have together paid for 1+ years and you didn't quit voluntarily, you can claim after unemployment registration: 1–<5 years pays up to 12 months, 5–10 years up to 18 months, 10+ years up to 24 months. The amount is set by each province — below local minimum wage, above subsistence allowance. During this period, unemployment insurance pays your employee medical insurance. Voluntary resignation doesn't count.
- Benefit: Conditions: cumulative unemployment insurance contributions by unit and self of 1+ years, interruption of employment not due to personal will, completed unemployment registration with job-seeking intent; 1–<5 years cumulative contributions max 12 months, 5–<10 years max 18 months, 10+ years max 24 months; amount set by provincial government, below local minimum wage and above urban subsistence allowance, with the national target of gradually raising it to 90% of the minimum wage (nationwide, regulations effective 1999).
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (1999). Regulations on Unemployment Insurance (State Council Order No. 258, Articles 14, 17, 18). <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=517>; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security. Unemployment insurance standards to be gradually raised to 90% of minimum wage. <https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fggz/jyysr/jysrsbxf/202206/t20220627_1328819.html>

Amounts vary greatly by province — follow the insured location's announcement. ### 2. If wages are withheld, first complain to labor inspection, then apply for labor arbitration — both routes are free, and most cases conclude within months
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; complaint and arbitration are both free; prepare the labor contract, payslips, attendance records, transfers, and chat logs; visit the HR bureau or arbitration committee 1–3 times; arbitration period is 1 year, but arrears during employment aren't subject to the 1-year limit — file within 1 year of leaving.
- Say it in plain language: Both routes are free. First call 12333 or file a complaint at the local HR bureau's labor inspection; once filed, investigation concludes within 60 working days, ordering payment by a deadline, with additional compensation of 50%–100% for lateness — in 2024 nationwide this recovered 8.65 billion yuan for 654,000 people. If that fails, apply for labor arbitration, concluding in 45 days; claims within 12× the local monthly minimum wage are final-instance, and the company can't appeal. But distinguish "case concluded" from "money in hand": if the company has no assets or the boss vanished, even a winning ruling may not be enforceable. The limitation period is 1 year — file within a year of leaving.
- Benefit: Step one: call 12333 or file at the local labor inspection. Once legally filed, investigation concludes within 60 working days (complex cases may extend 30 working days), can order payment by a deadline, and add 50%–100% compensation on the amount overdue; in 2024, inspection agencies nationwide handled 137,000 cases and recovered 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654,000 people. With an IOU or salary table and clear arrears facts, you can directly apply for a payment order at the basic court, issued within 15 days of acceptance. Step two: apply for arbitration — legally concluded within 45 days (extendable 15), wage claims can apply for advance execution, amounts within 12× local monthly minimum wage are final-instance and the company can't appeal; in 2024, 4.257 million cases handled nationwide, 4.156 million concluded, total 93.47 billion yuan, 79.6% mediation success, 98.2% arbitration conclusion rate, 73.7% arbitration finality rate. "Six months to a year" comes from the portion going to court after arbitration: first-instance 6 months (summary procedure 3 months) plus second-instance 3 months; in 2024 courts concluded 88,000 wage recovery cases, recovering 28.54 billion yuan, and 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A

### 3. If you can't afford a lawsuit, apply for legal aid — wage claims, alimony, and work-injury cases are already in scope
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; legal aid charges the aided person nothing.
- Say it in plain language: Wage claims, alimony, social security or subsistence benefits, and losses from righteous acts are already within free legal aid scope — call 12348 or visit the local legal aid center. Since 2022, no need to go back to your home registration to get financial hardship certificates; honestly stating your situation is enough. Cases with possible death sentences and blind, deaf-mute, and minor defendants are assigned lawyers regardless of financial status.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation's civil aid scope includes "lawfully requesting state compensation; requesting social insurance benefits or minimum living security benefits; requesting pensions or relief funds; requesting maintenance, child support, or alimony payments; requesting payment of labor remuneration; asserting civil rights and interests arising from righteous acts." Criminally, cases with possible death sentences and blind, deaf-mute, and minor defendants are assigned lawyers regardless of financial status.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2003). Regulations on Legal Aid (Order No. 385, Articles 10, 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>; Ministry of Justice official (2021). Q&A on implementing the Legal Aid Law. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Note: The Legal Aid Law effective January 1, 2022 changed "submit financial hardship certificate" to "honestly state financial situation" — no more trip back home for a certificate. Entry points are local legal aid centers, public legal service centers, or calling 12348.

### 4. When truly destitute, go to an assistance station — room, board, and return ticket provided
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=some benefit=medium dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; go to the local assistance management station (or tell police/civil affairs), honestly register your name and belongings.
- Say it in plain language: They provide food, lodging, medical care for sudden illness, help contacting family, and a ticket home if you can't afford it. Usually no more than 10 days; voluntary in and out — not internment, and no cash. Tickets are issued to the registered-residence or residence address, so decide where you're going before you go.
- Benefit: Assistance stations provide sanitary food, lodging, emergency medical care for sudden illness at the station, help contacting relatives and employers, and travel vouchers for those without transportation money to return to registered residence or residence address; assistance period generally no more than 10 days, extendable while awaiting pickup, treatment, or placement; police and other administrative officers who encounter vagrants during duty should inform them of assistance options, and guide/escort the disabled, minors, elderly, and those with mobility difficulties (nationwide, effective 2003).
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2003). Measures for the Administration of Assistance to Urban Vagrants and Beggars Without Livelihood (State Council Order No. 381, Articles 5, 6, 7). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>; Ministry of Civil Affairs (2003). Implementation Rules for the Measures for the Administration of Assistance to Urban Vagrants and Beggars Without Livelihood (Ministry of Civil Affairs Order No. 24, Articles 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Note: Voluntary assistance, voluntary departure — not internment. Basic food and lodging only; no cash. Travel vouchers are issued to registered-residence or residence address; think clearly about where you're going before seeking help.

### 5. For acute severe conditions, treatment comes before payment; call 120 even without money
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=large dimension=mortality -->
- Cost: 0 yuan phone call; treatment costs after emergency care are separate.
- Say it in plain language: Emergency centers and hospitals must not refuse, stall, or delay treatment of acute severe injuries over cost issues; violators are ordered to correct and responsible persons disciplined. Emergency costs for unidentified or genuinely unable-to-pay people are paid by the disease emergency rescue fund. So call 120 even without money. The fund only covers the emergency segment; subsequent hospitalization is separate.
- Benefit: Emergency centers (stations) and emergency network hospitals must not refuse or delay pre-hospital emergency care over cost issues; violators are ordered to correct and responsible persons disciplined; medical institutions at all levels and of all types must not refuse, stall, or delay treatment of acute severe injured patients for any reason; emergency costs for unidentified or unable-to-pay people are paid by the disease emergency rescue fund (nationwide, from 2013, 2014).
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2013). Guiding Opinions on Establishing a Disease Emergency Rescue System (Guobanfa [2013] No. 15). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission (2013). Measures for the Administration of Pre-hospital Emergency Medical Care (Order No. 3, Articles 25, 37). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Note: The fund only covers the emergency phase; subsequent hospitalization and treatment costs are still self-paid or via medical insurance/medical assistance (see item 8). Don't delay emergencies for fear of the bill.

### 6. For work, start with free public employment services and informal-labor markets; skip paid agencies
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=medium dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; register at county-level-and-above public employment service agencies (employment service centers, human resources markets) or local informal-labor markets, or call 12333 for addresses.
- Say it in plain language: Public employment service agencies (employment service centers, human resources markets) provide by law free job placement, vocational guidance, policy consultation, and employment/unemployment registration; the law explicitly prohibits them from operating commercially. Informal-labor market information registration and posting are also free. Do unemployment registration along the way — both unemployment benefits and identifying employment-difficult persons require it.
- Benefit: Legal free provision of employment policy consultation, job supply/demand information, vocational guidance and placement, employment and unemployment registration, and employment assistance for employment-difficult persons; agencies must not engage in business activities. Informal-labor-market job-seeking/recruitment information registration and posting are provided free to the public, included in public employment information services, with enhanced assistance for informal workers with long waiting times or low-income households (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2007, 2015 revision). Employment Promotion Law (Article 35). <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security et al. (2022). Opinions on Strengthening Construction of Informal-Labor Markets and Improving Job-Seeking Recruitment Services (Renshebu Fa [2022] No. 38). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Note: Unemployment registration is the prerequisite for unemployment insurance benefits and identifying employment-difficult persons — do it along the way. For daily-paid informal work, verify the wage on the spot, keep contact details and records.

### 7. After sudden misfortune, first apply for temporary assistance
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=no benefit=medium dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; apply to the township government or street office, explain the misfortune (fire, traffic accident, family member's sudden major illness, surge in essential living expenses); after review and public notice, approved by county-level civil affairs; materials per the local list, usually 1–2 trips.
- Say it in plain language: If your household suddenly hits trouble (fire, car crash, family member's sudden serious illness) causing temporary severe hardship in basic living, you can apply for temporary assistance at the township government or street office — no requirement that you already be on subsistence allowance. Usually a one-time payment; amounts vary greatly by region.
- Benefit: Households facing temporary severe hardship in basic living due to unexpected events or a family member's sudden major illness, and subsistence-assistance households whose necessary living expenses suddenly exceed tolerance, may receive temporary assistance; specific items and standards set and published by county-level-and-above local governments (national system, amounts set locally, from 2014).
- Evidence level: B
- Source: State Council (2014, revised 2019). Interim Measures for Social Assistance (State Council Order No. 649, Articles 47, 48, 49). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>
- Note: No requirement to already be on subsistence allowance; usually one-time payment; amounts vary greatly by region — follow current local announcements.

### 8. If household income is below the local subsistence line, apply for subsistence allowance
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; family members living together apply in writing to the township government or street office at the registered residence; those with application difficulty can entrust the village or neighborhood committee to apply on their behalf; subject to family income and property checks; usually 2–3 trips; ID, household registration booklet, and income/property proofs needed.
- Say it in plain language: Households whose per-capita income is below the local subsistence line with qualifying property can apply. At end-2024, the national urban subsistence average was 798.1 yuan/person/month and rural 593.9 yuan, covering 6.25 million and 33.615 million people respectively. Apply at the registered residence; owning a house, car, or savings, or having children with support ability, may disqualify. Subsistence status also links to subsidized medical insurance enrollment, medical assistance, and legal aid without financial review.
- Benefit: Households whose per-capita income is below the local subsistence standard and whose property meets requirements may receive protection; end-2024 national averages: urban 798.1 yuan/person-month, rural 593.9 yuan/person-month, covering 6.25 million and 33.615 million people respectively; standards determined by the previous year's local per-capita consumption expenditure times a quantitative ratio, adjusted as needed (national average; varies by region).
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2014, revised 2019). Interim Measures for Social Assistance (State Council Order No. 649, Articles 9, 10, 11). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>; National Bureau of Statistics (2025). - Note: Subsistence is the safety net; apply at the registered residence; excess household property (house, car, savings) or children with support ability may disqualify; subsistence status also links to subsidized medical insurance enrollment, medical assistance, and legal aid without financial review (see items 2 and 8).

### 9. With a disability certificate, apply for the two disability subsidies
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=medium dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; bring the certificate and submit one application at the street or township.
- Say it in plain language: Disabled people in subsistence households can receive the living subsidy; those rated grade 1 or 2 needing long-term care can receive the care subsidy; the two can be received simultaneously. Standards vary by province, from a few dozen to several hundred yuan per month. Bring the second-generation disability certificate, one trip to the street or township.
- Benefit: Two systems established by the State Council: difficulty disabled living subsidy "targets disabled people in subsistence households; qualified localities may gradually expand to low-income disabled people and other disabled people in difficulty"; severe disability nursing subsidy "targets severely disabled people rated grade 1 or 2 needing long-term care." The two can be enjoyed simultaneously.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2015). Opinions on Comprehensively Establishing the Systems of Living Subsidies for Disabled Persons in Difficulty and Nursing Subsidies for Severely Disabled Persons (Guofa [2015] No. 52). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2015-09/25/content_10181.htm>
- Note: Standards set by provincial governments, varying greatly by region, from a few dozen to several hundred yuan monthly. Application requires the second-generation disability certificate; family members may apply on behalf when the person can't go.

### 10. Don't let residents' medical insurance lapse at 400 yuan/year; hardship households get reductions

- Say it in plain language: Individuals pay from 400 yuan/year, with the government topping up about 700 yuan per person. The special hardship receive full funding and subsistence households fixed funding. The cost of lapsing is real: those not enrolled in the concentrated period or not continuously enrolled face a 3-month fixed waiting period after enrollment, plus roughly 1 month per additional year lapsed.

### 11. For serious illness, go through basic medical insurance, major-disease insurance, medical assistance, and cross-province filing first — stay away from online loans

### 13. After unemployment registration, pursue employment-difficult-person status for social security subsidies or public-benefit posts
