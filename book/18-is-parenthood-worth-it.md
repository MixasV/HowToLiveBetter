# 18. Is raising children a good deal?

Caliber: money and time.As in verse 10, here we only break down the account but do not give a conclusion.If you are pregnant and need to follow the procedures, see Section 27.

### 1. First count what you can receive: the national childcare subsidy is 3,600 yuan per child per year, and is paid until the age of 3
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; apply at the child’s place of residence
- Speaking in human terms: If a child is born after January 1, 2025 and is under 3 years old, one parent can apply at the place where the child has a registered permanent residence. Each child can receive 3,600 yuan per year, totaling 10,800 yuan for three years.This money does not need to be taxed, and it is not included in family income when determining subsistence allowances and other assistance.
- Income: Plan issued by the General Office of the CPC Central Committee and the General Office of the State Council: Infants and young children under 3 years old born on January 1, 2025 will be paid at a rate of "3,600 yuan per child per year" and applied by the infant's parent or other guardian; this subsidy is "exempt from personal income tax" and "will not be included in family or personal income" when determining assistance such as subsistence allowances.
- Level of evidence: A
- Source: General Office of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, General Office of the State Council (2025). Implementation Plan for the Child Care Subsidy System. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12206/202508/content_7035435.html>
- Note: This is the lowest level unified across the country. Some provinces and cities also have local subsidies, which can be superimposed.The total of 10,800 yuan in three years is only a small part of childcare expenses. Don’t use it as a basis for decision-making.

### 2. The maternity leave is 98 days, and the maternity allowance is paid by the maternity insurance fund based on the average monthly salary of the employees in the previous year.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; declared by the unit
- Speaking in human terms: After giving birth to a child, you can take at least 98 days of maternity leave. You can take 15 days before giving birth, and you can take an additional 15 days for difficult labor or for each additional child.The salary during this period is called maternity allowance, which is paid by the maternity insurance fund based on the average monthly salary of employees of the unit in the previous year. If the unit does not provide you with insurance, it will be paid by the unit based on your salary before maternity leave.Including the maternity incentive leave in each province, the actual number can usually be more than 158 days.
- Benefits: The State Council stipulates: "Female employees are entitled to 98 days of maternity leave after childbirth, including 15 days before childbirth; in the case of difficult labor, 15 days of additional maternity leave; in case of multiple births, 15 days of additional maternity leave for each additional baby." Maternity allowance during maternity leave, for those who have participated in maternity insurance, "will be paid by the maternity insurance fund based on the employer's average monthly salary of employees in the previous year"; for those who are not insured, the employer will pay according to the pre-maternity leave salary standard.
- Level of evidence: A
- Source: State Council (2012). Special Provisions on Labor Protection of Female Employees (National Order No. 619, Articles 7 and 8). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-05/07/content_6584.htm>
- Note: 98 days is the national minimum standard. The population and family planning regulations of various provinces generally add more than 60 days of maternity bonus leave, and the total is usually 158 days.Which party pays the salary during the incentive leave period has different regulations in each province.

### 3. Know this: No salary reduction or dismissal due to pregnancy, childbirth, or breastfeeding.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0
- Speaking in human terms: It is illegal to have your salary reduced or fired during pregnancy, childbirth, or breastfeeding, and your employer will be fined 1,000 to 5,000 yuan per person.If your child is under one year old, you are allowed one hour of breastfeeding time every day, and your employer cannot arrange for you to work overtime or night shifts.The written notice of job reassignment and salary reduction and chat records will be retained. Please refer to Section 19 for the claim path.
- Income: The State Council stipulates: "Employers shall not reduce wages, dismissal, or terminate labor or employment contracts of female employees due to pregnancy, childbirth, or breastfeeding." Those who breastfeed infants under 1 year old are allowed 1 hour of breastfeeding time every day, and are not allowed to arrange extended working hours and night shifts. Violators will be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan per person.
- Level of evidence: A
- Source: State Council (2012). Special Provisions on Labor Protection of Female Employees (National Order No. 619, Articles 5, 9, and 13). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-05/07/content_6584.htm>
- Note: If it is really illegally terminated, please see Section 19 for the claim path.The focus of evidence collection is the written notice of job transfer and salary reduction and chat records

### 4. Time accounts are calculated based on “whose time is taken up”, not based on “hard work or not”
<!-- Cost tag: money=0 time=many perseverance=yes profit=big caliber=time -->
- Cost: Basically occupied all day before the age of 3
- Speaking in human terms: Divide the number of hours you take care of your children every day and the number of times you are woken up at night into time, and then multiply it by the hourly salary of the person responsible for these things. The number you get is generally much greater than the cost of milk powder and diapers.Before the age of 3, it is basically occupied full time, so it is worth discussing the division of labor in front of the same table before the birth, rather than arguing after the birth.
- Income: Convert the daily care time and the number of night interruptions into hours, and then multiply it by the hourly salary of the responsible party. The resulting figure is usually much greater than the cost of milk powder and diapers.The same criteria for unpaid labor discussed in Section 10 apply. Discuss the division of labor first and then decide.
- Level of evidence: C
- Source: Author’s experience, no direct documentation; see Section 10 for caliber
- Note: The key is not to calculate an accurate number, but to let the two people discuss the division of labor in front of the same table before giving birth, rather than quarreling after the birth.

### 5. The money account is divided into three periods: 0 to 3 years old, compulsory education, and after compulsory education
<!-- Cost tags: Money=a lot, time=medium, perseverance=some, profit=large, caliber=money -->
- Cost: Varies greatly depending on city and choice
- Speaking in human terms: Money from 0 to 3 years old is mainly spent on how to raise children (bring it by yourself, provide it to the elderly, or hire someone to care for it). During the compulsory education stage, it is mainly spent on housing and extracurricular activities. After that, it is mainly spent on the path to further education. The three stages of spending money are completely different. Mixing them together will only add up to a scary total.First, include the money that is certain to come in such as childcare subsidies, maternity benefits, and medical insurance reimbursements, and then see how big the gap is.
- Income: The majority of income from 0 to 3 years old is the care method (self-care, elder care, hiring someone to care for you); the majority of the compulsory education stage is housing and extracurricular expenses; the majority after that is the path to further education.The cost structures of the three segments are completely different. If you mix them together, you will only get a scary total.
- Level of evidence: C
- Source: Author’s experience, no direct literature
- Note: First count certain inputs such as subsidies, maternity benefits, and medical insurance reimbursements, and then calculate the gap.Don’t make decisions based on “how many thousands does it cost to raise a child” circulated on the Internet? The caliber of such figures is unclear.

### 6. Keep an account each for your elders, for your marriage, and for your retirement.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; a serious conversation
- Speaking in human terms: Others want you to have a baby. This can be a consideration for you, but the time and money are charged to your own account."Raising children for old age" needs to be calculated separately, because it is a bet on another person's financial ability and willingness twenty years from now.Write down the consequences of not having children and compare both sides. Writing only one side is not settling accounts.
- Benefits: The same idea as Section 10: other people’s expectations can be a factor in your consideration, but the costs must be recorded in your own account."Raising children for old age" should be calculated separately because it assumes the financial ability and willingness of another person twenty years later.
- Level of evidence: C
- Source: Author’s experience, no direct literature; see Section 10 for similar analysis
- Note: Also write down the "consequences of not having children" and compare both sides.Just writing one side of the accounts is not called settling accounts
