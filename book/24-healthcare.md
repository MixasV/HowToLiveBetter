# 24. Medical treatment: How to spend less money and avoid detours

Caliber: money and time.This section talks about how to go to the doctor itself - where to go first, how to transfer, where to spend less money, how to line up in the emergency room, and what identification and certificates should be obtained after treatment of serious injuries.See Section 7 for what benefits you can receive, Section 16 for long-term management of chronic diseases, and Section 13 for what to do first at the emergency site.

This section is mainly based on the "Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of a Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment System" issued by the General Office of the State Council in April 2026.This document directly links "going to the grassroots" and "saving money", but the implementation progress varies from place to place. The main text contains national-level rules. For the specific coordination area where you are located, the caliber of the local medical insurance and health department shall prevail.

### 1. Common diseases are first treated in the community, and then referred step by step at the grassroots level, and then the hospitalization deductible is calculated.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the cost is to go to the community first and mentally accept "not directly registering an expert account"
- Say it in plain language: If you are transferred from the grassroots level to a higher-level hospital, the deductible you have to bear first every time you are hospitalized is only counted once; if you go directly to a tertiary hospital for hospitalization and then transfer back to community rehabilitation, you may have to pay it twice.For those who are transferred from higher-level hospitals back to grass-roots hospitals and continue to live there, no additional deductibles will be set for the same disease during this period.
- Benefit: For insured patients who are referred step by step by the grassroots hospital, the hospitalization deductible line in the higher-level hospital can be calculated continuously; for inpatients who are transferred back to the grassroots hospital from the higher-level hospital, there will no longer be another deductible in the same disease cycle.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several measures to accelerate the construction of a hierarchical diagnosis and treatment system.<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "For insured patients who are referred step by step by primary medical and health institutions in the coordinated area, the hospitalization deductibles of higher-level hospitals can be calculated continuously; for inpatients who are transferred from higher-level hospitals to primary medical and health institutions, there will no longer be another hospitalization deductible in the same disease cycle."
- Note: The deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket for each hospitalization.If you go directly to a tertiary hospital for hospitalization and then transfer back to the grassroots level for rehabilitation, you may have to pay twice; if you go to the referral hospital, it will only be counted once.Referral rules are formulated by each province, city and medical consortium. Please ask the community before going.

### 2. In the same coordinated area, the lower the level, the higher the reimbursement ratio, with a gap of about 10 percentage points.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- Say it in plain language: For the same hospitalization fee, the lower the hospital level, the more it will charge. The difference in each region is about ten percentage points, and the new medical insurance funds are also tilted toward the grassroots.If you go to a large hospital for things that can be done in the community, the difference in out-of-pocket expenses may be several thousand yuan.
- Benefit: The document requires various localities to increase the hospitalization reimbursement levels of different levels of medical institutions. In principle, the difference will be about 10 percentage points step by step; the annual new medical insurance fund will also be tilted towards the grassroots
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several measures to accelerate the construction of a hierarchical diagnosis and treatment system.<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "According to local conditions, the hospitalization reimbursement levels of insured persons at different levels of medical and health institutions should be appropriately increased. In principle, the gap in the hospitalization reimbursement ratios of medical and health institutions in the region should be gradually widened by about 10 percentage points." "Where conditions are met, the outpatient reimbursement levels of insured persons at different levels of medical and health institutions can be increased as appropriate according to the guidance of hierarchical diagnosis and treatment."
- Note: For the same hospitalization fee, the difference between out-of-pocket expenses at a primary hospital and a tertiary hospital may be several thousand yuan.This does not mean that all diseases should be treated in the community. It means that those who can be dealt with at the grassroots level should not go to higher levels.

### 3. If you want to go to a big hospital, go to the grassroots referral center or the hospital’s referral center. Don’t go to the traffickers.
<!-- Cost tag: money=0 time=medium perseverance=no income=medium caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one more referral procedure
- Say it in plain language: Large hospitals should set aside some numbers and beds for people transferred from the grassroots level, and set up referral centers or fixed departments to handle referrals, with full coverage by 2027.Not being able to get a registration does not mean there is no way; the registration from the registration dealer is not only expensive, but may also send you to the wrong department.
- Benefit: The leading hospital in a close medical consortium must reserve a certain proportion of numbers and beds for the grassroots, and promptly receive patients referred by the grassroots; medical institutions must set up referral centers or designated fixed departments to achieve full coverage by 2027
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several measures to accelerate the construction of a hierarchical diagnosis and treatment system.<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "Leading hospitals must reserve a certain proportion of numbers and beds for grassroots medical and health institutions, and promptly receive patients referred by grassroots hospitals." "Medical institutions must strengthen the unified management of referral services, set up referral centers or designate fixed departments to undertake patient referral services, and achieve full coverage by 2027."
- Note: The reserved number source is a channel for referred patients. Failure to get a number does not mean there is no way.In addition to being expensive, the path of the number dealer may land you in the wrong department.If you can't find a referral center, call the hospital's service hotline and ask.

### 4. Before seeking medical treatment across provinces, ask locally. In principle, the necessity must be evaluated by a deputy chief physician or above.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan, ask one more time
- Say it in plain language: Whether you need to go out of town for medical treatment is in principle evaluated by a doctor above the deputy chief physician in a second- or third-level hospital. The reimbursement level for temporary medical treatment abroad is lower than that in hospitals of the same level in insured areas.It is better to ask locally before departure than to find out that you cannot apply when you arrive in another place.
- Benefit: In principle, the necessity of medical treatment in different places across coordinating regions and provinces shall be assessed by deputy chief physicians and above from second- and third-level hospitals; the reimbursement level for those who temporarily go out for medical treatment across provinces should remain reasonably different from that of institutions at the same level in the insured area.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several measures to accelerate the construction of a hierarchical diagnosis and treatment system. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "In principle, deputy directors of second- and third-level hospitals
Chief physicians and above shall evaluate the necessity of patients seeking medical treatment across coordinating regions and provinces."Further strengthen the direct settlement management service for medical treatment in other places, and maintain a reasonable difference between the reimbursement levels of people who temporarily go out for medical treatment across provinces and the reimbursement levels of medical institutions of the same level in the insured areas."
- Note: "Temporarily going out for medical treatment" and "long-term living in another place" are two types of registration, and the reimbursement levels are different.Please refer to Section 16 for how to register and which types of diseases can be directly settled.Don’t think about reimbursement after traveling to other places.

### 5. Stop using tertiary hospitals as drug prescribing points, and return chronic diseases in the stable stage to the grassroots level
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; get rid of the habit of "only trusting big hospitals"
- Say it in plain language: Tertiary hospitals will provide emergency, critical and difficult diseases, common disease follow-up visits and general outpatient clinics for chronic diseases with stable conditions will be gradually reduced, and general outpatient clinics provided by expert teams will be extended to the community.If you treat big hospitals as drug prescribing points, you will be less and less able to get registered in the future.
- Benefit: Tertiary hospitals should focus on acute, critical and difficult and complex diseases, and gradually reduce the number of follow-up visits for common diseases and general outpatient clinics for chronic diseases with clear diagnoses and stable conditions; higher-level hospitals will extend the general outpatient clinics of expert teams to grassroots hospitals
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several measures to accelerate the construction of a hierarchical diagnosis and treatment system.<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "Tertiary hospitals should focus on critical, severe and difficult and complex diseases, strengthen referral consultation and inpatient services, and gradually reduce the number of follow-up consultations for common diseases.and general outpatient clinics for chronic diseases with clear diagnoses and stable conditions.""Higher-level hospitals within a close medical alliance should open outpatient clinics for common diseases and chronic diseases such as hypertension, diabetes, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease in primary medical and health institutions, provide diagnosis and treatment and health management services, and extend general outpatient clinics of expert teams to primary medical and health institutions."
- Note: This is a policy direction, not a suggestion: in the future, it will be increasingly difficult to get admitted to general outpatient clinics in large hospitals.For stable chronic diseases, medicines can be prescribed for 12 weeks at a time at the primary level, see Section 16.

### 6. Every time you see a doctor, keep a copy of your medical records, examination reports and images.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hospitals can charge a cost-based fee for photocopying in accordance with regulations, and the charging standards should be made public
- Say it in plain language: It is the right given by law to review and copy one's own medical records, which include outpatient medical records, hospitalization records, laboratory test orders, imaging, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology data and expense lists; the hospital has a special window to accept it, and can only charge a cost-based fee and the standards must be made public.Changing hospitals, seeking second medical opinions, applying for commercial insurance, and actually having disputes all rely on this pile of materials.
- Benefit: Regulations clearly list access and copying of medical records as patients' rights, and the scope covers all information such as outpatient medical records, hospitalization records, laboratory tests, medical images, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology data, medical expenses, etc. These are all relied upon when changing hospitals, seeking second opinions on diagnosis and treatment, applying for commercial insurance, and in case of real disputes.
- Evidence level: A

"If a patient requests to copy medical record information, the medical institution shall provide copying services and stamp the copied medical record information with a certification mark... - Note: There is a special window for photocopying, so there is no need to go to the doctor.The list to be copied should be read directly according to item 16.Save a folder in chronological order, discharge summary, surgical records, pathology reports, and key images are the most important.Previous indicators of chronic diseases are marked in the same book, see Section 16.

### 7. If you have any questions about diagnosis and treatment, ask to seal the medical records on the spot. Both parties should be present, make a list, and each keep a copy.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; submit a sealing request to the medical institution and wait until both parties are present to complete the procedure.
- Say it in plain language: If you have any questions about the diagnosis and treatment, you can ask to seal the medical records: both parties are present, make a list, sign and seal, each keeps one copy, and seal the completed part first if it is not finished.If it is suspected that there is a problem with infusion, blood transfusion, injection, or medication, the bag of liquid, the medicine, and the infusion set must be sealed together.Once it is closed, no one can write or change it.
- Benefit: The regulations stipulate that "when a medical dispute occurs and medical records need to be sealed or unsealed, they should be done in the presence of both doctors and patients." The seal can be the original or a copy, which is kept by the medical institution. If the medical record has not been completed and needs to be sealed, the completed part must be sealed first, and then the completed part can be sealed. The medical institution should prepare a seal list for the sealed medical records, and both doctors and patients should sign or seal, each holding one copy.If adverse consequences are suspected of being caused by infusion, blood transfusion, injection, medication, etc., both doctors and patients should jointly seal and unseal the physical objects on site. If inspection is required, both parties should jointly entrust an institution with inspection qualifications. If both parties cannot jointly entrust, the health authority of the county-level people's government where the medical institution is located shall designate; if blood transfusion is suspected of causing adverse consequences and the blood needs to be sealed, the medical institution should notify the blood station to send personnel to the scene.There is another hard restriction: "No unit or individual may tamper with, forge, conceal, destroy or seize medical records." If the dispute has been resolved after sealing, or if no request for resolution is made for three years, the medical institution can unseal it on its own (national, effective from October 1, 2018)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2018). Regulations on the Prevention and Resolution of Medical Disputes (State Council Order No. 701, Articles 15, 24, and 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhen
gce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Note: Sealing and copying are two different things: copying is something that should be done at ordinary times (see Article 6), sealing is to fix the evidence when a dispute occurs, and to prevent subsequent writing and modification.If there is a suspected problem with infusion, blood transfusion, or medication, in addition to the medical records, the bag of fluid, the medicine, and the infusion set should also be sealed together, and do not let others take them away and throw them away.Medical institutions should publish the methods, procedures and contact information for resolving medical disputes in a conspicuous place, and set up a unified complaint management department. First, go to that department instead of the doctor on duty.

### 8. If you have an acute injury or illness, go straight to the emergency pre-examination triage table. Do not queue at the registration window.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=medium Caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; walk a few steps after entering the door to find the triage desk, and explain the most terrible symptom in one sentence
- Say it in plain language: Emergency services are not on a first-come, first-served basis, but are divided into four levels according to the condition.Those with Level 1 critical conditions will be admitted to the resuscitation room or emergency room immediately, those with Level 2 emergencies will be treated within 10 minutes, those with Level 3 emergencies will be re-evaluated after waiting more than 30 minutes before Level 4, and those with Level 4 non-emergency conditions will be re-evaluated after waiting for more than 4 hours.The place for grading is the pre-examination triage table at the emergency entrance, not the registration window - if you don't go to triage, you have no grade in the system, and no matter how badly injured you are, you can only move slowly with the team.Let’s report the most dangerous ones first: heavy bleeding, out of breath, unconsciousness, something stuck in the body, and chest pain.On the other hand, if you just prescribe medicine, remove sutures, and treat old problems, you will be classified as level 4, and you will only have to wait longer to go to the emergency room.
- Benefit: The 2024 version of the emergency medicine quality control indicators of the National Health Commission stipulates that emergency pre-examination and triage "patients are divided into four levels according to the severity of their condition: Level I is acute and critical patients, requiring immediate treatment; Level II is acute and severe patients, often assessment and treatment are carried out at the same time; Level III is emergency patients, requiring diagnosis and treatment in a short time; Level IV is non-emergency patients or sub-acute patients, who are treated sequentially in the general diagnosis and treatment area."Beijing's implementation standards provide response requirements at all levels: Level 1 "immediately assess and treat patients, arrange for the patient to enter the resuscitation room or rescue room"; Level 2 "immediately monitor vital signs, receive treatment within 10 minutes, and arrange for the patient to enter the emergency room"; Level 3 "prioritize diagnosis and treatment before Level 4 patients, and arrange for patients to wait in the general diagnosis and treatment area; if the waiting time is more than 30 minutes, re-evaluation is required"; Level 4 "sequential treatment, unless the condition changes, the waiting time will be longer; if the waiting time is greater than4 hours and can be re-evaluated"; triage should "adhere to the principle of priority for emergency and severe cases", and a pre-examination triage area should be set up at a prominent location at the entrance of the emergency department (the fourth-level standard is the national standard, and the response time is the Beijing standard)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the National Health Commission (2024). Notice on the issuance of six professional medical quality control indicators (2024 version) including emergency medicine (National Health Office Medical Affairs Letter [2024] No. 150) Attachment 1, indicator four "Emergency grade and triage implementation rate".<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>; Beijing Municipal Health Commission (2019).Beijing's work plan for strengthening emergency pre-examination and triage classification and the attachment "Beijing Hospital Emergency Pre-examination and Triage Grading Standards (Trial)": "Adhere to the principle of priority for urgent and critical patients. According to the severity of the patient's condition, follow the principle of from severe to light, and from rapid changes to relatively stable conditions, reasonably arrange the order of patients." "Set up an emergency pre-examination and triage area at a prominent position at the entrance of the emergency department."<http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Note: The four-level triage is a national standard, and the response time at each level is determined by each province and city. The Beijing version is a version that can be verified word for word.Grading looks at vital signs and symptoms, not how much pain you feel: hard indicators for grade 1 include heart rate greater than 180 beats/min or less than 40 beats/min, systolic blood pressure less than 70 mmHg, blood oxygen less than 80%, shock, definite myocardial infarction, and acute disturbance of consciousness.If you feel that the triage is low, add the most dangerous symptom; if your condition changes while waiting for treatment, you should immediately return to the triage desk for re-evaluation.When the patient is really critical, it is faster to call 120 than to go there yourself. The pre-hospital doctor will initially grade the patient according to the same set of standards and notify the hospital in advance to prepare for treatment (see Section 13).

### 9. If you don’t bring any money, no ID, and you can’t tell who you are, you must first seek emergency treatment.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- Say it in plain language: The state has a disease emergency relief fund, which is dedicated to paying first-aid expenses for two types of people: those whose identities cannot be found, and those whose identities are clear but cannot afford to pay.Medical institutions are not allowed to refuse, shirk or delay treatment for any reason, and emergency centers are not allowed to refuse or delay transportation due to cost issues.So don’t just carry it at home because you don’t have any money, and don’t be afraid to call an ambulance because you’re afraid of arrears.This money is only for the first aid period, and subsequent hospitalization will still be covered by medical insurance and medical assistance.
- Benefit: The target of the disease emergency assistance system is "patients who have suffered acute, severe or critical injuries in China and need first aid but whose identities are unclear or unable to pay the corresponding fees." The fund pays "the first-aid expenses incurred by patients whose identities cannot be identified" and "the first-aid expenses owed by patients whose identities are clear but unable to pay"; "Medical institutions at all levels and their staff must provide timely and effective rescue to patients with acute, severe and critical injuries, and must not refuse, shirk or delay treatment for any reason."Pre-hospital section: The national pre-hospital medical emergency call number is "120". Emergency centers (stations) "may not refuse, shirk or delay pre-hospital medical emergency services due to command and dispatch reasons" and "may not refuse or delay pre-hospital medical emergency services due to cost issues". Transfers follow the "principles of being nearby, urgent, meeting professional needs and taking into account the patient's wishes" (National, starting from 2013)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2013). Guiding Opinions on Establishing a Disease Emergency Relief System (Guobanfa [2013] No. 15). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission (2014). Pre-hospital Medical Emergency Management Measures (Order No. 3No. 13, 22, 23, and 25). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Note: The fund only pays for the "first aid expenses", and treatment is done first, and the medical institution applies to the fund afterwards, instead of you going to the window to collect money. ### 10. The disability assessment must be done after the treatment is completed. If done too early, the grade will be lowered.
<!-- Cost tags: Money=little, time=medium, perseverance=some, profit=big, caliber=money -->
- Cost: The appraisal fee ranges from a few hundred to two thousand yuan, and is usually paid by the applicant first; it must wait until the treatment is completed or the clinical effect is stable, usually three to six months or even longer after the injury.
- Say it in plain language: The amount of compensation depends mainly on the level of the treatment, and the level of the treatment depends on how much dysfunction is left after treatment.The standards state that it should be done after the treatment is completed or the clinical treatment effect is stable. If the bone is not grown well and the steel plate is not removed, then you go to evaluate it as it was at that time, which is equivalent to lowering the grade.The tenth level corresponds to a disability rate of 10%, and the difference is 10% for each level above. The first level is 100%.If a fracture wants to be rated ten levels, it must fall on specific conditions, such as a vertebral body compressed by one-third, two or more fractures in the pelvis with malunion, and a loss of more than 25% of the function of a large joint. Ordinary fractures that do not affect function after recovery cannot be rated.
- Benefit: The "Classification of Human Body Injury and Disability Degrees" stipulates that the timing of identification "should be carried out after the treatment of the primary injury and its related complications has been completed or the clinical treatment effect has stabilized"; "This standard divides the degree of human injury and disability into 10 levels, from Level 1 (Human Body Disability Rate 100%) to Level 10 (Human Body Disability Rate 10%). The disability rate of each level is different.10%"; the judgment is based on "structural damage and dysfunction of human tissues and organs and their dependence on medical treatment and nursing, with due consideration of social interactions and psychological factors caused by disability"; the terms related to fractures in Level 10 include "compression fracture of one vertebral body (compression degree up to 1/3) or comminuted fracture", "fracture of more than two places in the pelvis or comminuted fracture, malunion", "25% loss of function of any major joint of the limbs (except ankle joint)""Above"; Disabilities of the same part and nature shall not be counted twice, and the level of disability in two or more places shall be stated separately (National, effective from January 1, 2017)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of National Security, Ministry of Justice (2016). Announcement on the release of "Grading of Human Injury and Disability Degrees" (released on April 18, 2016, effective on January 1, 2017), Standard Articles 4.2, 4.4, 4.5, 5.10.6, 6.2.<https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Note: The three sets of disability assessments are not universal, so don’t confuse them: injuries from injuries, traffic accidents and other personal injuries are subject to the judicial appraisal of the "Human Injury Disability Grading", which is used to calculate disability compensation (see Section 9); work-related injuries are subject to the labor ability assessment, which uses another set of ten disability levels, which governs work-related injury benefits (see Section 19); if you want to enjoy the policy benefits of disabled people, you must apply for a separate disability certificate (see Section 11).Before identification, medical records, surgical records, and reexamination images must be prepared (see Article 6). Lack of images is the most common reason for lowering the grade.If the injury coexists with the original injury or illness, the appraisal opinion must also indicate how much role the injury plays in the consequences of disability. ### 11. If functional impairment remains after treatment, go to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation in your place of residence to apply for a disability certificate
<!-- Cost tag: Money=little, time=medium, perseverance=some, income=medium, caliber=money -->
- Cost: There is no production fee for the certificate itself. In principle, the assessment fee and photo fee are borne by yourself. If you are in difficulty, you can apply for exemption; bring your ID card, household register and 3 two-inch white background photos to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation at the place of residence. After passing the assessment, it will be publicized in the village (community) for 5 working days, and the Disabled Persons' Federation will complete the review within 10 working days.
- Say it in plain language: The Disability Certificate is the threshold for receiving disability benefits, not proof of injury.It is divided into seven categories: vision, hearing, speech, physical, intelligence, mental and multiple categories. Each category has levels one to four, and the assessment is based on national standards.The process is application, acceptance, evaluation by designated agencies, publicity, review and approval, and issuance of certificates. It is valid for ten years and will be replaced free of charge upon expiration.If you can't rate it, you can't rate it. This isn't something you can do with acquaintances.Later, if the injury condition changes, you can apply for re-evaluation; if you have any objection to the conclusion, you can apply for re-evaluation at the municipal Disabled Persons' Federation within ten working days.
- Benefit: The Disability Certificate "is a legal certificate for identifying disabled people and their disability categories and levels. It is an important basis for disabled people to enjoy the preferential policies of the state and local governments in accordance with the law." The evaluation standard is the national standard "Disability Classification and Grading of Disabled Persons" (GB/T26341-2010); Adhere to voluntary and territorial management of applications, and seven types of disabled people with vision, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental and multiple disabilities can apply, with disability levels ranging from one to four; the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation is responsible for application acceptance and issuance, based on the assessment conclusion made by a provincial-level designated hospital or professional institution; if the assessment conclusion meets the standards, it will be publicized in the village (community) for five working days, and the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation will "complete the review within ten working days"; "No cost will be charged for applying for a disability certificate.”, the assessment and photo fees “are in principle borne by the applicant personally; local finance can provide subsidies where conditions permit, and applicants with special difficulties should coordinate with relevant departments to reduce or reduce the cost”; it is valid for ten years and will be replaced free of charge upon expiration; those with changes in disability category or level can apply for reassessment; those who have objections to the assessment conclusion can apply for reassessment at the prefecture-level or municipal Disabled Persons’ Federation within ten working days. If there are still objections, the provincial-level Disability Assessment Expert Committee will make the final conclusion (National Disability Assessment Expert Committee, 2018Effective from January 1, 2019)
