# 10. Is it worthwhile to fall in love and get married?

This section breaks down "whether marriage is worth it" into several accounts that can be calculated separately: money, time, health, legal risks and relationship quality. Each account only gives numbers that can be found in official statistics or meta-analysis, without making moral judgments or drawing conclusions for readers. Health figures are based on death risk, time figures are based on minutes per day, and legal figures are based on articles. The three are not converted to each other.For methods of splitting accounts, a fill-in-the-blank list and common misunderstandings, see [docs/Is Getting Married a Cost-Effective.md](../docs/Is Getting Married a Cost-Effective.md).

### 1. Get to know more people instead of fighting against one person: Whether two people will get along or not cannot be predicted before meeting.
<!-- Cost tag: money=0 time=medium perseverance=some income=medium caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; spend your time expanding your knowledge rather than thinking about the same person over and over again
- Say it in plain language: Two speed dating studies asked people to fill out more than 100 mate selection questionnaires before meeting. The results could only calculate "this person is generally easy to like others" and "this person is generally popular", but could not calculate "whether there are calls between these two specific people." Even the person's own ideal type (whether he values appearance or economic prospects) could not predict his interest in a certain person on the spot.You won't know whether you're compatible until you meet in person, so it's more cost-effective to spend your time getting to know a few more people than betting on one person.
- Benefit: In two speed dating studies, participants filled out more than 100 self-report scales related to mate selection in advance. The random forest model can predict "how easy a person is to like others as a whole" (variance 4%-18%) and "how popular a person is overall" (7%-27%). However, "whether there are calls between these two specific people" cannot be predicted using any combination of traits and preferences before the meeting.An earlier speed-dating experiment by the same researchers also found that participants' self-reported ideal preferences before the meeting (appearance or financial prospects) did not predict their interest in a specific person in the meeting.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2017). Is romantic desire predictable? Machine learning applied to initial romantic attraction. Psychological Science, 28(10), 1478-1489.do people know what they initially desire in a romantic partner? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 94(2), 245-264. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.94.2.245>
- Note: These two studies say "you can't figure it out before you meet", not "no matter how hard you try, it's useless."Since you can only know if you are compatible or not after meeting, it is more cost-effective to spend the cost to get to know a few more people and actually meet each one, rather than investing all your time on one person.They are all samples of European and American college students. China’s marriage and love market has a different structure. It is concluded that migration should be discounted.

### 2. Stop after the other party clearly refuses. Continuing to pester is a security violation and is not "sincerity"
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=Yes Income=medium Caliber=Free -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; refrain from sending messages or waiting for anyone
- Say it in plain language: If the other party still sends a message and goes to wait for someone after he clearly refuses, it will be a public security violation from January 2026: Detention for not more than 5 days or a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan, and if the case is serious, detention for 5 to 10 days.The police can also order you to prohibit contact with the other party for a period of time, and detain you for an additional 5 to 10 days if you violate the prohibition of contact.Such records will also affect the qualifications for public examinations, editorial examinations, political examinations and some occupations.
- Benefit: January 1, 2026The Public Security Management Punishment Law, which came into effect today, singles out pestering and stalking: "Those who repeatedly send obscene, insulting, threatening and other information or use methods such as nuisance, pestering, stalking, etc. to interfere with the normal life of others" shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan. In more serious cases,Detention for not less than five days but not more than ten days may be accompanied by a fine of not more than one thousand yuan; and there is one more layer to this - with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ, the offender can be ordered to prohibit contact with the victim within a certain period of time. Anyone who violates the prohibition of contact shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2025 revision). Public Security Management Punishment Law (Article 50, Paragraph 1, Item 5, Paragraph 2). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml> (Reprinted by the Supreme People's Procuratorate)
- Note: There is no research to support "Whether chasing someone to the end will move the other person", but the detention and no-contact orders are written in black and white.The records left behind will also affect the qualifications for public examinations, editorial examinations, political examinations and some professions.The dividing line between chasing someone and harassing someone is not determined by your motives, but by whether the other person has clearly stated their refusal.

### 3. To determine whether the other party is interested, look at behavior rather than "signals": who takes the initiative, who arranges, and who follows up
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; shift your focus from interpreting expressions and tone to recording facts
- Say it in plain language: Let 52 pairs of strangers chat for ten minutes and then judge each other whether they are flirting. The result is that "the other person is not flirting" is more accurate, but "the other person is flirting" cannot be seen. When 261 people watched a one-minute video to judge, the conclusion was the same.So don’t infer your thoughts based on your facial expressions and tone of voice, look at the observable behaviors: who initiates the initiative, who arranges the next time, and whether the other party follows up when you don’t push forward.
- Benefit: Two studies measured people's accuracy in identifying flirting: 52 pairs of people of the opposite sex who did not know each other talked for 10 to 12 minutes and each reported whether they were flirting and whether they thought the other party was flirting. The result was that "interactions where no flirting occurred were judged more accurately, and interactions where flirting occurred were judged worse." The second study used 26 one-minute videos to give 261Judgments by onlookers have reached the same conclusion - people can more reliably see that "the other party is not flirting", but it is difficult to see that "the other party is flirting"
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Hall JA, Xing C, Brooks S (2015). Accurately detecting flirting: error management theory, the traditional sexual script, and flirting base rate. Communication Research, 42(7), 939-958. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0093650214534972>
- Note: Controversy.Other studies believe that men do not overestimate women's sexual interest, but women underreport it when reporting themselves. It is still inconclusive whether the judgment of the two parties is more accurate (Perilloux C, Kurzban R (2015). Do men overperceive women's sexual interest? Psychological Science, 26(1), 70-77. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614555727>).But both groups agree on one thing: the accuracy of inferring the other person's thoughts from subtle clues such as facial expressions and tone of voice is very low.Observable behavior is much more reliable—whether you initiated it, whether you scheduled it for another time, whether you followed through even if you didn’t push forward.

### 4. Whether a relationship is good or not mainly depends on your own feelings in the relationship, not on the conditions of the other party.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; replace the mate selection list with an observation of the process of getting along
- Say it in plain language: In an analysis of 29 laboratories and 43 paired tracking data, it was found that the best indicator of whether a relationship is good or not is the person's own feelings - feeling the other person's commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, feeling satisfied with the other person, and whether there is much conflict, not the other person's conditions; the predictive power of these things reported by oneself is two to four times that of the partner's report.But the same study also found that whether the relationship got better or worse in the future is basically impossible to calculate using any self-report questionnaire.
- Benefit: Machine learning analysis of 29 laboratories and 43 longitudinal paired data: The variables with the strongest predictions of relationship quality all come from the parties' feelings about the relationship - feeling the other party's commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, feeling the other party is satisfied, and conflict; the strongest ones at the individual level are life satisfaction, negative emotions, depression, avoidant attachment, and anxious attachment.Relationship variables explained up to 45% of the variance at baseline and up to 18% at the end of the study.Moreover, the predictive power of "self-reported variables" is two to four times that of "partner-reported variables". After adding individual differences and partner reports, there is no additional predictive power compared to using only self-reported relationship variables.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Allison CJ, et al. (2020). Machine learning uncovers the most robust self-report predictors of relationship quality across 43 longitudinal couples studies. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 117(32), 19061-19071. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1917036117>
- Note: The same study also found that "changes" in relationship quality (whether it got better or worse during the study period) were largely unpredicted by any combination of self-reported variables.So the useful part of this article is: to judge whether the current relationship is good or not, asking yourself how you feel is more accurate than checking the other person's condition list; but don't expect to predict what it will be like in the future.It is the same thing as the article in this section "Don't just ask whether there is emotional value." This article gives the specific items.

### 5. If you want to get closer, take turns answering a set of questions from simple to difficult, don’t learn to talk.
<!-- cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=small Caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; 45 minutes
- Say it in plain language: Pair up two strangers and take turns answering three sets of 36 questions from easy to deep. After 45 minutes, the feeling of closeness to each other is significantly higher than that of a control group who chatted for the same length of time. It is even higher than the average rating of that group of students on their closest relationship in reality.What works is that both parties take turns and tell the truth layer by layer, not words.It only measures closeness in a short period of time and does not mean that it can develop into a long-term relationship.
- Benefit: Pair up two people who do not know each other and let them take turns answering three sets of progressively in-depth questions (a total of 36 questions, ranging from "Who do you most want to treat to dinner" to "When was the last time you cried in front of others"). After 45 minutes, the sense of closeness was significantly higher than that of the control group who chatted for the same length of time. It was also higher than the average rating of college students in the study on their most intimate relationship in reality.
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Aron A, Melinat E, Aron EN, Vallone RD, Bator RJ (1997). The experimental generation of interpersonal closeness: a procedure and some preliminary findings. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 23(4), 363-377. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003>
- Note: The single experiment and the sample were college students. The test was about the sense of closeness in a short period of time, not whether it can develop into a long-term relationship.The usable part is its mechanism: intimacy comes from escalating two-way self-disclosure, not words, routines, or one-sided performances.Therefore, the effective action when chatting is "I will say something true, and I will also ask something of equal depth", and both parties will take turns.

### 6. Living in a different place is no worse than living in the same city. The real hurdle is the first three months after moving together.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = a lot, time = a lot, perseverance = yes, income = medium, caliber = time -->
- Cost: travel expenses and time; meeting frequency is low
- Say it in plain language: Being in a different place is more stable during the separation period because we see less and have a higher imagination; the real hurdle comes after moving together.According to the tracking data, about half of the long-distance couples ended up in the same city, and the other half ended during the separation period; and of the half of the reunited couples, one-third broke up within 3 months.The longer the distance between each other and the more beautiful the thoughts when they are separated, the more unstable they will be after reunion.What you need to prepare for is the months after the reunion: less room for autonomy, more understanding, and more conflict and jealousy.
- Benefit: The two studies give a consistent picture: long-distance relationships are more stable than same-city relationships during separation, because the degree of idealization (idealization distortion, romantic love, relationship memories, perceived consistency) in long-distance relationships is significantly higher, and idealization is related to low meeting frequency; but this stability only exists when the two parties are still separated. Once they become in the same city, long-distance couples are more likely to break up.The study that tracked long-distance-to-city transfers found that about half of long-distance couples made it to the point of moving in together, and the other half ended it during their separation; and of the half who reunited, a third broke up within three months of the reunion.The longer the interval between meetings during separation and the stronger the idealization, the more unstable the reunion will be.
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Stafford L, Merolla AJ, Castle JD (2006). When long-distance dating partners become geographically close. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 23(6), 901-919.and stability in long-distance dating relationships. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 24(1), 37-54. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407507072578>
- Note: The sample is the romantic relationships of American college students. It does not include marriage and is of an earlier age. Don’t apply the absolute proportion directly.Another study of 717 people in different places and 425 people in the same city found that there was little difference in relationship quality between the two types of relationships, and that it was personal and relationship characteristics rather than distance itself that predicted relationship quality (Dargie E, et al. (2015). Journal of Sex & Marital Therapy, 41(2), 181-202.<https://doi.org/10.1080/0092623X.2013.864367>).Available conclusions: Do you want to live in a different place? Don't use "the distance will definitely break up" as a reason; what you need to prepare in advance is the few months after the reunion - there will be less independent space, more understanding of each other, and more conflicts and jealousy.

### 7. Look at the registration data first and then listen to the elders: What are the marriage and divorce rates?
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=small Caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; read the marriage registration paragraph in the annual bulletin of the Ministry of Civil Affairs, a few minutes
- Say it in plain language: In 2024, 6.106 million pairs of marriages will be registered nationwide, 20% less than the previous year, and 3.513 million pairs of divorces will be processed.The "divorce rate of more than half" uploaded online is based on dividing these two numbers (about 57.5%). The numerator and denominator are not the same group of people at all. This indicator is not in the communiqué and cannot be read as "half of marriages will divorce."The marriage rate in the communiqué is 4.3‰ and the divorce rate is 2.5‰. The denominator is the entire population rather than the married population.
- Benefit: In 2024, 6.106 million pairs of marriages were registered in accordance with the law nationwide, a decrease of 20.5% from the previous year, and the marriage rate was 4.3‰; 3.513 million pairs of divorce procedures were processed in accordance with the law (2.622 million couples were registered for divorce by the civil affairs department, and 891,000 couples were divorced by court judgment and mediation), and the divorce rate was 2.5‰; the communiqué stated that the marriage (divorce) rate =The number of marriages (divorces) in that year / the average total population in that year × 1000‰, which is a "rough rate", and the denominator is the entire population rather than the married population

### 8. Include health benefits, but discount observational data
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=No Benefit=small Caliber=mortality -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; know that these numbers are related to "married people have lower mortality rates", not the cause and effect of "getting married will make you live longer"
- Say it in plain language: Married people are about 12% less likely to die in the same period than unmarried people, and never-married people are about 24% more likely to die than married people. The older you get, the smaller the gap, and men are more obviously affected.But this is correlation, not causation: people who are healthy, have high incomes, and socialize a lot are more likely to get married and live longer. The more rigorous the research, the smaller the gap will be. Moreover, the figures are basically from European and American samples and can only be used as a reference for domestic purposes.
- Benefit: Meta-analysis of the elderly (53 comparison groups, more than 250,000 people), the RR of all-cause death for married versus non-married people was 0.88 (95% CI 0.85–0.91), 1.11 for widowed, 1.16 for divorced or separated, and 1.11 for single. There was no difference between men and women, and the most conservative estimate was RR 0.94; meta-analysis of single (never married) versus married (95 articles, 5More than 100 million people) HR 1.24 (1.19–1.30), and the effect becomes smaller with age; in a meta-analysis split by gender (21 studies, more than 7.89 million people), non-marriage is associated with all-cause, cancer, and cardiovascular death in both genders. The association with cardiovascular and all-cause death is stronger in men. Unmarried men have 31% more stroke deaths and 9% more all-cause deaths than unmarried women.
- Evidence level: A (disputed)
- Source: Manzoli L, et al. (2007). Marital status and mortality in the elderly: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Soc Sci Med 64:77–94. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2006.08.031>; Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011).meta-regression. Am J Epidemiol 174(4):379–389. <https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwr111>; Wang Y, et al. (2020). Sex differences in the association between marital status and the risk of cardiovascular, cancer, and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 7,881,040 individuals. Glob Health Res Policy 5:4.<https://doi.org/10.1186/s41256-020-00133-8>
- Note: The controversy lies in the selection effect: people who are healthy, have high incomes, and socialize more are inherently
It is easier to get married and live longer. The three meta-analyses are all observational studies. The authors themselves also stated that there is publication bias or method limitations. Roelfs also found that the higher the quality of the study, the smaller the effect. These numbers come from mainly European and American samples and can only be used as a reference for the domestic population.

### 9. The time account will be calculated as "unpaid labor". Discuss the division of labor before receiving the certificate.

- Cost: 0 yuan; spend an hour or two before marriage to negotiate a clear division of unpaid labor such as housework, care, and whose work will be given, and then implement it according to the division of labor
- Say it in plain language: In the National Time Use Survey, men spend an average of 1 hour and 52 minutes a day on unpaid tasks such as housework and taking care of family members, while women spend 3 hours and 29 minutes, a difference of one and a half hours; the participation rate is also 16 percentage points different.The survey did not group people by marital status, so there is no official figure on "How much more housework will there be after getting married?" This gender gap can only be used as an upper limit reference.The division of labor can be completely negotiated before receiving the certificate.
- Benefit: According to the 2024 National Time Utilization Survey, participants spent an average of 2 hours and 45 minutes on unpaid work (housework, accompanying and caring for family members, etc.) per day, with men doing 1 hour and 52 minutes, and women doing 3 hours and 29 minutes. ### 10. When it comes to money accounts, first check the legal default rules before deciding whether to agree in writing.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=Medium Perseverance=Some Benefit=Large Caliber=Money -->
- Cost: Starting from 0 yuan; read through the property provisions of the Marriage and Family Section of the Civil Code, and sign a written property agreement between both parties if necessary (the law only requires written form, notarization is not a necessary condition)
- Say it in plain language: Without a written agreement, the law assumes that wages and bonuses earned after marriage, business and investment income, intellectual property income, inherited or donated property shall belong to both parties jointly; pre-marital property, personal injury compensation, wills or gift contracts indicate that only one party, and daily necessities for the exclusive use of one party are personal.The two parties can agree in writing that each party shall own the property individually or part of the property shall be jointly owned. Once signed, it will be binding. Notarization is not a necessary condition.The party who has contributed more to raising children and caring for the elderly can claim compensation during divorce.
- Benefit:The default rule is that wages, bonuses, labor remuneration, production and operation investment income, intellectual property income, inherited or donated property obtained after marriage are jointly owned by the husband and wife (Article 1062); pre-marital property, personal injury compensation, property that is clearly owned by one party in a will or gift contract, and daily necessities exclusively owned by one party are personal property (Article 1000)Article 63); Both parties may agree in writing that the property before and after marriage shall be owned individually, jointly or partly jointly, and the agreement shall be legally binding on both parties (Article 1065); the party who has more obligations due to raising children, caring for the elderly, or assisting the other party in work shall have the right to request compensation in the event of divorce (Article 1088)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (effective from January 1, 2021, Articles 1062, 1063, 1065, 1088). National Laws and Regulations Database<https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?title=%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B0%91%E6%B3%95%E5%85%B8&id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>; The full text of the article can be found in the Gazette of the Supreme People's Court.<http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>
- Note: There are no official statistics on betrothal gifts, weddings, and wedding rooms, so no figures are included in this article; the amount of compensation in Article 1088 is agreed upon by both parties, and if the agreement fails, it is determined by the court. There is no legal formula; property agreements are two-way, and are useful for both the party with higher income and the party with more unpaid labor.

### 11. When your parents contribute money to buy a house, when transferring money, clearly indicate whether it is a loan or a gift.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; write a one-page explanation on the day of investment, indicating whether the money is donated or lent to whom, and signed by the parents and children who contributed the money (when writing a loan, ask both husband and wife to sign), and the transfer remarks are in the same sentence
- Say it in plain language: If parents contribute money to buy a house before marriage, it will be regarded as belonging to their children by default; if there is no agreement on the money after marriage, it will be treated as joint property.Starting from February 2025, if one parent in the marriage fully contributes the capital and the gift contract does not specify that it is only for his or her children, the court can award the house to the investor's children during divorce. It will then decide whether to compensate the other party based on the living and childbearing situation, whether there are any faults, who has contributed more to the family, and how much the house is now worth.It must be written clearly and signed by both parties on the same day according to the loan application.
- Benefit: If parents contribute capital to purchase a house for both parties before marriage, it will be deemed as a personal gift to their children, unless the parents expressly express the gift to both parties; if the capital is contributed after marriage, if there is an agreement, it shall be treated as agreed. If there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, it shall be handled according to the principle of community property of the husband and wife.February 1, 2025Starting from the date of marriage, if one parent fully contributes the capital during the marriage, but the gift contract does not specify that it will only be given to his or her children, the court may rule that the house belongs to one of the investor's children during divorce and division. The court will then determine whether or how much compensation should be given to the other party based on the circumstances of living together and having common children, the fault of the divorce, the size of the contribution to the family, and the market price of the house at the time of the divorce. If one parent contributes part of the capital or both parents contribute, the court may rule that the house belongs to one of the children of the investor based on the source and proportion of the capital contribution and make reasonable compensation (nationwide)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Supreme People's Court (2020). Interpretation on the Application of the Marriage and Family Section of the Civil Code (I) (Fa Interpretation [2020] No. 22, Article 29). <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>; Supreme People's Court (2025).Interpretation on the Application of the Marriage and Family Section of the Civil Code (II) (Fa Interpretation [2025] No. 1, Article 8). <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452771.html>; National People’s Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 1062, Paragraph 1, Item 4).<https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: This is useful for both sides.Parents who contribute money no longer need to rely on filling out an IOU in the event of divorce to keep the investment - after explanation (2) is implemented, the source of the investment itself will be the basis for division, and the name under whose name it is registered will no longer determine the ownership; the party receiving the investment does not need to wait until divorce to know what the money counts.If you want to follow the rules of a loan, you should really follow the rules of a loan: the loan agreement, the repayment agreement, the transfer notes, and the repayment record when necessary. Any missing items must be explained in court.

### 12. If one spouse borrows a large amount of money and you do not sign or ratify it, it does not automatically become your debt.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; do not sign "co-borrower" or "guarantor" on your spouse's IOU or guarantee letter, and do not write "we will repay this money" in the chat, which may be regarded as subsequent ratification.
- Say it in plain language: If your spouse borrows money behind your back, as long as it obviously exceeds the daily expenses of the family and you have not signed or ratified it, it will not automatically become your debt. The debtor must prove that the money was used for joint living or joint business.But if the money for buying a house does turn into a house that you live together or share, the other party will often be able to prove this level.During the divorce, if the other parent suddenly takes out an IOU signed only by the spouse, they must first cross-examine whether the loan took place: if there is only a transfer voucher, the party claiming the loan must continue to provide evidence.
- Benefit: Debts borne by both spouses with a joint signature or a later ratification by one party or other joint expressions of intention, as well as debts borne by one party in his own name during the marriage for the daily needs of the family, are joint debts of the husband and wife; debts borne by one party in his personal name that exceed the daily needs of the family are not joint debts of the husband and wife, unless the creditor can prove that the debt is used for the joint life, joint production and operation of the husband and wife, or is based on the common intention of both parties.The money spent to buy a house is obviously beyond the daily needs of the family, and the burden of proof is on the party claiming the creditor's claim; but on the other hand, if the money does become a house that the couple lives together or shares, the creditor can often prove that it is "used for the couple's common life," so what really works is the step in Article 11 (National, starting from 2021)
- Evidence level: A

### 15. Don’t just ask “Is there any” about emotional value? It depends on the quality of the relationship.
<!-- Cost tag: money=0 time=medium perseverance=some income=medium caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; use several conflicts and several months of getting along to observe the quality of the relationship before marriage, instead of just checking the list of conditions
- Say it in plain language: A summary of 126 studies and more than 72,000 people shows that the higher the quality of marriage, the better the health. However, this relationship is very weak. The magnitude is similar to the impact of healthy behaviors such as diet on health. There is basically no difference between men and women.The usable part is: whether a relationship is good or not depends on how many conflicts and how many months we have been together, not on a checklist of conditions.As for "whether a bad marriage is worse than no marriage at all", there is no direct comparison data and it is only an inference at this time.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 126 studies and more than 72,000 people showed that higher marital quality is associated with better health. The effect size r is between 0.07 and 0.21. Among them, the risk of death r = 0.11 and the cardiovascular response during conflict r = −0.13. The magnitude is equivalent to the impact of healthy behaviors such as diet on health. Studies that explicitly test gender differences have basically found no differences between men and women. A review in the cardiovascular field pointed out that marital dissatisfaction and marital quality have a significant impact on cardiovascular risk.
- Evidence level: B (disputed)
- Source: Robles TF, et al. (2014). Marital status and outcomes in patients with cardiovascular disease. Trends Cardiovasc Med30(4):215–220. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcm.2019.05.012>
- Note: The controversy is that the effect size is small, some outcomes have publication bias, and most of them are cross-sectional designs that cannot infer cause and effect. The sentence "a bad marriage is worse than no marriage" can only be regarded as an inference, not a numerical conclusion.
