# 8. Don't Put Yourself at Risk: Legal and Property Security

This section's dimension is money and personal freedom: what the law says the liability is, how much loss you can avoid, and what procedural rights you have; it does not convert to lifespan. Article numbers follow the verified official originals. Ordering is from highest to lowest by "biggest loss from a single incident, easiest action to take"; items requiring advance arrangement go in the middle; experience-based items go last.

### 1. After a traffic accident, first stop, help people, call the police — don't flee
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=large dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; stop, turn on hazard lights, help people, call the police; at most it costs an hour or two waiting for the traffic police; for minor accidents with no injuries and no dispute over the facts, photograph the other party's name, phone number, license plate, driver's license number, insurance certificate number and the collision point, then both parties sign and can leave the scene to settle it themselves
- Say it in plain language: If you flee, liability is presumed to be all yours: your license is revoked and you can never retake it for life; the crime of causing a traffic accident with flight gets 3–7 years, and flight leading to death gets 7+ years. Even more practical: vehicle-damage and third-party liability insurance never pay out for flight — a few hundred thousand in compensation all comes out of your own pocket. Staying to help and call the police costs at most an hour or two.
- Benefit: The fleeing party bears full responsibility for the accident, reduced only if the other party's fault is proved; flight leads to license revocation and a life ban on re-obtaining it; the crime of traffic accident with flight gets 3–7 years, flight causing death 7+ years; commercial vehicle damage and third-party liability insurance do not pay for "flight after a traffic accident" or "intentional destruction, scene faking, evidence destruction"; hundreds of thousands in compensation all self-paid (nationwide)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2021). Road Traffic Safety Law (2021 revision, Articles 70, 101). <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html> (Beijing Traffic Management Bureau reprint); State Council (2017). Implementing Regulations of the Road Traffic Safety Law (State Council Order No. 405, 2017 revision, Articles 86, 92). <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75>; NPC (1997). Criminal Law (Article 133). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; China Insurance Association (2020). Model Clauses for Commercial Motor Vehicle Insurance (2020 edition, Articles 9, 22). <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Note: When someone is injured, help first, then call the police; mark the position if you move the scene to help. "Hit and left without feeling it" can also count as flight in later determinations — call the police if unsure. The model clauses are an industry model text; each company's terms follow its own policy.

### 2. If you find you've been scammed, immediately call 110 or 96110 to request stop-payment — don't investigate on your own first
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one phone call, state the transfer time, amount, and the other party's account; keep the transfer screenshot and chat records
- Say it in plain language: The few dozen minutes right after the transfer is the only window: the police can carry out emergency stop-payment and fast freezing, and banks must cooperate; once the money is layered and moved away, it's basically unrecoverable. So call 110 or 96110 first; don't investigate or ask around on your own first. A 96110 incoming call means the police are trying to dissuade you — you must answer.
- Benefit: Public security organs have the power to make immediate account inquiry, emergency stop-payment, and rapid freezing of involved funds; banks and payment institutions must cooperate; frozen money is returned to the victim per procedure; once money is moved away and scattered, recovery probability drops sharply — the earlier the stop-payment, the more effective; 96110 is the national unified anti-fraud warning hotline, incoming calls are police dissuading you — answer them (nationwide, from December 2022)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 20, 34). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). 96110 incoming calls: please answer. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (provincial PSB page)
- Note: The law only requires "establishing a stop-payment freezing system"; how much can be recovered depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. The "National Anti-Fraud Center" App and 96110 introduction are cited from the Fujian PSB page; the Ministry of Public Security website original page couldn't be opened. Anyone who claims to be police/procuratorate/court and asks you to transfer to a "safety account" is a scam.

### 3. Remember the hard anti-fraud rules: don't trust unknown calls, don't reveal information, don't click links, verify a transfer twice
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=some benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; memorize the "three don'ts plus one do" rule; anything asking you to transfer, share screen, download a strange App, click a link, or report a verification code — hang up first, then verify by calling the official number; install the "National Anti-Fraud Center" App
- Say it in plain language: Anything asking you to transfer, share screen, download a strange App, click a link, or report a verification code — hang up first, then call back the official number to verify. People claiming to be police/procuratorate/court, customer service, bosses, or platform risk-control, asking you to "cooperate with investigation," "cancel membership," "earn commissions by placing orders," or "invest in inside info" — all the same script in different packaging. Recognizing it in advance is much cheaper than chasing money afterward.
- Benefit: The law requires the government and relevant departments to publicize the various types of telecom and online fraud; public security organs work with financial, telecom, and cyberspace departments to establish a warning and dissuasion system, promptly dissuading potential victims identified by early warning; 96110 is this national unified warning-dissuasion hotline, answer incoming calls; recognition in advance is much cheaper than stop-payment afterward; how much stop-payment can recover depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report (see item 2) (nationwide, from December 2022)
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 8, 20, 34). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). 96110 incoming calls: please answer. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (provincial PSB page, including the slogan "don't trust unknown calls, don't reveal information, don't click links, verify transfers twice" and "beware of scam calls starting with 192")
- Note: B-grade because the slogan is official publicity wording, with no figure for "how much less you get scammed by remembering it." ### 4. Seeing a face on video or hearing a voice on the phone isn't verification — for transfers, hang up and call back using an old number from your own contacts
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=some benefit=large dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one extra phone call, a few extra minutes; post fewer front-facing videos and long voice messages on public accounts
- Say it in plain language: Seeing a face and hearing a voice are not verification. The Ministry of Public Security's Cybersecurity Bureau's method: ask the other party to do one continuous head-turn, or fully cover the face with a palm and quickly move it away; current face-swapping tends to stutter and distort on such occlusion and light-change. But the most solid move is to hang up and call back an old number saved in your own contacts, or ask a mutual acquaintance to check on the side. If the other party refuses live video or refuses the designated action citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting," treat it as a scam directly. Also, compliant platforms must label AI content, and scammers won't — so "no AI label seen" cannot be used in reverse as evidence that the other party is real.
- Benefit: The MOPS Cybersecurity Bureau's three methods: action verification (ask the other party to do a continuous head-turn, or fully cover the face with a palm and quickly move it away — "current AI technology is prone to stutter, blur, or distortion when simulating these complex facial occlusions and light changes"); voice details ("AI-generated voice sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses and emotional inflections, or carries a slight mechanical feel and abnormal background noise"); multi-channel verification is the "most core move" — "hang up and directly dial the other party's commonly used number that you are familiar with (don't call back the number shown in the suspicious call), or verify indirectly through a third person you both know"; the slogan is "don't trust video and voice easily, verify with actions and multi-channel." Face-swapping and voice-swapping have long been legally banned for fraud; platforms services for face generation, face replacement, synthetic voice, and voice imitation must add prominent labels, and no organization or individual may maliciously delete, alter, fabricate, or conceal labels — but the ones playing by the rules are platforms; scammers won't add labels, so "no AI label seen" cannot be used in reverse as evidence the other party is real. In a case published by the MOPS Criminal Investigation Bureau, a 14-person gang was caught with over 50,000 synthetic dynamic face videos and 500,000+ pieces of citizens' personal information, with 60 cloud servers seized (nationwide, labeling obligation from September 1, 2025)
- Evidence level: B
- Source: MOPS Cybersecurity Bureau (2025). Seeing is not believing — beware of new AI fraud! <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm> (Fujian PSB reprint); Cyberspace Administration of China, MIIT, MOPS (2022). Provisions on the Administration of Deep Synthesis in Internet Information Services (Articles 6, 16, 17, effective January 10, 2023). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>; CAC, MIIT, MOPS, NRTA (2025). Measures for Labeling AI-Generated Synthetic Content (Article 10, effective September 1, 2025). <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>; MOPS Criminal Investigation Bureau, CCTV News (2026). Police seized over 50,000 synthetic dynamic face videos: who is "copying" your face? <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm> (Fujian PSB reprint)
- Note: B-grade because officials give recognition criteria and cases, not a figure for "how much the scam rate drops after verifying via this method." Call back using an old number saved in your own contacts, not the number shown on your caller ID, and not a new number the other party sent in the chat; if the other party refuses live video or designated actions citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting," treat as a scam. Action verification is only auxiliary — models keep improving; the only solid one is the third: multi-channel verification. Already transferred, see item 2 of this section; preventing your face and voice from being collected, see section 14. MOPS official website (mps.gov.cn) still returned 521 site-wide; the Cybersecurity Bureau and Criminal Investigation Bureau originals taken from the Fujian PSB reprint pages.

### 5. If accused or summoned, get a lawyer first; no private settlement, no deleting records
<!-- cost_tag: money=high time=medium willpower=some benefit=large dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: Lawyer fees start at several thousand yuan; if financially strapped, apply to a legal aid institution for a free lawyer; during interrogation answer only questions related to this case, leave the rest to your lawyer
- Say it in plain language: From the first interrogation or the first coercive measure, you have the right to retain a lawyer; when a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention house must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and meetings are not monitored. You have the right to refuse to answer questions unrelated to the case, and no one can force you to testify against yourself. One summons lasts at most 12 hours (24 for especially major and complex cases); continuous summons to effectively detain is not allowed. Don't privately contact the other party; any "private settlement" may be taken as an admission; chat records, calls, and travel history are your self-proof materials — deleting them only removes one of your own pieces of evidence.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation or the taking of coercive measures you have the right to retain a lawyer; only lawyers may be retained at the investigation stage; when a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention house must arrange it within 48 hours at most, and meetings are not monitored; no one may be forced to testify against himself, and you have the right to refuse answering questions unrelated to the case; one summons or summons-by-force lasts at most 12 hours, 24 for especially major and complex cases, and continuous summons as disguised detention is prohibited; fabricating facts to falsely accuse others into criminal pursuit gets 3 years or less, causing serious consequences 3–10 years; after detention or arrest, if the case is dropped, not prosecuted, or acquitted, you can apply for state compensation, calculated per day at the prior year's national average daily wage of employees (nationwide)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2018). Criminal Procedure Law (2018 amendment, Articles 34, 35, 39, 52, 119, 120). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>; NPC (1997). Criminal Law (Article 243). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; Standing Committee of the NPC (2012). State Compensation Law (2012 amendment, Articles 17, 33). <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html> (NBS reprint)
- Note: Don't privately contact the other party or their family; any "private settlement" may be read as an admission; chat records, call records, and travel records are your self-proof material — deleting only loses evidence. The crime of false accusation does not apply to mistaken reports or truthful reporting that doesn't hold up. This item makes no gender distinction for the accused. The three expanded items are in items 31 (pursuing the fabricator), 32 (insufficient evidence should acquit, and appeal/re-trial), and 33 (state compensation after detention calculated by the day).

### 6. If you've drunk, don't touch the steering wheel — including e-bikes and "just moving the car a bit"
<!-- cost_tag: money=low time=low willpower=some benefit=large dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: Designated-driver service or taxi, a few dozen yuan; agree beforehand at gatherings who won't drink
- Say it in plain language: Driving under the influence: 6-month license suspension plus a fine of one to two thousand; a repeat offense gets you detained and the license revoked; drunk driving revokes the license directly, no re-examination for 5 years, plus criminal liability for the crime of dangerous driving, leaving a record that affects political review and some occupations. Driving commercial vehicles is heavier: drunk driving gets a 10-year driving ban and lifetime ban on commercial vehicles. Commercial vehicle insurance never pays for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs. "Just moving the car a bit" counts as driving.
- Benefit: Driving under the influence: 6-month license suspension plus 1000–2000 yuan fine; repeat offense: detention under 10 days and license revocation; drunk driving: license revoked directly, no re-examination for 5 years, plus criminal liability for dangerous driving (detention and fine); operating vehicles: drunk-driving detention 15 days, 5000 yuan fine, 5-year ban; drunk-driving 10-year ban and lifetime ban on commercial vehicles; drunk-driving or driving under influence causing a major accident constituting a crime gets a lifetime driving ban; commercial vehicle insurance never pays for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs (nationwide)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2021). Road Traffic Safety Law (2021 revision, Article 91). <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html> (Beijing Traffic Management Bureau reprint); Standing Committee of the NPC (2015). Criminal Law Amendment (IX) (Article 8, i.e. Criminal Law Article 133-A). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>; China Insurance Association (2020). Model Clauses for Commercial Motor Vehicle Insurance (2020 edition, Articles 9, 22). <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Note: Drunk driving is a criminal offense, leaving a record that affects your political review and some professional qualifications. The blood-alcohol thresholds for "under the influence" and "drunk" are set by the national standard; this section hasn't verified the specific values — don't estimate by feeling.

### 7. Don't lend your bank card, phone card, or payment account to anyone; "running points" is not a side job
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=large dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; refuse requests like "lend me your card to relay money, I'll pay you a few hundred"
- Say it in plain language: Renting, lending, or selling your bank card, phone card, or payment account is itself illegal: confiscate illegal gains and fine 1–10×, up to 200,000 yuan if no gains, detention up to 15 days for serious circumstances, plus credit-record entries and account-function restrictions. If you know the other party will use them for crime yet still provide payment-settlement help, that's the crime of aiding information-network criminal activities, punishable by up to 3 years. In practice "knowledge" is often presumed from objective circumstances — taking benefits, clearly abnormal transaction volumes can all count.
- Benefit: Renting, lending, or buying/selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, and internet accounts is itself illegal: confiscate illegal gains with a 1–10× fine, up to 200,000 yuan if no gains, detention up to 15 days for serious circumstances, plus possible credit-record entries, account function restrictions, and suspension of non-counter services; anyone who knowingly provides payment settlement or other help to others committing crimes via the internet, with serious circumstances, commits the crime of aiding information-network criminal activities, punishable by up to 3 years and a fine (nationwide)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 31, 44). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Standing Committee of the NPC (2015). Criminal Law Amendment (IX) (Article 29, i.e. Criminal Law Article 287-B). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Note: "Knowledge" in practice is often presumed from objective circumstances; taking benefits or clearly irrational transaction volumes may all be found as knowledge. If your card is used to receive stolen money, even without a crime the account gets frozen and you get disciplined.

### 8. Check your credit report free twice a year — look for loans and cards you didn't apply for
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=no benefit=medium dimension=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; ten minutes online
- Say it in plain language: Regulations give you the right to get your own credit report free twice a year, ten minutes online. When an ID is used by someone else for online loans or cards, people usually only find out when a home-purchase loan is rejected — by then it's been dragging on for years. Adverse records stay for 5 years from the end of the act, and should be deleted after; you can file objections, with a written reply within 20 days. Check via the PBOC Credit Reference Center official site or mobile banking, not third-party "credit-check" Apps — those Apps are themselves collecting your info.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation provides "the personal information subject has the right to obtain his credit report free twice a year"; adverse information "stays for 5 years from the end of the adverse act or event; over 5 years it should be deleted"; objections to the report can be raised, with the reference agency or information provider replying in writing within 20 days of receiving the objection
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2013). Credit Reporting Industry Regulations (State Council Order No. 631, Articles 16, 17, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-01/29/content_2303.htm>

### 9. In a conflict, call the police instead of throwing a punch — almost always, the one who throws first loses
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=low willpower=some benefit=large dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; hold back the retort and the shove, back away to a crowded area and call the police, let the other party's actions stay on camera
- Say it in plain language: Hitting someone: 5–10 days detention plus 500–1000 yuan fine; in a group, or hitting old people, pregnant women, children, or the disabled, or repeated or one-against-many beatings: 10–15 days detention plus one to two thousand; a minor injury is a criminal case, up to 3 years; serious injury 3–10 years. Mutual fighting is not self-defense — so almost always, the one who throws the first punch loses. Backing to a crowded area and calling the police so the other party's actions stay on camera works better than hitting back. If the other party is already down or has run and you keep pursuing, it's no longer defense.
- Benefit: Assaulting another or intentionally hurting: 5–10 days detention plus 500–1000 yuan fine; in a group, against old people, pregnant women, children, or the disabled, repeated assault, or one-against-many: 10–15 days detention plus 1000–2000 yuan fine; group fighting under the crime of provocation: up to 15 days detention and 2000 yuan fine; intentionally injuring another to the degree of a crime: up to 3 years; serious injury 3–10 years; mutual fighting is not self-defense, but counterattack is generally found as defense when the other party strikes first with clearly excessive methods, or when you tried to avoid the conflict and the other party continued the infringement; excessive defense requires both "clearly exceeding necessary limits" and "causing major harm," and even when excessive, punishment should be reduced or waived (nationwide; Public Security Administration Punishments Law effective January 1, 2026)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the NPC (2025). Public Security Administration Punishments Law (2025 revision, Articles 30, 51). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>; NPC (1997). Criminal Law (Articles 20, 234). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; SPC, SPP, MOPS (2020). Guiding Opinions on the Lawful Application of the Justifiable Defense System (Fa Fa [2020] No. 31, items 6, 9, 11, 14). <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/251611.html>
- Note: After the 2025 revision of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law all article numbers changed; assault moved from old Article 43 to new Article 51, so materials citing old article numbers are outdated. The injury standard for "minor injury enters criminal law" is set by the appraisal standard, not verified in this section. Once the other party is down or has fled and you continue to pursue, the unlawful infringement has ended — it's no longer defense.

### 10. Whoever wronged you — unpaid wages, fired, swindled money — go through complaints, arbitration, and lawsuit; don't go take personal revenge
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=yes benefit=large dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; complaints and arbitration are free; two visits and a few months of waiting — the hard part is holding back from violence during those months
- Say it in plain language: The worst outcome of the legal path is the money not coming back; the outcome of revenge is the money still not coming back, plus your own life. The Wuxi case: Xu Jiejin, upset about not getting his diploma and dissatisfied with his internship pay, attacked with a knife killing 8 and wounding 17, sentenced to death and already executed — he was angry at the school and the money, stabbed fellow students, and didn't get a single cent. For unpaid wages use complaints and arbitration; in 2024 the whole country recovered 8.65 billion yuan for 654,000 people this way.
- Benefit: The legal path has official numbers: in 2024, labor inspection nationwide handled 137,000 cases, recovered 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654,000 people; 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration; how to proceed is in section 7, item 2. The revenge path's price is written in Criminal Law Article 232: "Whoever intentionally kills another person shall be sentenced to death, life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years; where the circumstances are relatively minor, fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years." Official case: Xu Jiejin, failing to receive his diploma due to failing exams and dissatisfied with his internship remuneration, attacked on November 16, 2024 at Wuxi Institute of Arts and Technology with a knife, killing 8 and wounding 17; sentenced at first instance to death for intentional homicide with deprivation of political rights for life, executed on January 20, 2025 after approval by the Supreme People's Court — he was angry at the school and the pay, stabbed fellow students, and got none of the money (nationwide)

### 11. No matter how full of hate, don't strike at unrelated people: driving into crowds or attacking in public places is the crime of endangering public safety by dangerous means — starting at 3 years, and death means the death penalty

Official case: Fan Weiqiu, "determined to vent personal resentment by driving into a crowd," drove on the night of November 11, 2024 into a crowd exercising at the Zhuhai Sports Center, killing 35 and wounding 43; sentenced at first instance to death for endangering public security by dangerous means with deprivation of political rights for life, executed on January 20, 2025 after Supreme People's Court approval — he was angry at the divorce judgment, hit strangers running (nationwide)

### 35. Cyberbullied: first enable protection, first fix the evidence, then pick among platform, injunction, and police report
<!-- cost_tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=medium dimension=freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; ten-some minutes on the spot for protection and evidence; applying for an injunction or private prosecution means running to court and possibly hiring a lawyer
- Say it in plain language: First do three on-the-spot things — turn on all the platform's protection settings (block strangers or specific users, restrict the visibility of your own posts, forbid reposts or comments, receive only friends' private messages or even no private messages); fix the content with the platform's quick-evidence feature; then report through the prominent quick-entry. Then three paths: if it's ongoing and not stopping promptly would cause irreparable harm, apply to the court for a personality-rights infringement injunction to make it stop; insult and defamation in principle need you to sue yourself, and if you genuinely have difficulty getting evidence, the court can ask police to help identify who and collect evidence of spread and impact; once the evidence is collected to the standard of private prosecution, the court shall file it; at the "seriously endangering social order" tier it converts to public prosecution, no need for your own evidence. Doxxing that posts your personal information, with serious circumstances, is handled as the crime of infringing citizens' personal information.
- Benefit: Fa Fa [2023] No. 14, item 15: where the right holder has evidence that the actor is performing or about to perform unlawful acts infringing personality rights, and failing to stop promptly would cause irreparable harm to his lawful rights and interests, upon application per Civil Code Article 997, courts may lawfully issue a personality-rights infringement injunction; item 11: for private-prosecution cases, "where the victim genuinely has difficulty providing evidence, police assistance may be requested," and police should promptly identify the actor and collect evidence of spread and impact, with internet service providers providing necessary technical support; "where after police-assisted evidence collection the case meets private-prosecution acceptance conditions, the people's court shall decide to file the case," and where evidence can't be collected it should be explained in writing; item 12 lists five situations to be found as "seriously endangering social order" under Criminal Law Article 246(2): causing the victim or their near relatives mental disorders, suicide, or other serious consequences with bad social impact; arbitrarily targeting ordinary members of the public with large-scale information dissemination stirring mass vulgar malicious comments with bad social impact; insulting/defaming multiple people or repeated dissemination with bad social impact; organizing or directing people to disseminate across multiple platforms on a large scale with bad social impact; and others. Item 4: organizing "doxxing," unlawfully collecting and publishing citizens' personal information to a large unspecified number of people, with serious circumstances, is convicted as the crime of infringing citizens' personal information. The Governance Provisions on Cyberbullying Information Article 23 requires platforms to provide protection options such as blocking strangers or specific users, restricting the visibility of one's own posts, forbidding reposts or comments, receiving only friends' private messages or refusing all private messages; Article 25 requires providing quick-evidence functions; Article 26 requires prominent quick complaint/report entry points; Article 15 requires platforms to report to police promptly on discovery of suspected crimes
- Evidence level: A
