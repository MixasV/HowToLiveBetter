# 12. Starting a business and doing business: don’t lose your family’s wealth

The focus of this section is on money and legal responsibilities: opening a store or setting up a company, which step will make your personal property, family property and spouse's property liable? How is the law stipulated and whether it can be separated in advance? It is not converted into life span.The order is from high to low in order of "the easiest way to lose your family's wealth and the easiest way to avoid it", with signatures at the top, items about how to do things at the back, and experience items at level C.

### 1. Only use the money you can afford to lose to start a business, without leaving your family assets or borrowing money to start a business.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; set a number for yourself before taking action. If you lose it all, it will not affect the mortgage payment, raising children, and medical treatment. If you exceed this number, you will not invest; family money and borrowed money are not included in this number.
- Say it in plain language: The money owed by a self-employed person must be repaid with personal property. If it is not clear that it is a family business, the money must be repaid with the family property; if the husband and wife sign together, or the borrowed money is used for joint business, the property of both people must be repaid together.When the company cannot repay its due debts, it will also be required to withdraw the undue capital contributions in advance.Therefore, the amount of investment is determined based on "If you lose all money, you can still pay off the mortgage and see a doctor."
- Benefit: Loss is not just money lost: the debts of individual industrial and commercial households are borne by personal property if they are run by individuals, or by family property if they are run by a family or cannot be distinguished; debts signed by a husband and wife or ratified later are joint debts, and money borrowed by one party in the name of an individual is also considered a joint debt as long as the creditor can prove that it was used for the joint production and operation of the husband and wife; when the company repays its due debts, creditors who have subscribed for capital contributions that have not yet matured can demand early payment (nationwide)
- Evidence level: B
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 56, 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>; National People's Congress Standing Committee (2023). Company Law (revised in 2023, Articles 47, 54).<https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Note: For official statistics such as enterprise survival rate and average life span, we could not find the verifiable original text on the official websites of the National Bureau of Statistics and the State Administration for Market Regulation, so we did not write numbers, only legal consequences, and were given a B grade."Affordable" means that you don't have to sell your house or ask relatives to borrow money to repair the hole even if you lose everything.

### 2. Do not sign a personal guarantee for a company loan, let alone your spouse.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; when banks, suppliers, or landlords require "shareholder personal joint guarantee" and "spouse signature", treat that page as a waiver of limited liability. If you can't sign it, don't sign it. If you want to sign it, just sign a general guarantee and indicate the upper limit of the guarantee amount.
- Say it in plain language: If you sign a "joint and several liability guarantee" and the company still doesn't pay back the money, the creditors can skip the company and ask for it directly from you; if you write a "general guarantee", you should sue the company first, and then it's your turn after the company's assets are still short of the execution.If your spouse signs, it becomes the property of both people and is returned together - after signing this page, the "limitation" of the limited company will not count for you personally.
- Benefit: The guarantee contract stipulates that you will repay if the debtor fails to repay; if it is written as a "joint and several liability guarantee", the creditor can come to you directly without going to the company; if it is written as a "general guarantee", it will be your turn to sue or arbitrate and enforce the company's property if the company's property is not paid.Debts that are jointly signed by husband and wife or ratified later are joint debts of husband and wife. If the spouses sign, the property of both people will be repaid together (Nationwide, starting from 2021)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 681, 687, 688, 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: In the practice of small and micro enterprise loans, banks almost require shareholders to jointly and severally guarantee the loan. This does not mean "don't loan", but it means knowing what you are signing before signing, and limiting the guarantee amount to the amount you can "afford to lose" in Article 1.The general rules for IOUs and guarantees are found in Section 8, Article 16.

### 3. Choose the right entity before opening: a limited company will only be "limited" if self-employed individuals and partners pay the ultimate loss.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=little, time=medium, perseverance=no, profit=big, caliber=money -->
- Cost: Register at the local government service network office. The main cost is the time for monthly accounting and tax filing of the limited company or the agency accounting fee (different from place to place); a few hours at a time
- Say it in plain language: Self-employed individuals and general partners will be compensated to the end, while shareholders of a limited company will only be compensated for the amount they subscribed.However, the amount subscribed must be paid out within 5 years. The registered capital of 1 million is a potential debt of 1 million; the capital withdrawn must be paid back. The company's accounts and its own accounts are mixed, and a one-person company cannot tell whether the two amounts of money are separate, so the "limited" is still invalid.
- Benefit: The debts of an individual business owner are borne by personal or family property; the partners of a general partnership bear unlimited joint and several liability for the debts of the partnership; in a limited partnership, only the limited partners are limited to their subscribed capital contributions; the shareholders of a limited liability company bear liability to the company limited to the amount of their subscribed capital contributions.However, "limited" will become invalid in three situations: the subscribed capital must be paid in full within 5 years of the company's establishment, and creditors can demand early payment when the company repays its due debts; capital withdrawn must be returned; those who abuse the company's independent status to evade debt, or one-person company shareholders cannot prove that the company's property is separated from their own property, will bear joint and several liability for the company's debts (National, starting from July 2024)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 56). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>; National People's Congress Standing Committee (2006). - Note: The premise of "limited" is that public and private accounts should be kept separate: company accounts and personal accounts should not be mixed, company money should not be used to pay household expenses, and all accounts should be kept.Don’t fill in big numbers for registered capital just to look good. The amount you subscribe for will be paid out within 5 years. Filling in 1 million means 1 million in potential debt.

### 4. Not being a nominal shareholder or holding equity on behalf of others
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; reject the request to "borrow your ID card to register as a shareholder" or "put the shares in your name first"
- Say it in plain language: Anyone whose name is registered in the register is a legal shareholder: the subscribed capital contribution must be paid in full on time. If the capital contribution is not paid, the company will be compensated for losses in addition to making up the payment. When the company is established, if other shareholders fail to pay in full, you will be responsible for the shortfall.The business is done by others, and the debt is in your name.
- Benefit: Registered shareholders are legal shareholders: they must pay their subscribed capital contributions in full and on time according to the articles of association. If they fail to pay on time, they will be compensated for losses to the company in addition to making up the payment; if there are shareholders who fail to make actual contributions when the company is established, other shareholders at the time of establishment will be jointly and severally liable to the extent of insufficient capital contributions; when the company cannot pay its due debts, creditors can require the undue subscribed capital contributions to be paid in advance.The actual business person is not on the registration, but the debt is in your name (Nationwide, starting from July 2024)
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2023). Company Law (revised in 2023, Articles 47, 49, 50, and 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>

### 11. When receiving money, write "deposit" instead of "deposit". Please specify the amount of liquidated damages. The account period will be reviewed when lending.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; use the word "deposit" in the contract, and the amount should not exceed 20% of the contract amount. Collect payment first and then ship. Check the other party before giving the account.
- Say it in plain language: Write the word "deposit" to have a double rule: if you don't get your deposit back if you break the contract, the other party will refund you double the deposit if it breaks the contract, and the part exceeding 20% of the contract amount will not count.A "deposit" is usually treated as an advance payment and nothing is guaranteed.The amount of liquidated damages is specified, and the court can adjust it if it is too high or too low; the payment period is equivalent to lending your own money to the other party without interest. If the other party fails, it will be a bad debt.
