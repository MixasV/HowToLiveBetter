# 19. On-the-job, separation and work-related injuries

Caliber: money.This section only talks about how to calculate money, how to sign, and how to calculate deadlines.The first three should be paid during employment, the middle six are due to resignation, and the last seven are occupational diseases and work-related injuries. The money for work-related injuries is an order of magnitude greater than that of layoffs, and the deadlines are also stricter. The injuries caused by occupational diseases cannot be replaced at all.

### 1. Overtime pay is calculated in three levels: 1.5 times, 2 times, and 3 times. If you don’t pay, you will complain to the labor inspection. If you don’t pay overtime, you will have to pay an additional 50% to 100%.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; usually keep a copy of your clock-in records, shift schedules, overtime approval forms, and assignment records in the work group
- Say it in plain language: Overtime work on normal days is calculated as 1.5 times the salary; overtime work on holidays without compensatory time off is calculated as 2 times the salary; working on legal holidays such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day and National Day is calculated as 3 times the salary, and it cannot be topped up by compensatory time off.Overtime work generally does not exceed 1 hour per day, up to 3 hours for special reasons, and does not exceed 36 hours per month.If you don't pay, you can go to the labor inspector. If you still don't pay after being ordered to pay within a time limit, the unit will have to pay an additional half to one time of the amount owed.
- Benefit: Legal working hours shall not exceed 8 hours per day, and the average working hours shall not exceed 44 hours per week; extended working hours shall generally not exceed 1 hour per day, and those that need to be extended for special reasons shall not exceed 3 hours per day and shall not exceed 36 hours per month.Three levels of overtime pay: If extended working hours are arranged, the payment shall be no less than 150% of the salary; if work is arranged on rest days and compensatory time off cannot be arranged, the payment shall be no less than 200%; if work is arranged on statutory holidays, no less than 300% shall be paid - there is no "compensatory time off" option in the statutory holiday legislation.If the unit arranges overtime and fails to pay overtime pay, the labor administrative department shall order the payment within a time limit; if the unit fails to pay overtime, the unit shall be ordered to pay additional compensation to the worker at a standard of not less than 50% but not more than 100% of the amount payable (nationally)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Labor Contract Law (Article 85, Item 3).<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Rest days and statutory holidays are not the same thing: Statutory holidays throughout the year are only those statutory days such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, and National Day. Consecutive days made up of extra days will be counted as rest days at 200%."The company stipulates that overtime must be approved" and "I stayed here voluntarily" cannot be exempted from payment obligations, but it will affect the proof, so records of job assignments and approvals must be kept.The comprehensive calculated working time system and the irregular working time system must be approved by the labor administrative department before they can be implemented. It is not the company itself that has the final say.The statute of limitations for arbitration of disputes is set out in Section 8, Article 17.

### 2. Annual leave is calculated as 5, 10, or 15 days based on accumulated service years. Unused leave will be discounted at 300% of daily salary.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; sort out your working years in all units, as evidenced by social security payment records
- Say it in plain language: If the total length of service in all units is 1 year but less than 10 years, 5 days of annual leave will be given each year, 10 days for 10 years or more, and 15 days for 20 years or more. The same applies if you change jobs.If the unit cannot arrange it and you insist on it, you will be paid 3 times the daily wage for those days. If you deduct the original share, you will get 2 times more. The daily wage is the monthly wage divided by 21.75.Once the "Voluntary Giving Up of Annual Leave" form is signed, only the normal salary is left.
- Benefit: Annual leave will be enjoyed after working continuously for 12 months; the number of days is determined by the **accumulated** working time, 5 days for 1 year but less than 10 years, 10 days for 10 years but less than 20 years, and 15 days for 20 years. National statutory holidays and rest days are not included in the annual leave.If the employer is unable to arrange the leave due to work needs and does not take the leave with the employee's consent, "annual leave wages will be paid at 300% of the employee's daily wage income" for the number of untaken days. This 300% includes the portion during the normal working period, so the actual additional payment is 200%.Daily salary = personal monthly salary ÷ 21.75. The monthly salary is calculated based on the average monthly salary excluding overtime wages in the 12 months before payment.For new employees in the current year, the salary will be calculated based on the remaining calendar days in the unit; when leaving the company, the salary will be calculated and paid based on the working time in the current year, and no deduction will be made for those who have already taken extra leave.If the unit fails to arrange and pay, it will be ordered to make corrections within a time limit. If it fails to make corrections within the time limit, additional compensation will be paid based on the amount of untaken annual leave wages (national, effective from January 1, 2008)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2007). - Note: Accumulated working hours are accumulated across units, not "how long you have been working at this company." ### 3. The probation period has a legal upper limit, the salary must not be less than 80%, and it can only be agreed once

- Cost: $0; please check the contract period and trial period length before signing.
- Say it in plain language: If the contract is signed within 1 year, the probation period can be up to 1 month, between 1 to 3 years, up to 2 months, and over 3 years, up to 6 months; the same company can only trial you once, and it is illegal to transfer you again after you become a regular employee.The salary during the probation period cannot be less than 80% of the minimum salary for the same position or the salary agreed in the contract, nor can it be less than the local minimum wage.During the period that exceeds the legal length, the unit will make additional compensation based on your monthly salary standard at the end of the probation period.
- Benefit: If the contract period is more than 3 months but less than 1 year, the probation period shall not exceed 1 month; if the contract period is more than 1 year but less than 3 years, it shall not exceed 2 months; if the contract period is more than 3 years and has no fixed period, the probation period shall not exceed 6 months; for contracts with a term of completing certain work tasks or with a term of less than 3 yearsA three-month contract must not stipulate a probation period; "the same employer and the same employee can only stipulate a probation period once"; if only a probation period is stipulated, the probation period will not be established, and that period will be the term of the labor contract.The salary during the probation period "should not be lower than the lowest wage for the same position in the unit or 80% of the salary agreed in the labor contract, and shall not be lower than the minimum wage standard in the location of the employer."If the illegally agreed probation period has been fulfilled, the employer must pay compensation based on the employee's full month's salary during the probation period and the period that has been fulfilled and exceeds the statutory probation period.During the probation period, if the employer wants to terminate the contract, it can only do so in accordance with legal circumstances and must explain the reasons to the employee (National)

### 4. If you are laid off, you will be paid first. N: one month’s salary for every year, and half a month for less than six months.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; one count lasts ten minutes
- Say it in plain language: The economic compensation for being laid off is one month's salary for one year of service, one year for half a year but less than one year, and half a month's salary for less than half a year.The monthly salary here is the average of the 12 months before leaving. Bonuses, allowances, and subsidies are all taken into account. Companies most often undercut this item.If the salary is higher than 3 times the local average monthly salary in the previous year, the salary will be capped at 3 times, and the maximum number of years is 12 years.
- Benefit: The economic compensation standard stipulated in the Labor Contract Law: "One month's salary shall be paid for every full year... If it is more than six months but less than one year, it shall be calculated as one year; if it is less than six months, half a month's salary shall be paid to the worker"; if the monthly salary is three times higher than the local average monthly salary of employees in the previous year, it shall be calculated as three times, and the maximum period shall not exceed twelve years.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (effective on January 1, 2008) (Article 47). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Monthly salary refers to the average salary in the twelve months before the labor contract is terminated, including bonuses, allowances and subsidies, not just basic salary.This is most likely to be underestimated by companies

### 5. The company did not provide 30 days’ notice in advance and will have to pay an extra month’s salary.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0
- Say it in plain language: If the company lets you go without giving 30 days' written notice in advance, it will give you an extra month's salary in addition to N. This is often called N+1.When negotiating, list this month separately and don't let the company include it in the N.
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law stipulates that if the employer terminates the labor contract in accordance with this article, it shall "notify the employee in writing 30 days in advance or pay the employee an additional month's salary before terminating the labor contract."This extra month is commonly known as payment in lieu of notice, which is money other than N.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (effective on January 1, 2008) (Article 40). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: So the common outcome for legal layoffs is N+1.When negotiating, calculate N and 1 separately. Don’t let the company include notice payment into N.
