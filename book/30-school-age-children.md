# 30. Children after school

Verse 20 is about the first year after birth, verse 18 is about the money and time to raise a child, and the intervening years from 3 to 18 years old are empty.This section is added to answer only one question: when "don't delay learning" and "body" collide, what things cannot wait - because they have a time window, and after it passes, it is irreversible.Whether academic studies should be so long is beyond the scope of this section. This section only gives windows and numbers.

The first six calibers are mortality and health endpoints, number 7 is money, and numbers 8 and 9 are time.See Section 1 for drowning, traffic, helmets, and HPV vaccines. ### 1. If your child complains of sudden severe pain that is getting worse, go to the hospital immediately and don’t let him endure it until school is over.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Mortality -->
- Cost: 0, take half a day off, go to the emergency room
- Say it in plain language: Some illnesses are calculated by the hour.When boys suddenly experience severe pain in one side of the scrotum, often accompanied by nausea and vomiting, it is testicular torsion: if treated within 6 hours, the rate of testicular preservation is 97.2%; if it is delayed to 7 to 12 hours, it drops to 79.3%, 61.3% from 13 to 18 hours, 42.5% from 19 to 24 hours, and only 7.4% remains after 48 hours.It has no room to "take a painkiller first and see", nor can it wait until school is over or after exams.The same rule applies to all pain that comes on suddenly, gets worse, or is different from usual pain.
- Benefit: Systematic review of 30 case series, 2116 patients with testicular torsion: of 1283 cases in 6-hour intervals, testicular survival rates were 97.2% at 0 to 6 hours, 79.3% at 7 to 12 hours, 61.3% at 13 to 18 hours, 42.5% at 19 to 24 hours, 24.4% at 25 to 48 hours, and beyond 48 hours7.4%; the cumulative caliber is 90.4% within 12 hours, 54.0% from 13 to 24 hours, and 18.1% over 24 hours.The authors concluded that survival time can be longer than the commonly stated 6 to 8 hours, so it should be managed aggressively even if the pain has been there for a long time
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Mellick LB, et al. (2019). A Systematic Review of Testicle Survival Time After a Torsion Event. Pediatric Emergency Care. <https://doi.org/10.1097/PEC.0000000000001287>
- Note: Testicular torsion is more common in adolescent boys, and many people endure it because it is difficult to talk about it - this sentence should be made clear to the boy in advance, and it must be said immediately if such a thing happens.Also calculated on an hourly basis are acute appendicitis (perforation that lasts for a long time), diabetic ketoacidosis, and meningitis. This section does not verify the time curves one by one. The common signal is "sudden onset, continuous aggravation, and different from any previous time."See Section 13 for emergency identification and taxi calling methods.

### 2. Don’t put off the treatment you need to do just to “wait until the exam is over”. Some windows follow bone age, not exam arrangements.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=mortality rate -->
- Cost: 0, the decision is whether to do it early or late, not whether it costs money; asking for leave and follow-up consultation will take up time, and the treatment itself is additional (the brace needs to be worn for at least 18 hours a day)
- Say it in plain language: There is a clear window for adolescent idiopathic scoliosis - if you wear a brace while the bones are still growing, the proportion of curves that require surgery is significantly reduced: in the random group, the success rate of wearing a brace was 75%, while only observation was 42%, and the effect was better if it was worn for enough time. The trial was stopped early because the brace was indeed effective.This window follows skeletal maturity, not the high school entrance examination or college entrance examination.Waiting until the exam is over, often the only option left is surgery.
- Benefit: A multicenter trial of 242 adolescent idiopathic scoliosis patients with indications for bracing (116 randomized and 126 self-selected). The primary endpoint was scoliosis progressing to more than 50 degrees (treatment failure) and not reaching that degree at skeletal maturity (treatment success): In the intention-to-treat analysis, the efficacy was 75% in the randomized bracing group and 42% in the randomized observation group (OR 4.11, 95% CI1.85–9.16); 72% vs 48% after combining randomized and self-selected cohorts (propensity score adjusted OR 1.93, 1.08–3.46); There was a significant positive correlation between the number of wearing hours and the success rate (P<0.001); The trial was terminated early because the brace was effective
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Weinstein SL, et al. (2013). Effects of bracing in adolescents with idiopathic scoliosis. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1307337>
- Note: Scoliosis is used as an example because it has a randomized trial, a clear time window, and it happens to be one of the key items in the student physical examination (see Article 5).Similar procedures include elective surgeries for strabismus, amblyopia, dental caries and congenital heart disease. The common point is that the later the procedure is performed, the worse the effect or the greater the cost. Ask a specialist for specific procedures.Asking for leave is not a problem: the maximum period of leave from compulsory education is 1 year and the student status is retained, see Article 9.

### 3. If a child is bullied, report to school on the same day and request a written response. If the child is bullied, report to the police directly if it involves beating, stealing money, or spreading rumors.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=mortality rate -->
- Cost: 0, contact the class teacher and the school’s student bullying management organization; call the police and call 110
- Say it in plain language: Children who are bullied by peers are about 2.2 times more likely to have suicidal thoughts than those who have not been bullied, and about 2.6 times more likely to have committed suicidal behavior. The correlation between cyberbullying and suicidal thoughts is stronger than offline. This conclusion does not vary with gender and age.You have a clear starting point in the system: when the school receives a report of bullying, it should immediately investigate, and if it is determined to constitute bullying, it will impose educational punishment or disciplinary sanctions on the offending student; for serious bullying that violates public security management or is suspected of crime, the school must not conceal it and must report it to the public security agency.Therefore, what we need to do is to report the incident on a daily basis, require the identification process, and leave a written record, rather than "let the teacher talk about it."
- Benefit: Meta-analysis of 34 studies, 284,375 children and adolescents: Peer bullying was associated with suicidal ideation, OR 2.23 (95% CI 2.10–2.37); and 9 studies, 70,102 individuals, with suicide attempts, OR2.55 (1.95–3.34); the results were not moderated by gender, age, and study quality, and the association between cyberbullying and suicidal ideation was stronger than that of traditional bullying.Article 21 of the "School Protection Regulations for Minors" lists five types of behavior: beating, kicking, slapping, biting, biting, pushing, pulling, insulting, taunting, and insulting nicknames, robbing, forcibly taking, or deliberately destroying property, malicious exclusion and isolation, fabricating facts, slandering, or spreading rumors through the Internet, and stipulates having an advantage in age, body, or numberIf a party intentionally or maliciously commits a crime and causes personal injury, property loss or mental damage, it can be deemed as bullying; Article 22 requires faculty and staff to promptly understand and report a student's abnormal mood or physical injury; Article 23 requires schools to immediately investigate after receiving the report and submit it to the bullying management organization for determination and notification.
Parents participate, and educational punishment or disciplinary sanctions are imposed if bullying is determined. Serious bullying that violates public security management or is suspected of crime must not be concealed, and must be reported to the public security organs and education administrative departments in a timely manner (National, effective from September 1, 2021)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: van Geel M, et al. (2014). Relationship between peer victimization, cyberbullying, and suicide in children and adolescents: a meta-analysis. JAMA Pediatrics. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2013.4143>; Ministry of Education (2021). Regulations on the protection of minors in schools (Ministry of Education Order No. 50, Articles 21, 22, and 23).<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-06/02/content_5614946.htm>
- Note: The meta-analysis is observational evidence and cannot prove that bullying alone causes suicide, but the direction and magnitude of the risk are clear.The "Comprehensive Plan for Strengthening the Comprehensive Management of Bullying among Primary and Secondary School Students" also requires a strict distinction between bullying and playing among students, which means that schools are obliged to make identification and cannot just say "children are playing around".If your child has unexplainable injuries, suddenly refuses to go to school, or repeatedly loses things, follow the signals in Article 22 to ask questions first.

### 4. Let children spend 2 hours outdoors every day. This is currently the only method to prevent myopia supported by randomized trials.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=Medium Perseverance=Some Benefit=Large Caliber=Mortality -->
- Cost: 0, but you have to make time every day, most of which you have to squeeze in from homework and online classes
- Say it in plain language: A randomized trial in 12 primary schools in Guangzhou gave first grade students 40 minutes more outdoor activity classes every day and encouraged them to go out more after class. After three years, the proportion of myopia was 30.4%, compared with 39.5% in the control school, a decrease of 9.1 percentage points.The amount given by the National Health Commission is no less than 2 hours outdoors during the day, or a total of 14 hours per week, including cloudy days.It’s the act of being outdoors that matters, not the type of exercise you do.
- Benefit: A cluster randomized trial of first-grade students in 12 primary schools in Guangzhou (952 students in intervention schools, 951 students in control schools, average age 6.6 years): The intervention added a 40-minute outdoor activity class to each school day and encouraged parents to increase outdoor activities after class; the 3-year cumulative incidence of myopia was 30.4% in the intervention group (259 cases among 853 eligible persons) and 39.5% in the control group (726287 cases), the difference was -9.1 percentage points (95% CI -14.1 to -4.1, P<0.001); the 3-year spherical equivalent change was -1.42 D vs -1.59 D (difference 0.17 D, P=0.04); the difference in axial growth was not statistically significant (P=0.07).The "Ten Core Knowledge for Preventing and Controlling Myopia in Children and Adolescents" requires children and adolescents to engage in outdoor activities for no less than 2 hours a day or a total of 14 hours per week. It is noted that outdoor activities can have a protective effect even on cloudy days, and it is required to avoid high temperature and strong sunlight in the afternoon.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: He M, et al. (2015). Effect of Time Spent Outdoors at School on the Development of Myopia Among Children in China: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2015.10803>; General Office of the National Health Commission (2023).Ten core knowledge for preventing and controlling myopia in children and adolescents (National Health Office Maternity and Childhood Letter [2023] No. 278). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6894284.htm>
- Note: This trial was only measured within the 3-year intervention window and only in children starting at age 6. The absolute difference in the incidence of myopia was 9.1 percentage points, not "no myopia".The same ten items of core knowledge also include reading and writing "one foot, one punch, one inch", using eyes at close range for 20 minutes and then looking far away for more than 20 seconds, and primary and secondary school students' use of electronic screens for non-learning purposes shall not exceed 15 minutes at a time and shall not exceed 1 hour in total per day."Staying outside" and "taking physical education class" are not the same thing. Standing outside during class also counts.

### 5. You should read the report form of the student physical examination every year by yourself. If there are any abnormal items, take them to the hospital for examination that year.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=Low Perseverance=No Benefit=Medium Caliber=Mortality -->
- Cost: 0. The physical examination fee for the compulsory education stage is borne by the school’s public funds, and parents do not have to pay for it separately.
- Say it in plain language: Primary and secondary schools organize a physical examination for students every year, including spine examination by surgeons and distance vision and refractive index by ophthalmology. The report card stipulates that overweight, obesity, malnutrition, abnormal spinal curvature, poor vision, and dental caries are listed as key guidance contents - just the ones that are most likely to be dragged away by "wait and see".So don’t just glance at the height and weight and put the list away: if there are any abnormalities in these items, take your child to a specialist to check it out that year instead of waiting for the next physical examination.
- Benefit: The "Measures for the Management of Health Examinations for Primary and Secondary School Students (2021 Edition)" stipulates that primary and secondary schools organize 1Health examinations for school students; surgical examinations include the head, neck, chest, spine, limbs, skin, and lymph nodes; eye examinations include eye appearance, far vision, and refraction; individual report sheets should include objective results, comprehensive evaluations, and health guidance suggestions, of which overweight, obesity, malnutrition, abnormal spinal curvature, poor vision, and dental caries must be the focus of guidance; the education administration department should establish files and follow up on students with health problems; the cost of health examinations for students in compulsory education is covered by school public funds (National, September 2021issued monthly)
- Evidence level: B
- Source: National Health Commission and Ministry of Education (2021). Measures for the Management of Health Examinations for Primary and Secondary School Students (2021 Edition) (National Health Commission [2021] No. 29). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-10/21/content_5644100.htm>
- Note: The reason I gave B is because this is a work specification, not an effect study - it guarantees that "check these every year, and follow up if there are any abnormalities." It does not guarantee that if it is detected, it will be dealt with.Physical examination is a screening but not a diagnosis: if you have poor vision, you should go to an ophthalmologist for a dilated refraction (Article 10); if you have abnormal spinal curvature, you should go to an orthopedics or spine surgery (Article 2). Parents must take both steps with them.

There is insufficient evidence for those aged 11 and under, and there is insufficient evidence to specifically screen for "suicide risk."The same document mentions that suicide is the second leading cause of death for people aged 10 to 19 years.Domestic schools will do a psychological assessment every school year, but that
It is a census, not a diagnosis - if the child himself says he is uncomfortable, or if you see his eating, sleeping, interests, or grades change significantly in a short period of time, you should go directly to a psychiatry or clinical psychology department.
- Benefit: U.S. Preventive Services Task Force 2022 Recommendation Statement: It is recommended that adolescents aged 12 to 18 years be screened for major depressive disorder (category B recommendation, moderate certainty, moderate net benefit); there is insufficient evidence to screen children 11 years and younger for major depression (I statement); there is insufficient evidence on the benefits and harms of screening children and adolescents for suicide risk (I statement); the document states that suicide is 10 to 19 years oldThe second cause of death in children and adolescents, severe depression in children and adolescents is closely associated with recurrent depression in adulthood, other mental disorders, and an increased risk of suicidal ideation, suicide attempts, and suicide death.The "Ten Measures to Further Strengthen the Mental Health Work of Primary and Secondary School Students" requires the establishment of a three-level service system of class teachers, full-time and part-time mental health teachers, and psychiatrists. ### 7. Do not buy products and services that claim to "cure myopia" or "reduce myopia"
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=medium Caliber=money -->

Those who falsely use the name of traditional Chinese medicine, use patients' images to compare before and after efficacy, and those who make guaranteed promises about efficacy are all within the scope of investigation.So when you see such words, you can directly exclude them without comparing prices.Eye protection devices and the like are medical devices, and e-commerce platforms are required to review their operating qualifications.

### 11. Perform pit and fissure sealing after the permanent molars erupt.
<!-- Cost tags: Money=little time=little perseverance=no profit=medium caliber=mortality rate -->
- Cost: $3–30 per tooth, no anesthesia, no teeth grinding, just a few minutes at a time; many places have free programs for school-age children
- Say it in plain language: The grooves on the occlusal surface of the permanent molars are sealed with resin. - Benefit: Cochrane review of 38 trials, 7924 children aged 5 to 16 years: Resin-based sealants versus no sealant, OR 0.12 for caries at 24 months follow-up in permanent first molars in children aged 5 to 10 years (95% CI 0.08–0.19, 7 trials, 1548 randomized, 1322 assessed, moderate-certainty evidence); if control group within 24 months16% of tooth surfaces were carious, 5.2% (3.13%–7.37%) after sealing, 6.25% (3.84%–9.63%) at 40% in the control group, and 19% (12.3%–27.2%) at 70% of the control group; OR at 48 to 54 months follow-up0.21 (0.16–0.28); authors concluded that resin sealants reduced caries by 11% to 51% at 24 months compared with no sealants; no adverse events occurred in the 4 trials that reported adverse events; there was insufficient evidence for the relative effectiveness of glass ionomer sealants and different sealing materials
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Ahovuo-Saloranta A, et al. (2017). Pit and fissure sealants for preventing dental decay in permanent teeth. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001830.pub5>
