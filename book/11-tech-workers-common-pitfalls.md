# 11. Red lines that programmers and technicians easily step on

The scope of this section is personal freedom and money: people who write code and do technology often regard the following things as "just technical issues". In fact, they correspond to specific crimes in the criminal law or specific civil compensation. Each article gives the number in the law and the cases published by the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the Supreme Judge.The Criminal Law quotes the consolidated text "According to the 11th Amendment to the Criminal Law" reprinted by the Beijing Municipal Public Security and Traffic Management Bureau; the order is from high to low in the order of "technical people are most likely to think that nothing is wrong × the consequences are heaviest", and Article 1 is the general principle.

### 1. Before taking action, ask three questions: whose interests have been harmed, how capable the other party is of pursuing the case, and how much evidence I have left; immediately find a criminal lawyer after being held accountable.
