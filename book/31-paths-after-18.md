# 31. What are the paths after the age of eighteen?

The caliber is money and time, and the objection to military service also concerns personal freedom.This section is a path map, not an instruction manual for a particular road: first put together the legal thresholds of each road, and then write down the things that "will be useful only if you know them" one by one - what you can get as a soldier, what opportunities are there in the system besides taking the public examination, whether you can make up for your academic qualifications later, and what about social security for those who do not join the work unit.

Which path you choose is your own business. This section only provides two things: which document the threshold is written in, and what deadlines and qualifications are invalidated after you go up.See Section 23 for the question of whether to study or work. See Section 19 for overtime pay, work-related injuries and severance compensation after joining the company. See Section 12 for opening a store and running a company. See Section 7 for details on what to do after losing your job. They are not repeated.

### 2. You must register for military service before October 31 of the year you turn 18; the compulsory military service lasts for two years
<!-- Cost tag: money=0 time=many perseverance=yes income=medium caliber=time -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; registration only takes a few minutes to fill out online. The cost of joining the military is two years of full-time service
- Say it in plain language: Military service registration is a legal obligation, not signing up to join the army.For men who have reached the age of 18 before December 31 of that year, the county-level military service agency should organize the initial registration before October 31 of that year. They can register online and complete it independently on the platform. If they do not register, the county-level people's government will order them to make corrections within a time limit. If they fail to make corrections within the time limit, they will be forced to perform military service obligations and be fined.If you really go, the period of active service for conscripts is two years. If you volunteer, you can join the army at the age of 17.If you want to continue working after two years, you can become a sergeant. A sergeant must serve for 12 years before being assigned a job, and a sergeant must serve for 30 years or reach 55 years old before being eligible for retirement placement.Priority in recruitment is guaranteed to college graduates. Current students and recent graduates can register at their place of residence or school.
- Benefit: "Regulations on Military Service Registration": "The military service agencies of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities without districts, and municipal districts shall, before October 31 of each year, organize male citizens in their administrative regions who have reached the age of 18 before December 31 of that year to register for initial military service." "Initial military service registration is mainly carried out through online registration, and is completed independently by citizens through the military service registration online platform."Paragraph 1 of Article 57 of the Military Service Law: "Those who refuse or evade military service registration" shall "be ordered by the people's government at the county level to make corrections within a time limit; if they fail to make corrections within the time limit, the people's government at the county level shall force them to perform their military service obligations and impose a fine"; "The period of active service for compulsory soldiers is two years"; "According to the needs of the army and their own volition, they may be recruited for a yearCitizens who are 17 years of age or older but less than 18 years old shall serve in active service"; sergeants "who have served in active service for 12 years or more or meet other conditions stipulated by the state shall be arranged by the local people's government at or above the county level in the resettlement place"; "who have served in active service for 30 years or more or who have reached the age of 55 or who meet other conditions stipulated by the state shall be placed in retirement placement"."Regulations on Military Recruitment" "Priority is ensured in the recruitment of military personnel who are graduates of ordinary colleges and universities and those who have special requirements for political, physical conditions or professional skills"; "Citizens who are full-time students or fresh graduates of ordinary colleges and universities can apply at the place of residence or school location before enrollment" (National)

### 3. Those who refuse to perform military service after being drafted will not be allowed to leave the country or return to school within two years, and will not be able to join civil servants or state-owned enterprises.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the cost is to think clearly before signing up whether you want it or not.
- Say it in plain language: Those who refuse or evade active service after being drafted will be subject to a series of joint punishments under the Military Service Law: they shall not be employed as civil servants or persons managed according to the Civil Servant Law; they shall not be recruited or hired as staff of state-owned enterprises and public institutions; they shall not be allowed to leave the country or return to school within two years; they shall be included in the list of persons who have seriously dishonestly performed their national defense obligations.This road blocks almost all roads within the system at once, as well as leaving the country and returning to school.Therefore, it is a very expensive idea to "submit it first, and then return it if it doesn't work."
- Benefit:Item 2 of paragraph 1 of Article 57 of the Military Service Law states that "Citizens who are subject to military service who refuse or evade recruitment for active service shall be ordered by the people's government at the county level to make corrections within a time limit; if they fail to make corrections within the time limit, the people's government at the county level shall force them to perform their military service obligations and impose a fine"; paragraph 2: "Those who commit the acts in item 2 of the preceding paragraph and refuse to make corrections shall not be employed as civil servants or staff members managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, and shall not be recruited or employed as state-owned enterprises and enterprises."Staff members of commercial units shall not be allowed to leave the country or return to school for two years, and shall be included in the list of persons who have seriously dishonestly performed national defense obligations and shall be subject to joint punishment."Article 58 imposes the same set of punishments on those who have joined the army: soldiers who refuse to perform their duties or flee the army for the purpose of evading military service, and are removed from the army, expelled from the military, or investigated for criminal responsibility in accordance with the law, shall be punished in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 57, and those who are removed from the list shall also be fined (National, 2021).Effective from October 1, 2020)
- Evidence level: A

### 5. There are only six situations where the government will arrange work after retirement; self-employment must be reported within 30 days
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; bring your retirement certificate to the Veterans Affairs Department of the resettlement place
- Say it in plain language: "Soldier distribution" is no longer a common situation.The current regulations only list six situations in which the government can arrange work: sergeants have served in active service for 12 years or more, individuals have received medals or honorary titles, they have received third-class military merit or above, they have received first-class commendations, they have been disabled due to combat and have been rated as Level 5 to 8, and they are children of martyrs.After two years of compulsory military service and retirement, the vast majority fall into the self-employment category.For those who are qualified to arrange work, the resettlement government must implement it within 6 months after receiving them. The unit will sign a contract of no less than 3 years. Those with military service of more than 10 years will sign a no-fixed term. During the period of waiting for placement, living allowances will be paid according to the local minimum wage.The self-employed level has a hard deadline: you must report within 30 days from the date you are approved to retire from active service. If you exceed 30 days without justifiable reasons, you will be deemed to have given up the resettlement benefits. If you go home and rest for a while, you may lose the entire benefits.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the "Regulations on the Resettlement of Veterans" lists six situations in which work is arranged by the people's government of the place of resettlement: "(1) Sergeants who have served in active service for 12 years or more; (2) Individuals who have received medals and honorary titles during their active service; (3) Individuals who have received third-class military merit, second-class merit or above during their active service; (4) Individuals who have received first-class commendations during their active service; (5) Individuals who have been disabled due to combat and have been assessed as level 5 to 8(6) Those who are children of martyrs"; "The people's government of the resettlement place shall complete the task of arranging the work of retired sergeants and conscripts within 6 months of receiving them"; the employer "should sign with them a medium- and long-term employment contract or labor contract of no less than 3 years in accordance with regulations. ### 7. If you want to enter the system, you must take more than just public examinations: if you have worked in grassroots service projects for two years, you can apply for the 10% annual targeted admissions examination plan.
<!-- Cost tag: money=0 time=many perseverance=yes income=medium caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the cost is to serve at the grassroots level in townships for two years, and the income is financial subsidies, not market prices
- Say it in plain language: Grassroots service projects such as "Three Supports and One Support" and the College Student Volunteer Service West Plan are another entry point within the system for fresh graduates.The three-support and one-support service period is two years, and each province must allocate about 10% of the civil service examination plan each year to conduct targeted examinations for grassroots service project personnel; public recruitment by county and township public institutions must allocate certain positions to special recruitment candidates who pass the assessment at the end of the assessment.
