# 21. Going abroad, traveling and overseas safety

The scope of this section is money and personal freedom, with a few items related to personal safety.Overseas rules are determined by the country where they are located. There are clear boundaries for what China’s overseas agencies can do. This section will first clarify where this boundary is.

### 1. Check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ safety reminder before departure, and do not go to places marked “Do Not Go”
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Mortality -->
- Cost: $0; open the China Consular Service website and browse through it, it will take a few minutes
- Say it in plain language: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs divides the security risks of foreign countries into four levels. The highest level is "do not travel yet."From September 15, 2026, administrative regulations have been written to avoid high-risk countries and regions. You will also be reminded when applying for a permit and exit border inspection, and you will be discouraged from going to the highest-risk areas when necessary.It only takes a few minutes to turn the page before heading out.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs publicly issues safety reminders abroad according to levels. From low to high, they are roughly "pay attention to safety", "strengthen security precautions", "travel with caution" and "do not travel yet".The new regulations that will be implemented on September 15, 2026 have also written the obligations of citizens into administrative regulations: "Chinese citizens should pay attention to foreign security reminders and travel destination safety risk warnings, and avoid traveling to and staying in high-risk countries or regions." At the same time, immigration management agencies are required to remind citizens who are going to high-risk areas when applying for permits and exit border inspections. "Chinese citizens who are preparing to go to countries or regions with the highest risk level or where cases that seriously endanger personal safety should be discouraged when necessary."

### 3. Know what consular protection can and cannot do: you can visit, but you can’t extort people, and you have to pay the fees yourself.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=little Perseverance=No Income=medium Caliber=money -->
- Cost: $0; read the boundary once
- Say it in plain language: What the embassy or consulate can do when you are arrested is to verify the situation with the local people, ask for fair treatment in accordance with the law, visit you, and attend court hearings. They cannot change your sentence for you, nor can they take you away.The food, accommodation, transportation, and medical expenses paid by others first will still be paid by you in the end.So prepare money and insurance before going abroad, and don’t think of “the country will pay for it if something goes wrong” as a plan.
- Benefit: What the embassy or consulate can do when arrested is: verify the situation with the relevant departments of the country where it is located, demand fair and fair treatment in accordance with the law, and "visit or contact the person in accordance with the laws of the country where it is located and the international treaties concluded or jointly participated by my country and the country where it is located, understand its relevant needs, and require the relevant departments of the country where it is located to provide the Chinese citizen with humanitarian treatment and fair treatment." It can also observe during the court session and demand protection of litigation rights.In the event of injury or disaster, we can urge local emergency rescue and assist family members in dealing with the aftermath.But the cost side is written equally clearly: "Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that receive materials and services such as food, accommodation, transportation, and medical care from third parties during the process of consular protection and assistance shall pay the expenses that should be borne by themselves."
- Evidence level: A
- Source: State Council (2023). Regulations on Consular Protection and Assistance (National Order No. 763). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Articles 9, 10, 14, 26)
- Note: The embassy or consulate will not pay fines, medical expenses, or air tickets for you, nor can it change the judicial conclusion of the host country.It provides information on local lawyers, translators, medical and funeral agencies, as well as assistance in contacting relatives and friends and obtaining relief.The practical use of understanding this boundary is: prepare your own money and insurance before going abroad, and don't take "the country will take care of everything if something goes wrong" as a plan

### 7. Before driving overseas, first confirm whether the local area recognizes the Chinese driver’s license. Most of the “international driver’s licenses” sold online are waste paper.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Freedom -->
- Cost: $0; check the destination regulations before departure
- Say it in plain language: The "International Driver's License" issued online is not valid in mainland China: that certificate is a translation that is mutually recognized among the contracting parties to the 1949 Geneva Convention. Mainland China is not among the 103 contracting parties, so it has no legal effect if it is issued by anyone in the country.Whether you can drive with a Chinese driver's license depends on the regulations of your destination. Driving on the road without a license is a heavy fine or criminal offense in most countries, and insurance will most likely deny compensation in the event of an accident.
- Benefit: The so-called "International Driving Permit" (IDP) is a mutually recognized translation between the contracting parties to the 1949 Road Traffic Convention.The Convention was concluded in Geneva on September 19, 1949 and came into effect on March 26, 1952. There are currently 103 parties, and mainland China is not included in the list of parties.Therefore, China does not issue this kind of international driver's license, and any agency that "issues an international driver's license" in the country will not have legal effect; whether you can drive with a Chinese driver's license depends on the regulations of the destination country or region (some recognize the Chinese driver's license plus a notarized translation, some require a local driver's license, and some do not recognize it at all)
- Evidence level: A

If you use false materials, you will be deemed to have committed fraud, and the consequences will be on your future entry and exit records.
- Benefit: After the cancellation of the access permit for private entry-exit intermediary service agencies in 2018, the number of agencies has grown rapidly, and there have also been problems such as "the number of agencies is unclear, some agencies handle business in violation of laws and regulations, and damage the legitimate rights and interests of exit and entry personnel."Starting from September 15, 2026, this type of intermediary will be subject to registration management: the agency shall register with the local immigration management agency within 15 days from the date of establishment, and those that have been operating previously must register within 90 days from the date of implementation.Reissue within days; prohibited behaviors include "publishing false information, or soliciting service recipients through exaggerated publicity, misleading publicity, etc." "Providing or assisting in providing false materials to assist others in illegally applying for visas, stay and residence documents, passports and other exit and entry documents or procedures" "Leaking, selling, illegally providing... personal privacy or personal information" "Organizing or assisting others to engage in cross-border illegal and criminal activities."Overseas enterprises and institutions are not allowed to provide such intermediary services in China

The unit will be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel will be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

- Benefit: When a Chinese citizen's whereabouts abroad are unknown, relatives can seek help from diplomatic missions abroad.
