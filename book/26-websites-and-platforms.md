This section answers one thing: what kind of business is the website or app you want to create legally? Which certificate is required? Where should the server be placed? Who is responsible for the things users send on it and the money they receive.The caliber is personal freedom and money: first write about those that will encounter criminal red lines, then write about those that require administrative permission, then the daily obligations of the platform, and finally the experience items such as server selection that can only be judged.The red line for employing technical personnel is in Section 11. ### 1. The platform should not collect the money itself and then transfer it to the seller. Instead, let the money go to a licensed payment institution for direct settlement.

### 10. Do not leave user information abroad casually. There are legal conditions and number thresholds for leaving the country.

### 11. When choosing a server, first check whether the downtime can be tolerated, then whether there is someone to operate and maintain it, and finally compare prices.
<!-- Cost tags: Money=little, time=medium, perseverance=no, income=medium, caliber=money -->
- Cost: An entry-level VPS costs tens of yuan per month, a cloud host that can handle real traffic costs hundreds to thousands of yuan per month, and independent server hosting starts at several thousand yuan per year and you need to buy your own hardware.
- Say it in plain language: The difference between the three levels is not in price, but in what you have on hand on the day of the accident: mainstream cloud vendors have registration channels, open qualifications, and have announcements and compensation clauses for failures; small service providers can be half the price, but you have no one to appeal when overbooking, running away, or the upstream is blocked; independent server hosting is only cost-effective when the bandwidth hardware is obviously cost-effective and you have someone to operate and maintain it yourself.For a static site without user data, a VPS of several tens of yuan per month is enough; for registration and transactions, choose a cloud host that can elastically expand capacity and snapshot backup, and store the backup in two places.
