# 9. Legal red lines that ordinary people easily step on

The scope of this section is personal freedom and money: the following things that ordinary people often think are "nothing" will actually lead to detention, fines or sentences. For each article, specific figures in the law and cases announced through official channels are given.The Public Security Management Punishment Law refers to the 2025 revised version (effective from January 1, 2026, the article number is different from the old law). The Criminal Law quotes the "Amended in accordance with the Criminal Law Amendment (Eleven)" reproduced by the Beijing Municipal Public Security and Traffic Management Bureau to integrate the text and indicate the corresponding amendments; the order is from high to low, "the easiest to inadvertently step on × and the heaviest consequences".

### 1. Do not forward disaster, epidemic, or police information that is not known to be true or false in the group, do not use P-pictures, or use AI to create on-site pictures.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = less Perseverance = some Benefit = big Caliber = freedom -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before reposting, check to see if it is an official announcement. If you are not sure, do not repost, do not change the picture, and do not add "it is said"
- Say it in plain language: If you forward a disaster, epidemic, or police news that you have not verified, even if you did not make it up, you will be detained for 5 to 10 days and fined 1,000 yuan; if you disrupt social order, you will be sentenced to less than 3 years, and if you cause serious consequences, you will be sentenced to 3 to 7 years.P-pictures and fake scene pictures generated by AI are the same thing as making up your own.
- Benefit: Those who deliberately spread rumors and falsely report dangers, epidemics, disasters, or police situations to disrupt public order shall be detained for 5 to 10 days and fined not more than 1,000 yuan. If the circumstances are less serious, they may be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan; those who fabricate false dangers, epidemics, disasters, or police situations and spread them online, or knowingly spread them despite knowing they are false, and seriously disrupt social order, shall be sentenced to less than 3 years, and those who cause serious consequences will be sentenced to 3 to 7 years (nationwide)

### 20. If a child cannot be raised after birth, the only legal way out is registration with the civil affairs: collecting money to give the child to someone else may be considered a crime of abduction, while leaving the child alone may be considered a crime of abandonment.

- Cost: 0 yuan; go to the civil affairs department at or above the county level to apply for adoption registration, submit materials, etc. for review and adoption evaluation
- Say it in plain language: There is a way out for those who cannot adopt, but there is only one legal one: adoption registration is done by the civil affairs department, and the adoption relationship is only established on the day of registration; those who can be adopted are guardians of orphans, child welfare agencies, and "biological parents who have special difficulties and are unable to raise their children."Giving a child to someone privately, especially for money, may directly turn from "putting up a child for adoption" to the crime of child trafficking, with a penalty of more than 5 years in prison, and in particularly serious cases, the death penalty.The official line drawn is "whether there is a purpose of illegal profit": selling the child after giving birth, knowing that the other party has no purpose of raising the child, or "giving" the child for money regardless of whether the other party can raise it, or accepting a huge amount of money that is obviously not nutrition fee, is considered as selling a biological child.On the other hand, those who are really forced to put them up for adoption privately due to life difficulties and only receive a small amount of nutrition fees as a thank you fee are classified as private adoptions and are not treated as trafficking.Abandoning a child is considered a crime of abandonment, with a sentence of less than 5 years; strangling or drowning is intentional homicide, and it does not make him lighter just because he was born.
