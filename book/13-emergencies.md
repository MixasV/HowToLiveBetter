This section only answers one thing: what to do first, what to do next, and what not to do in an emergency.The calibers of first aid and wilderness entries are specific causes of death and survival rates, while the calibers of threat and scam entries are money and personal freedom. The two types of numbers are not convertible to each other; the order is "most common × the difference between life and death of doing right and doing wrong" from high to low.According to the beneficiary scale of the book, the main focus of first aid items is yourself, your spouse, and your immediate family members—79.2% of cardiac arrests occur at home, and you are most likely to save family members; friends and colleagues are the second most likely to be saved; strangers are not zero benefit, but they are the lowest level and involve being blackmailed and involved in the case, so this type of item puts self-protection actions and exemption clauses at the front.

### 1. If someone falls to the ground and is not breathing, immediately press the chest hard and ask others to call 120 and find an AED.
