# 28. Don’t ruin your body just for appearance.

This section collects the methods of "doing things with the body in order to change the appearance": starving, taking medicine, injections, and surgery.The common point is that most decisions come from comparing with others, but all the costs fall on oneself, and a lot of them are irreversible.

This section does not comment on what anyone wants to become - that is everyone's own business.It only answers one question: For the same goal, what is the risk difference between taking the path of "having a prescription, having qualifications, and having a review" versus taking the path of "online shopping, beauty salons, and increasing the dosage yourself".The main focus is on health endpoints, while the two articles on medical beauty also involve criminal liability for illegal medical practice.

### 1. Don’t use extreme dieting, fasting or vomiting to control your weight. If you want to lose weight, lose weight from the side of exercise.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Mortality -->
- Cost: 0, which in turn saves money on weight loss products
- Say it in plain language: People with anorexia nervosa are nearly 6 times more likely to die than their peers, with 5.1 deaths per 1,000 people per year, and one in five deaths is suicide.And its entry point is dieting: after tracking students from 44 middle schools for three years, the probability of developing eating disorders among girls who are severely dieting is 18 times that of those who are not dieting, and the probability of developing eating disorders is 5 times that of those who are moderately dieting. Looking at weight and exercise alone, neither can predict new illnesses, only "diet" can.If you want to control your weight, move more and don't eat less until you get hungry.
- Benefit: 36 study meta-analysis (cumulative observation of anorexia nervosa 166,642 person-years): the standardized death ratio of anorexia nervosa is 5.86, 5.1 deaths per 1000 person-years, and about 1/5 of the deaths are due to suicide; the standardized death ratio of bulimia nervosa is 1.93, and other eating disorders 1.92.A 3-year and 6-wave cohort of 14–15-year-old students in 44 secondary schools in Victoria, Australia: New-onset eating disorders among girls were 21.8/1000 person-years. The risk of developing eating disorders among severe dieters was 18 times that of non-dieters, and that of moderate dieters was 5times; after adjusting for previous dieting and psychiatric symptoms, BMI, physical activity, and gender no longer predicted new eating disorders. The authors concluded that "among adolescents who use exercise rather than food restriction to control weight appear to have a lower risk of developing eating disorders."
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Arcelus J et al. (2011). Mortality rates in patients with anorexia nervosa and other eating disorders. A meta-analysis of 36 studies. Arch Gen Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archgenpsychiatry.2011.74>; Patton GC et al. (1999).<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.318.7186.765>
- Note: "Severe dieting" here refers to reducing food intake to much less than necessary for a long time, and does not mean skipping a meal or skipping midnight snacks.If you are already vomiting, blaming yourself after overeating, or unable to stop weighing yourself repeatedly, go to a psychiatric department or the clinical psychology department of a general hospital. This is a disease with standardized treatment, not a problem of willpower.For the relationship between BMI and mortality, see Section 2, Article 28. That article talks about long-term weight ranges, not weight loss methods.

### 2. Before giving injections, suture embedding, or surgery, check two things: whether there is "medical cosmetology" on the institution's license, and whether the person doing it is the attending physician.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Mortality -->
- Cost: 0, it takes a few minutes to check the two certificates; the institution should hang the "Medical Institution Practice License" in the lobby, and the doctor's qualifications can be checked in the medical practitioner query of the health department
- Say it in plain language: As long as it is surgery, injection, or medical equipment that breaks the skin or enters the body, it is considered a medical behavior and can only be done in an institution that has obtained a "Medical Institution Practice License" and has approved the "Medical Cosmetology" subject on the license.The doctor who performs the surgery must be qualified and registered as a medical practitioner, and those who perform cosmetic surgery must have more than 6 years of clinical experience in plastic surgery or cosmetic surgery.Doing this in beauty salons, studios, or hotel rooms is illegal medical practice: for serious cases, the sentence is less than 3 years, for injuries, 3 to 10 years, and for death, more than 10 years.Before doing it, the doctor must tell you the indications, contraindications and risks in writing and ask you to sign. Skipping this step means that the process itself is wrong.
- Benefit: Article 2 of the "Measures for the Administration of Medical Cosmetic Services" defines medical cosmetology as "the use of surgeries, drugs, medical devices and other traumatic or invasive medical technical methods to repair and reshape the human appearance and the shape of various parts of the human body"; Articles 8 and 24 require that a "Medical Institution Practice License" be obtained and approved by the registration authority before medical cosmetology diagnosis and treatment can be carried out; Article 11 requires the attending physician to have the qualification and registration of a practicing physician, and more than 6 years of cosmetic surgery and 5 years of cosmetic dentistry are required.More than 2 years of relevant professional clinical work experience in cosmetic traditional Chinese medicine and cosmetic dermatology. Article 20 requires written notification of indications, contraindications, medical risks and precautions before implementation and obtain signed consent.Article 336 of the "Criminal Law": A person who has not obtained the qualification to practice medicine shall illegally practice medicine. If the circumstances are serious, he shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than 3 years, criminal detention or surveillance, and shall also be fined, or shall be fined alone; who seriously damages the health of the patient shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 3 years but not more than 10 years and shall be fined; who shall cause the death of the patient shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 10 years and shall be fined.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Ministry of Health (2002). Measures for the Administration of Medical Beauty Services (Ministry of Health Order No. 19, Articles 2, 8, 11, 16, 20, 24). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62198.htm>; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Article 336).<https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Note: Article 16 of the same measure also requires that medical beauty projects must be performed in beauty medical institutions or medical institutions with medical beauty departments, so "door-to-door services" and "private studio operations" themselves are not compliant.The sentence in Article 20 states that "without the consent of the guardian, no medical cosmetic projects shall be carried out for persons who are incapacitated or have limited capacity." Medical cosmetic procedures for minors require the signature of the guardian.Lifestyle beauty (facial, massage, manicure) is not within this scope, but once needles and knives are used, it is medical beauty.

### 3. For facial fillers, avoid the dorsum of the nose, between the eyebrows, and the forehead. Don’t go for informal injections to save money.
<!-- Cost tag: Money = 0 Time = Less Perseverance = No Benefit = Large Caliber = Mortality -->
- Cost: 0, just change the part or do nothing
- Say it in plain language: The filler is pushed into the facial blood vessels and backflows to block the ophthalmic artery, making the person invisible on the spot.Among the 48 cases of post-injection blindness reported from 2015 to 2018, 56.3% were in the nose, 27.1% in the glabella, 18.8% in the forehead, and 14.6% in the nasolabial folds. More than 80% were injected with hyaluronic acid.Only 20.8% of these people fully recovered their vision, 16.7% partially recovered, and the rest did not recover; nearly 20% also developed brain complications.At present, no rescue method has been proven to be stable and effective, so this matter can only be avoided in advance.
- Benefit: World literature review (48 new cases of partial or complete vision loss after injection fillers from January 2015 to September 2018): the highest risk areas are the nose 56.3%, glabella 27.1%, forehead 18.8%, nasolabial folds 14.6%; 81.3% of cases were filled with hyaluronic acid; 43.8% had skin changes, 18.8%Central nervous system complications occurred; 10 cases (20.8%) had complete vision recovery and 8 cases (16.7%) had partial recovery; the authors concluded that management methods varied widely and no treatment was proven to be consistently effective
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Beleznay K et al. (2019). Update on Avoiding and Treating Blindness From Fillers: A Recent Review of the World Literature. Aesthet Surg J. <https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjz053>
- Note: This is a summary of cases reported in the literature. There is no denominator. It cannot calculate the "probability of blindness after one injection". It can only read "which parts have the most accidents and are basically impossible to save afterwards."This is also why it's a B grade.Risk reduction practices are already routine in formal institutions: preoperative assessment of blood vessel course, use of blunt needles, small injections while withdrawing, and hyaluronidase ready at all times—these require the operator to be a trained doctor and are reasons not to go to informal channels.Check before doing so according to Article 2 of this section.

### 4. Don’t buy slimming pills, slimming coffee, slimming candies and enzyme plums that promise “quick weight loss”
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=mortality rate -->
- Cost: 0, in turn save this money
- Say it in plain language: The most commonly detected illegal additive in this type of product is sibutramine.It turned out to be a serious weight-loss drug, but was discontinued in China in 2010: In a trial involving nearly 10,000 people who took it for an average of more than three years, those who took the drug were about 30% more likely to have non-fatal myocardial infarction, and about 36% more likely to have non-fatal stroke. The accident rate was 11.4% vs. 10.0%, and they only lost 1.7 kilograms more over three years.It is illegal to add it to food and health food, and strict inspections will be carried out, and those suspected of committing crimes will be transferred to the police - but that is an afterthought, you have already eaten it.
- Benefit: SCOUT trial (9,804 overweight or obese people 55 years and older with cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes randomized, treated for a mean of 3.4 years): primary endpoint (nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, resuscitated cardiac arrest, cardiovascular death) rate 11.4% vs 10.0%, HR 1.16 (95% CI)1.03–1.31, P=0.02); nonfatal myocardial infarction 4.1% vs 3.2%, HR 1.28 (1.04–1.57); nonfatal stroke 2.6% vs 1.9%, HR 1.36 (1.04–1.77); cardiovascular death and all-cause death were not increased; the sibutramine group lost an average of 1.7 kg more weight after randomization.Based on the safety assessment, the former State Food and Drug Administration believed that "the risks of weight loss treatment outweigh the benefits." In October 2010, it was notified to stop the production, sale, and use of sibutramine preparations and APIs; both sibutramine and phenolphthalein are prohibited from being added to food (including health foods)
- Evidence level: A
- Source: James WPT et al. (2010). Effect of sibutramine on cardiovascular outcomes in overweight and obese subjects. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1003114>; Tianjin Municipal Market Supervision and Administration Commission. Be wary of the illegal addition of "sibutramine hydrochloride" and "phenolphthalein" to some foods.<https://scjg.tj.gov.cn/tjsscjdglwyh_52651/hdpt/cjwtyxfts/202007/t20200720_2972549.html>
- Note: SCOUT enrolled people at high risk of cardiovascular disease or diabetes. The trial did not prove that healthy young people would have the same problems when eating it; however,
Precisely because risk groups cannot be identified, regulatory authorities directly withdraw the market.Another common illegal added ingredient is phenolphthalein (laxative). Long-term overdose can cause electrolyte imbalance and induce arrhythmia.The judgment method is very simple: serious weight loss drugs are prescription drugs, and their approval numbers can be checked in pharmacies and hospitals; those sold under the names of "coffee", "candy", "plum", and "meal replacement" and promising a few kilograms per week should not be effective by definition. To be effective is to add something.The original text of State Food and Drug Administration Office [2010] No. 432 is on the website of the State Food and Drug Administration. Local access always returns 412, so it quotes the paraphrase page of the provincial regulatory authorities. Among them, "the risks outweigh the benefits" and "stopped production, sale and use in October 2010" can be verified verbatim.

### 5. Do not use anabolic steroids ("muscle-building shots" or "oral pills") to build muscle
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=mortality rate -->
- Cost: 0, save money on medicine; the price is to accept that muscles grow slowly
- Say it in plain language: Denmark tracked 545 people who used drugs based on positive doping tests in the gym. Compared with 5,450 people of the same age, the risk of death was three times that of the control group. The median number of trips to the hospital per year was 0.81 times versus 0.36 times. More than 10% of people were affected by acne, gynecomastia, and erectile dysfunction. The results were the same when reviewed with another group of people.This type of drug is a prescription drug in China and is also listed in the stimulant catalog. It is illegal for any unit or individual to illegally produce, sell, import or export it - so the channel through which it can be sold to you is itself illegal, and no one can guarantee whether the drug is genuine.
- Benefit: Danish retrospective matched cohort (545 men who tested positive for androgenic steroids in the gym from 2006-01-03 to 2018-03-01, paired with 5,450 controls; 644 test refusers and 6,440 controls were reviewed): user mortality was 3 times that of controls, HR 3.0 (95% CI1.3–7.0); the median number of hospitalizations/visits per year was 0.81 vs 0.36 (P<0.0001); acne, gynecomastia, and erectile dysfunction each affected more than 10% of users, all significantly higher than the control (P<0.0001); the results of the review cohort were consistent.Article 7 of the "Anti-Doping Regulations": The state implements strict management of prohibited substances listed in the doping catalog, and no unit or individual is allowed to illegally produce, sell, import or export; the same regulations stipulate that anabolic agents and peptide hormones are subject to prescription drug management
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Horwitz H et al. (2019). Health consequences of androgenic anabolic steroid use. J Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.12850>; State Council (2004). Anti-Doping Regulations (State Council Order No. 398, Articles 7 and 9).<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63129.htm>
- Note: Controversy: This is an observational cohort, and people who use drugs may be more risky in other ways. The 3 times figure cannot be fully attributed to drugs; the confidence interval of 1.3–7.0 is also very wide.However, "acne, gynecomastia, and erectile dysfunction are more than 10%" are directly observable side effects and have little to do with selection bias."Anabolics" is what the regulations call these drugs.Ordinary protein powder and creatine are not within the scope of this article. The problem lies in the fact that products that "claim to have a particularly fast muscle-building effect" may be illegally added. The judgment method is the same as Article 4.

### 6. If you want to take weight loss pills, go to the hospital to get a prescription. Don’t buy them from online stores that ship them without a prescription.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=Low Time=Medium Perseverance=No Benefit=Medium Caliber=Mortality -->
- Cost: One registration plus round trip, $3–15, half a day
- Say it in plain language: According to regulations, prescription drugs must be sold with a prescription, and pharmacies must keep the prescription for five years; when selling prescription drugs online, they must have a true and reliable prescription and their real name. The page must state "Prescription drugs must be purchased and used under the guidance of a pharmacist with a prescription."So when you see a drug that is issued immediately without consultation or prescription, there are only two possibilities: it is not selling the medicine it says it is, or the store itself is violating regulations - in both cases, no one will be responsible for your dosage and contraindications.This is what I spent tens of dollars on registration.
- Benefit: The "Measures for the Supervision and Administration of the Quality of Drug Operation and Use" stipulates that drug retail enterprises shall abide by the classification management system for prescription drugs and non-prescription drugs, sell prescription drugs according to regulations, and keep prescriptions for no less than five years; pharmacists or other pharmaceutical technicians should put up a notice when they are not on duty, and prescription drugs shall not be sold without their review; prescription drugs shall not be sold on the shelves.Article 9 of the "Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Drug Online Sales" stipulates that those who sell prescription drugs to individuals through the Internet must ensure that the source of the prescription is authentic and reliable and implement a real-name system; Article 10 requires that risk warning information be highlighted under each drug display page, and instructions shall not be displayed or purchase services shall be provided before passing the prescription review; Article 8 stipulates that vaccines, blood products, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, medical toxic drugs, radioactive drugs, pharmaceutical precursor chemicals and other drugs subject to special management by the state shall not be sold online
- Evidence level: B
- Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (2023). Measures for the Supervision and Administration of the Distribution and Use of Drugs (General Administration Order No. 84).No., Articles 8 to 10). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Note: The law itself can be verified word for word, but there is no quantitative study on "how many fewer problems can occur if you take it according to the prescription rather than buying it yourself", so it is marked as "difficult to quantify" and marked as B.This article is not just about weight loss pills: steroids and sex hormones mentioned in Articles 5 and 7 of this section follow the same set of rules.If there is really an indication for weight loss, the doctor will first look at the BMI, comorbidities and contraindications before deciding whether to use medication and which kind to use. This judgment cannot be bought.

### 7. Sex hormone drugs should only be used after being prescribed by a doctor and reviewed regularly. Do not buy them online or increase the dosage yourself.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=Low Time=Medium Perseverance=No Benefit=Medium Caliber=Mortality -->
- Cost: Regular registration and blood reexamination, $30–150 a year, several half-days
- Say it in plain language: This article does not discuss whether it should be used, only how to use it.In a study of transgender people with an average follow-up of about four years, transgender women (most of whom were taking estrogen) had significantly more venous thrombosis than controls of the same age: 4.1 more cases per 1,000 people in two years and 16.7 more cases in eight years. For those who only started taking medication during the follow-up period, the gap between thrombosis and ischemic stroke was more obvious; there was no overall difference in myocardial infarction.The key is that blood clots can be checked and intervened in advance, provided that there is someone checking you regularly - online shopping and adjusting your own dosage, but there is no such person.
- Benefit: Kaiser Permanente Electronic Medical Record Cohort (2,842 trans women, 2,118 trans men, mean follow-up 4.0 vs. 3.6 years, each matched by birth year, race, site, and enrollment time approximately 10x cisgender controls, 48,686 cisgender men vs. 48,775 cisgender women): 2 years vs. 8 years of venous thromboembolism in trans womenThe annual risk difference was 4.1 (95% CI 1.6–6.7) vs. 16.7 (6.4–27.5) per 1000 for cisgender men and 3.4 (1.1–5.6) vs. cisgender women.13.7 (4.1–22.7); ischemic stroke and myocardial infarction were generally similar in each group, but the difference between venous thrombosis and ischemic stroke was more obvious among transgender women who started hormone therapy during the follow-up period; the evidence for transgender men was insufficient to draw conclusions.The authors concluded that "these results may suggest the need for long-term vigilance against the vascular side effects of estrogen in cross-sex hormones."
- Evidence level: A
- Source: Getahun D et al. (2018). Cross-sex Hormones and Acute Cardiovascular Events in Transgender Persons: A Cohort Study. Ann Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-2785>; State Administration for Market Regulation (2022). Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Drug Online Sales (General Administration Order No. 58, Article 9).<https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Note: This study has its own limitations: it is impossible to know how many people are taking medication outside the system, so the classification of "medication or not" is inherently unclean - which exactly explains why medication should be included in regular follow-up.It measures vascular events and does not evaluate the indications and benefits of hormone therapy itself. That is another set of evidence.The same principle applies to all uses of sex hormones: birth control pills, menopausal hormone therapy, testosterone supplements. Anyone who purchases online, adjusts the dosage according to online posts, or does not check blood clots and liver and kidney indicators, the risk lies in the "no one is monitoring", not in the motivation for taking the medication.The domestic standard path is to go to the endocrinology department or corresponding specialty of a formal medical institution for evaluation, prescription, and follow-up.

### 8. If you "feel ugly" to the point where you repeatedly look at yourself in the mirror and want to change it, do a body image assessment first before discussing surgery.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=little, time=medium, perseverance=some, income=medium, caliber=mortality rate -->
- Cost: One psychiatry or clinical psychology department registration costs $3–15
- Say it in plain language: Looking at 33 studies combined, 15.04% of people who went for plastic surgery met the criteria for body dysmorphic disorder - a defect that is basically invisible to others and magnified to affect their lives; 12.65% of people who went to dermatology for beauty, and more than 70% of both were women.The problem of this kind of trouble is not on the face. The surgery changes the face. If the cycle of "repeated inspection and repeated dissatisfaction" is not changed, it will be easy to continue to have one procedure after another.Spend the registration money first to confirm whether you fall into this category, and then decide whether to take action.
- Benefit: 33 studies systematic review and meta-analysis: the prevalence of body dysmorphic disorder among plastic surgery patients is 15.04% (2.21%–56.67% in each study), the average age is 34.54±12.41 years, 74.38% are female; the dermatology patients are 12.65% (4.52%–35.16%), the average age27.79±9.03 years old, 76.09% female; the authors recommend that plastic surgeons and dermatologists proactively assess, identify high-risk individuals, and arrange for multidisciplinary management
- Evidence level: B
- Source: Ribeiro RVE (2017). Prevalence of Body Dysmorphic Disorder in Plastic Surgery and Dermatology Patients: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Aesthetic Plast Surg. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00266-017-0869-0>
- Note: The results of each study range from 2.21% to 56.67%, with a huge span. The screening tools and the population are not uniform, so we can only read "This proportion is not low and it is worth screening first." We cannot take 15.04% as the accurate value, which is why it is recorded as B.This article does not mean that people who want to undergo cosmetic surgery have psychological problems, the vast majority do not; it refers to the small group of people whose problems are “repeated, persistent, and have affected their work and social life.” Surgery cannot solve their problems.Judgment clues: Spending a long time checking or covering a certain part of the day, avoiding socializing or taking photos, and focusing on the next after finishing one area.
