# 17. There are elderly people at home

Caliber: Money and personal freedom.Most of the trouble is not that there is not enough money, but that the matter is not explained clearly and signed in advance.

### 1. While the old man is awake, designate a future guardian in writing
<!-- Cost tag: Money=less, time=less, perseverance=some, profit=big, caliber=money -->
- Cost: Notarization costs several hundred yuan; one conversation
- Say it in plain language: While the old man is still awake, spend several hundred dollars to notarize a written document to determine who will be the guardian in the future.Without this document, when the old man cannot make his own decisions, the court will decide who will sign for him and who will manage his money, and disagreements in the family will drag on even longer.
- Benefit: The Civil Code stipulates that adults with full capacity for civil conduct can negotiate in advance with close relatives or other individuals or organizations willing to serve as guardians to "identify their guardians in writing", and the guardian will perform their duties when they lose or partially lose their capacity for civil conduct.Without this document, who will sign and who will manage the money in the future will have to be appointed by the court.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 33). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: This is called voluntary guardianship, and its effectiveness takes precedence over the legal order of guardianship.Those who live alone, have children in other places, and have complicated family relationships are in greater need.Notarization is recommended

### 2. After making a will, remember that the one made later will overturn the one made first. The notarized will will no longer take priority.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan for self-written will; hundreds to thousands for notarization
- Say it in plain language: All six forms of wills are recognized by the law, but if the form is wrong, the whole copy will become invalid. This is the most common failure point: a self-written will must be handwritten in full, signed, and marked with the year, month, and day; a printed will requires more than two witnesses. The testator and witnesses must sign and indicate the year, month, and day on each page. Heirs, legatees, and their close relatives cannot be witnesses.If several copies are made and compete with each other, the last one shall prevail. From 2021, the one notarized will no longer automatically take priority.
- Benefit: The Civil Code recognizes six forms of wills: self-written, written on behalf of others, printed, audio and video, oral, and notarized, each with its own formal requirements; if there are several wills with conflicting contents, the last will shall prevail. From 2021, notarized wills will no longer have priority.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 1134 to 1142). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: A self-written will must be written in full and signed by the person, and the year, month, and day must be marked; a printed will must have two or more witnesses. The testator and witnesses must sign and mark each page of the will.Heirs, legatees and their close relatives cannot be witnesses.If the format is wrong, the entire copy will be invalid. This is the most common failure point.

### 5. Don’t touch any “investment for senior care” that asks the elderly to pay money first: applying for a card, buying a bed, buying a senior apartment, living in senior care, and buying senior products are all the same illegal fund-raising.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; read the following items to the elderly once. If you encounter a direct refusal, you will not attend the class, visit, or leave a phone number.
- Say it in plain language: All the projects for elderly care that require the elderly to pay money first and promise returns - applying for VIP membership cards, prepaying elderly care service fees, buying beds, buying shares in health care and elderly care bases, buying elderly care apartments, living in elderly care, and selling elderly products are all the same.The money cannot be recovered according to regulations, and the losses incurred by participating in illegal fund-raising shall be borne by the participants themselves.Look at the magnitude of the official report: someone used the name of booking beds in a retirement apartment to collect 136 million yuan, involving more than 2,100 victims; another person used the banner of investing in the elderly care industry to collect 13.207 billion yuan, involving more than 110,000 people.The only thing that can stop you is paying for it.
- Benefit:The five forms of manifestations listed by the Ministry of Civil Affairs are more reliable than identifying the person: in the name of a senior care service organization (no entity, temporarily renting a venue, and claiming to have signed a cooperation agreement with the senior care agency to "make trouble"); in the name of providing "senior care services" ("VIP card", "membership card", "prepaid card", prepayment of "senior care service fees", and promised services that clearly exceed the bed supply capacity); in the name of investing in "senior care projects" (taking shares in health care and senior care services)base, sell fictitious retirement apartments, long-term rental beds, and "time bank" mutual assistance for the elderly, using return sales, after-sales leasebacks, and agreed buybacks as guise); in the name of selling "elderly products" (commodity repurchase, consignment sales, consumption rebates, free physical examinations, gifts, conference marketing, health lectures, expert free clinics); in the name of enjoying "residential care" (low-cost or free travel, stored value rebates, points for retirement).The fact that the money cannot be recovered is written in the system: Article 25 of the Regulations on Prevention and Handling of Illegal Fund-raising "Loss suffered due to participation in illegal fund-raising shall be borne by the fund-raising participants themselves."The magnitude depends on the officially reported cases: Dai Mouping and others illegally raised 136 million yuan in the name of booking beds in a nursing home apartment, with more than 2,100 victims. ### 6. Except for the insurance company’s reverse mortgage pension insurance, don’t touch any other “house-based retirement plans” and never mortgage your house to buy financial services.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=less Perseverance=some Benefit=big Caliber=money -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the property certificate of the home will not be handed over to anyone who comes to the door in the name of "housing for retirement", and any mortgage, guarantee, or loan agreement shall be shown to the children before signing.
- Say it in plain language: The only formal housing-based pension insurance is the insurance company's housing reverse mortgage pension insurance, which is currently still being piloted and is relatively niche.The door-to-door sales of "housing for the elderly" has nothing to do with the national pilot program: it tricks the elderly into mortgaging their houses and using the borrowed money to buy the financial management products it recommends. The essence is to use new money to pay back old money.The original words of the supervision were that some people did not even know that their property was mortgaged, and eventually the house was gone and they were still carrying the loan.Financial management promises a question mark if the return exceeds 6%. It is very dangerous if it exceeds 8%. If it exceeds 10%, you must be prepared to lose all your principal.
- Benefit:The Bureau of Consumer Protection of the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission made it clear what is true and what is false: there is only one formal form of home-based retirement insurance, namely "reverse mortgage pension insurance for the elderly". Elderly people with full legal property rights in their homes mortgage their properties to the insurance company and continue to have the right to possess, use, benefit and dispose with the consent of the mortgagee, and receive pensions according to the agreed conditions until their death. It is still in the pilot stage and is relatively niche; the "house-based retirement insurance" mentioned by criminals is different from the national reverse mortgage of housing.Pension insurance is "completely unrelated" and is "a means of promoting illegal fund-raising activities under the guise of national policies". The operation is to trick the elderly into applying for real estate mortgages and then use the borrowed money to buy the financial products it recommends. "It is often a Ponzi scheme of 'repaying the old with new'." The original text of the consequences reads: "Some participants do not even know that their properties are mortgaged, and eventually lose their houses and are still burdened with loans." The yield judgment line given is "If a financial product promises a yield of more thanIf it exceeds 6%, there will be a question mark. If it exceeds 8%, it will be very dangerous. If it exceeds 10%, you will be prepared to lose all your principal. "Guaranteed capital and high returns" is a financial fraud." The same reminder also requires that "treat the contract signing process with caution and never sign a blank contract." (National, June 2021)

### 7. If the elderly at home are bedridden for a long time or are severely disabled, go to the medical insurance department in the insured place to apply for long-term care insurance.
<!-- Cost tag: Money=0 Time=Medium Perseverance=No Benefit=Large Caliber=Money -->
- Cost: Apply for one additional disability level assessment; the assessment fee for passing the first assessment will be paid by the fund
- Say it in plain language: If the elderly are severely disabled and this condition lasts for more than six months, they should go to the medical insurance department in the insured place to apply for long-term care insurance: there is no deductible for nursing fees that meet the regulations. The reimbursement for insurance under employee policies is about 70%, and the reimbursement for urban and rural residents is about 50%. The maximum reimbursement in one year is half of the per capita disposable income of local residents in the previous year.The evaluation fee for those who pass the first evaluation will be paid by the fund; bed fees, food expenses, etc. are not included in the reimbursement scope.
- Benefit: There is no deductible for long-term care services that meet the regulations. The fund insured according to the unit's employee policy pays about 70%, and about 50% is paid according to the policy for unemployed urban and rural residents; the annual maximum payment limit does not exceed 50% of the per capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the coordinated area in the previous year.
- Evidence level: A
- Source: National Medical Insurance Administration and eight other departments (2026). Notice on the issuance of the "Implementation Plan for Accelerating the Establishment of a Long-term Care Insurance System".<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm>: The protection targets are "disabled persons who have paid insurance premiums in accordance with regulations and whose disability has lasted for a long time (generally more than 6 months), and who have passed the assessment and identification upon application". "The system protects severely disabled persons in the initial stage"; "There is no minimum payment standard for benefits. The cost of long-term care services that meet the regulations is based on the urban and rural non-employment rate."Those who participate in the resident insurance policy, the fund payment ratio is about 50%; those who participate in the unit employee insurance policy, the fund payment ratio is about 70%, and retirees enjoy the benefits of unit employee insurance." "The maximum annual payment limit of the insured personnel fund shall not exceed 50% of the per capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the coordinated area in the previous year." "The fixed waiting period for benefits is in principle set at 6 months." Also see the "Opinions on Accelerating the Establishment of a Long-term Care Insurance System" by the General Office of the CPC Central Committee and the General Office of the State Council.<https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html>
- Note: The disability must be "long-lasting (generally more than 6 months)" and you cannot apply for it just by being hospitalized for a few weeks.Long-term care insurance will not pay for non-nursing expenses such as bed fees and meals, as well as medical expenses that should be paid by medical insurance; those who are already receiving work-related injury life care expenses will not be eligible for it again.Home and community care has a skewed payment ratio and does not necessarily require institutional care.The system will be implemented in batches in various places. First, call the medical insurance agency in the insured area to ask if it has been launched locally and how to make an appointment for evaluation.
